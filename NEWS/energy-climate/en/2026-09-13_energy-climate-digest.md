## II. Policy, Carbon Rules & Transition Governance

### 4. EU CBAM electricity guidance: renewable exports need an hourly, verifier-ready evidence chain (Carbon markets)
**Summary:** Coverage dated Sep 13 said the European Commission's new electricity-sector guidance makes clear that renewable generation alone does not qualify an imported megawatt-hour for “actual emissions” treatment under CBAM, versus punitive national default carbon values. Producers, traders and authorised EU declarants must tie physical delivery to a named installation, a qualifying PPA and accepted cross-border schedules; generation and nominated interconnector capacity must match within a measurement window of at most one hour, backed by smart-meter and TSO evidence. Guarantees of Origin can show renewable power was produced but do not by themselves prove the same power was contracted, scheduled and imported into the EU; plants must also meet connection/congestion tests and emit less than 550 g fossil CO₂ per kWh.

**Links:**

- [Serbia-Energy — EU guidance turns renewable electricity exports into an hourly evidence business](https://serbia-energy.eu/eu-guidance-turns-renewable-electricity-exports-into-an-hourly-evidence-business/)

**Commentary:** Green certificates yield to “hourly physical identity”—selling power into the EU is becoming a metering, scheduling and verification product.

### 6. U.S. appeals court vacates DOE emergency order keeping Michigan's Campbell coal plant online (Policy)
**Summary:** On Sep 11 the D.C. Circuit unanimously vacated a Department of Energy order under Federal Power Act section 202(c) that had forced Consumers Energy to keep the J.H. Campbell coal plant running; Reuters and legal briefings continued into the weekend. The court held the emergency power covers only acute shortfall risks needing immediate federal action—not overrides of Michigan PSC and MISO-approved retirement and replacement plans. Michigan, Illinois, Minnesota and environmental groups challenged the order; the panel said DOE exceeded its authority and that record materials showed adequate anticipated resources for peak load. The ruling vacated the expired initial order; a later order keeping the plant available through Nov 14, 2026 was not decided in the same stroke.

**Links:**

- [Reuters — US court blocks Trump administration bid to keep Michigan coal plant open](https://www.reuters.com/legal/litigation/us-court-blocks-trump-administration-bid-keep-michigan-coal-plant-open-2026-09-11/)
