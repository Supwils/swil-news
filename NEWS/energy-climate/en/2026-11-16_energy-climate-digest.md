## I. Climate Negotiations and COP31

### 3. Guterres: phase out unabated coal by 2040 and cut oil and gas use by a third

**Summary:** At the November 11 leaders' summit, UN Secretary-General António Guterres urged phasing out unabated coal by 2040, cutting oil and gas use by a third and halting new fossil infrastructure. He said emissions must fall 6% a year and that current national plans deliver only about a third of the needed cuts, and urged developed countries to deliver the $300 billion a year by 2035. Brazil's Lula proposed three roadmaps (finance, fossil transition, deforestation) and cited a 71% drop in Amazon deforestation between 2022 and 2026; Türkiye said renewables are 60% of its installed capacity.

**Links:**

- [Down To Earth — COP31: UN chief calls for faster phase-out of fossil fuels and increased climate finance](https://www.downtoearth.org.in/climate-change/cop31-un-chief-calls-for-faster-phase-out-of-fossil-fuels-and-increased-climate-finance)

**Commentary:** The calls are specific but non-binding; the real test is whether a fossil-fuel transition roadmap, absent at COP30, makes it into the final text at all.

### 4. UNEP Adaptation Gap Report 2026: $305 billion a year needed, tripling covers only 20–29%

**Summary:** Released on November 4, UNEP's report estimates developing countries need about $305 billion a year for adaptation by 2035 (up to about $420 billion with inflation), while international public adaptation finance rose by only about $1 billion between 2023 and 2024. Even tripling international adaptation finance would meet only 20–29% of needs, and private flows might close only 15–20% of the public-oriented gap. UNEP says warming is set to exceed 1.5°C.

**Links:**

- [IISD SDG Knowledge Hub — Growth in finance insufficient to meet needs: Adaptation Gap Report 2026](https://sdg.iisd.org/news/growth-in-finance-insufficient-to-meet-needs-adaptation-gap-report-2026/)
- [Down To Earth — $305 billion needed, $28 billion delivered](https://www.downtoearth.org.in/climate-change/305-billion-needed-28-billion-delivered-developing-countries-face-massive-climate-adaptation-funding-gap)

**Commentary:** The report supplies the ledger for the tripling debate at COP31: even if the target is met, the gap remains the bigger part of the bill.

## II. Clean Power and Storage

### 5. China's new-type energy storage reaches 167GW/435GWh, up 63% year on year

**Summary:** NEA data released on October 30 show China's new-type energy storage at about 167GW/435GWh at end-September, up 63% year on year. Cumulative wind and solar reached 2.01 billion kW (solar about 1,312.87GW, wind about 699.99GW) and total installed capacity 4.132 billion kW. H1 storage additions of 21.81GW/58.60GWh were down about 18% year on year, as the sector shifts from scale to utilization.
