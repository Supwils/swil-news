### 2. Deadlock's invite-only test passes Team Fortress 2's Steam peak (platform)

## II. Streaming and Regulation

### 6. Netflix says AI has been used on about 300 titles; a director cites cuts of up to 40% (streaming)
**Summary:** Variety reported on October 10 that Lee Sung-gyun, Netflix's senior director of APAC production, told a Busan International Film Festival panel that AI has been used in one form or another on about 300 Netflix titles, including previs, production design, set-dressing concepts, editorial, and VFX. On the same panel, co-hosted by Netflix and the MPA, The Outlaws director Kang Yoon-sung said AI workflows could take 30% to 40% off a feature's production budget. Using fundraising math for The Gulf of Aden, he put the reduction at about 40%, not only from replacing CG but also because the shoot itself became simpler and the schedule somewhat shorter. Weta FX chief technology officer Kimball Thurston, also on the panel, called the studio's deep-learning denoiser assistive AI and said it is not allowed to change creative intent.

**Links:**

- [Variety — Netflix has used AI on about 300 titles](https://variety.com/2026/tv/asia/netflix-ai-300-titles-kang-yoon-sung-40-percent-budget-busan-1236907359/)

**Commentary:** The figure of 300 titles is a scope of use, and 40% is one film's fundraising estimate, not an average saving across the Netflix catalog.

### 7. Singapore fines its two casinos a combined S$175,000 over promotion breaches (regulation)
**Summary:** VnExpress reported on October 10 that Singapore's Gambling Regulatory Authority, in its annual report for the financial year ended March 2026, disclosed fines totaling S$175,000 (US$136,800) against Marina Bay Sands and Resorts World Sentosa for breaches of casino advertising rules. The report was released earlier this month. Marina Bay Sands was fined S$100,000 for conducting or offering casino promotions without prior approval. Resorts World Sentosa was fined S$75,000 for failing to carry out promotions in line with the authority's approval. The same report says lottery and betting operator Singapore Pools was fined S$100,000 for inadvertently allowing a self-excluded person to place bets. It also says 91,000 citizens and permanent residents, 2.7% of the adult population, visited casinos in 2025, compared with 94,000 and 2.8% in 2024.
