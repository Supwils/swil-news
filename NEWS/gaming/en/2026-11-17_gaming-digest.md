## III. Streaming & M&A

### 7. Fox Completes Its Acquisition of Roku

**Summary:** Fox Corporation closed its Roku acquisition on November 6: each Roku share receives $95.30 in cash plus 0.9799 shares of FOX Class A stock, leaving FOX holders with about 74% and Roku holders about 26%, and Roku will stop trading on Nasdaq. Fox expects roughly $400 million in annual cost synergies within two years, and Roku founder Anthony Wood joins Fox's board and co-chairs the new FOX Digital Platforms division.

**Links:**

- [Señal News — Fox completes acquisition of Roku](https://senalnews.com/en/content/fox-completes-acquisition-of-roku-expanding-its-streaming-footprint)
