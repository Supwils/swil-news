### 5. NVIDIA Ships RTX Mega Geometry 2.0 for Streamed Ray-Traced Geometry (Tech)
**Summary:** Hardware Busters reported on September 26 that NVIDIA released RTX Mega Geometry 2.0 in RTX Kit 2026.3. The SDK streams pre-baked triangle clusters into VRAM on demand and sheds geometric detail when memory budgets are exceeded, rather than thrashing. Gears of War: E-Day (launching October 6) is the first announced adopter; the SDK requires an RTX GPU with at least 10GB of VRAM and a recent driver branch.

**Links:**

- [Hardware Busters — RTX Mega Geometry 2.0](https://hwbusters.com/news/rtx-mega-geometry-2-0-streams-ray-traced-geometry-into-vram-and-sheds-detail-before-it-runs-out-of-memory/)

**Commentary:** Matching raster detail in ray tracing is no longer only about compute—it is about fitting and streaming the mesh.
