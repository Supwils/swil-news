## II. Regulation, Deals, and Industry

### 5. Australia's Federal Court orders Apple to readmit Fortnite (regulation)
**Summary:** Australian Associated Press reported on October 22 that Federal Court Justice Jonathan Beach on Thursday ordered Apple to do everything necessary to readmit Fortnite and other Epic applications to the Australian App Store, and to let Australian iOS users download apps directly or from alternative stores after security checks. Epic Games Newsroom said on X the same day that Fortnite would return to the Australian App Store on Friday, and Engadget corrected an earlier claim that the game had already returned. Engadget's summary of the 1,104-paragraph order adds that Apple must pay 85 percent of Epic's legal costs, implement the broader orders by March 1, 2027, and file proof by February 15, 2027, if it needs an extension. AAP says no orders were made against Google on Thursday, and an Apple spokesperson said the company disagrees with many findings and will request a stay and appeal.

**Links:**

- [AAP — October 22](https://aapnews.aap.com.au/news/aust-court-orders-fortnite-back-into-app-store)
- [Engadget — October 22](https://www.engadget.com/2293801/fortnite-is-back-on-apple-australian-app-store-following-court-ruling/)

**Commentary:** Thursday's development is the remedies order; Epic timed the game's return for Friday, and Apple said it will seek a stay and appeal.
