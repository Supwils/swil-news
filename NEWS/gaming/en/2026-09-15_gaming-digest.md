### 7. Emmys in Advanced Talks to Move to Amazon Prime Video (Streaming)
**Summary:** Variety and TheWrap reported on September 15 that the Television Academy is in advanced talks to place the Emmy telecast on Amazon Prime Video, which would take the ceremony off broadcast TV for the first time since 1949. The 31-year “wheel deal” rotating ABC, CBS, Fox, and NBC ends with NBC/Peacock's airing of the 78th Emmys under an eight-year pact struck in 2018; networks were believed to pay just above $8 million a year. If completed, the Emmys would join other awards shows moving to streaming, following ACM Awards, the SAG Awards, and the Oscars' planned YouTube shift in 2029.
