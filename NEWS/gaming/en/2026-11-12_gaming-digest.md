## I. Majors & Products

### 1. GDC industry report: about one-third of workers laid off in two years, more than half still unemployed; generative AI use rises to about 40% (industry)
**Summary:** Variety and The Verge reported on November 12 that the GDC Festival of Gaming released its 15th State of the Game Industry report, based on more than 2,600 professionals. About 34% of respondents said they were laid off in the past 24 months (about 28% last year), and more than half of those had not found a new job; overall unemployment was about 21% (about 13% last year), while 62% had applied to 20 or more roles. About 40% said they use generative AI on the job (about 36% last year), with higher rates at mobile-only studios and free-to-play teams (about 70% and 58%); about 57% of users said they use it daily. The report also said about 10% and 8% of respondents are developing for the next PlayStation and Xbox “Project Helix,” respectively.

**Links:**

- [Variety — GDC: majority of laid-off game workers still unemployed](https://variety.com/2026/gaming/news/video-game-workers-laid-off-still-unemployed-1236946277/)
- [The Verge — More game developers using generative AI](https://www.theverge.com/games/1032085/gdc-festival-of-gaming-annual-survey-ai-layoffs)

**Commentary:** Layoff aftershocks and AI adoption were quantified on the same day—the baseline is higher unemployment plus heavier tool dependence, not a soft landing.
