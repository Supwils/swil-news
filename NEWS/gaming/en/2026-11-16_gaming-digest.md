## IV. Streaming & Entertainment

### 10. FOX completes Roku acquisition, forms FOX Digital Platforms

**Summary:** On Nov 6 FOX completed its Roku acquisition: Roku shareholders receive $95.30 in cash plus 0.9799 FOX Class A shares per share, leaving FOX holders with about 74% of the combined company. FOX expects about $400 million in annual cost synergies within two years and is combining Roku, Tubi and FOX One into FOX Digital Platforms, co-led by Roku founder Anthony Wood and Paul Cheesbrough. Roku's platform, serving over 100 million households, is to stay open.
