## I. Studios, Publishers, and Major Products

### 1. MindsEye Developer Build A Rocket Boy Enters Administration (Studio)
**Summary:** GamingBolt, PlayStation Universe, and others reported on September 25 that Build A Rocket Boy—the Edinburgh studio founded by former Rockstar / Grand Theft Auto producer Leslie Benzies—has confirmed insolvency and entered administration, appointing Opus Advisory Group as administrators to seek protection from creditor lawsuits. Accounts through September 30, 2025 show an operating loss of about £47.5 million, a net loss of about £36.2 million, and roughly £61.8 million owed to creditors. Flagship title MindsEye launched in June 2025 on PC, PS5, and Xbox Series to harsh reviews and weak commercial traction; a fresh round of layoffs last week sharpened fears that the studio’s survival is now in doubt.

### 10. Esports Veteran Complexity Ceases Operations After 23 Years; IP Reverts to GameSquare (Esports)
**Summary:** Win.gg and Inven Global reported that Complexity Gaming founder Jason Lake confirmed the organization has shut down. Lake said the wind-down decision was made at the end of 2025 to close orderly rather than “crash into the sun.” His group reacquired Complexity from GameSquare in 2024 for about $10.36 million, with roughly $9.6 million on a seller note they could not refinance; brand and IP have reverted to GameSquare. Founded in 2003 and long defined by Counter-Strike, Complexity exited CS2’s top tier in August 2025. Lake said he is not leaving esports.
