## I. Big Publishers: Earnings and Studio Shifts

### 1. Roblox Q3: DAUs down 9% YoY as leadership bets on generative AI (earnings)
**Summary:** Roblox reported third-quarter 2026 results on October 29, with industry follow-up on October 30. Revenue was about $1.57 billion, up roughly 16% year over year, while bookings were about $1.70 billion, down about 12%. Daily active users averaged about 138 million—up about 12% quarter over quarter but down about 9% year over year and below a prior ~145 million expectation—with nearly 34 billion hours engaged. Game Developer reported that more than 80% of the platform’s top 100 games in the quarter used AI tools; CEO David Baszucki told investors AI is reshaping how fast game content can be created. The company also outlined strategies such as Roblox Everywhere for self-publishing beyond the Roblox app.

**Commentary:** A tough year-over-year compare meets an AI narrative—investors will judge whether content throughput can actually bend the bookings curve back up.

### 2. Bloomberg follow-up: EA shuttered BioWare to prioritize a Star Wars: Battlefront title over Mass Effect (M&A aftermath)
**Summary:** Game Developer reported on October 30, citing Bloomberg, that EA’s shutdown of BioWare and cancellation of early-stage Mass Effect: Universe stemmed in part from roughly $18 billion in debt after its leveraged buyout, leaving the publisher unwilling to fund two big science-fiction projects at once and favoring a more predictable multiplayer Star Wars: Battlefront shooter. The move sits uneasily beside EA’s earlier public pivot away from licensed games, even as Marvel’s Iron Man and other licensed titles continue. BioWare’s closure and about 400 job cuts (~3% of staff) had already been widely reported around October 21.

**Links:**

- [Game Developer — BioWare shutdown and Battlefront trade-off](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/business/report-ea-shut-down-bioware-in-favor-of-star-wars-battlefront-sequel-after-previously-decrying-licensed-games)
- [Kotaku — Bloomberg report readout](https://kotaku.com/bioware-shut-down-mass-effect-star-wars-battlefront-3-ea-cancel-2000751575)

**Commentary:** The first big private-equity knife cut a narrative RPG studio—debt cycles prefer reusable shooter pipelines over long-cycle RPGs.

## IV. M&A, Regulation, and Esports

### 11. Evolution cancels Galaxy Gaming deal after licensing approvals miss the deadline (M&A)
**Summary:** SectorWire reported on October 30 that Evolution terminated its planned roughly $85 million acquisition of Galaxy Gaming after required gaming regulatory approvals were not obtained by the agreement deadline, and will pay a termination fee exceeding $5 million. Galaxy’s earlier 8-K said the July 17, 2026 Outside Date had passed with two gaming approvals still outstanding and Evolution declining to waive them. The collapse underscores how license gates can kill cross-border gaming-tech deals faster than price terms.

**Links:**

- [SectorWire — Evolution cancels the deal](https://sectorwire.news/item/evolution-cancels-galaxy-gaming-deal-after-regulatory-approvals-remain/)
- [StockTitan — Galaxy Gaming 8-K summary](https://www.stocktitan.net/sec-filings/GLXZ/8-k-galaxy-gaming-inc-reports-material-event-8ec50859d6fb.html)

**Commentary:** When approvals are the uncontrollable variable, the merger clock often leaves sellers with a break fee instead of a closing.
