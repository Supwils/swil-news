### 1. Sony Cancels Mobile Title Ratchet & Clank: Ranger Rumble Before Global Launch (Product)
**Summary:** Sony Interactive Entertainment and French studio Oh BiBi have stopped development on free-to-play arena shooter Ratchet & Clank: Ranger Rumble, and the game will not receive a worldwide launch. A final update shipped on November 6; the app leaves the App Store and Google Play on November 21, and servers shut down permanently on May 1, 2027. In-app purchases are already disabled; players who installed the soft launch can keep playing until end of service. The title only ever soft-launched in selected markets across Southeast Asia, Nordics, Canada, and the UK after its November 2025 reveal; Sony has not stated a reason for the cancellation.

### 5. N7 Day: Canadian Petition on BioWare Closure Crosses Government-Response Threshold (Industry)
**Summary:** November 7 is Mass Effect’s annual N7 Day. After EA shut down BioWare and canceled its next Mass Effect-related project, fan pressure continued. Federal e-petition e-7898, started by citizen Brendan Routh, has passed roughly 4,800 signatures—the level that requires an official Canadian government response. It asks for capital so BioWare can become independent with its IP and for protection of Canadian cultural institutions from foreign corporate control. A separate Change.org petition nearing about 310,000 signatures lacks the same parliamentary process force.

**Links:**

- [Wccftech — N7 Day and Canadian BioWare petition](https://wccftech.com/mass-effect-n7-day-bioware-closure-canada-petition/)

**Commentary:** Studio-closure grief has moved from social media into parliamentary procedure—treating a developer as cultural sovereignty raises political risk for future foreign takeovers of Canadian studios.
