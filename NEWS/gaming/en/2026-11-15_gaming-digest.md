## II. Regulation & Deals

### 4. EU Digital Fairness Act draft targets loot boxes and paid progress for minors (Regulation)
**Summary:** The European Commission tabled a Digital Fairness Act proposal around November 11; Game Developer, heise, Politico, and Commission pages continued coverage through mid-November. The draft would ban selling variable-reward loot-box systems to minors, restrict pay-to-win / pay-to-progress aimed at under-18s, default-disable purchasable virtual currency for minors, require loot-box odds and contents disclosure, show real-money equivalents for virtual-currency prices, and curb dark patterns that push overspending. Parliament and Council still must negotiate; industry groups warn the pay-to-win wording is overly broad. Binding effect is widely discussed around roughly 2029 after transition periods.

**Links:**

- [Game Developer — EU Digital Fairness Act and games](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/business/european-union-proposes-digital-fairness-act-which-could-have-major-effects-on-game-development)
- [heise — What the EU draft means for loot boxes](https://www.heise.de/en/news/Digital-Fairness-Act-What-the-EU-draft-means-for-loot-boxes-in-games-11521109.html)

**Commentary:** Brussels is writing “kids + loot boxes” into hard law—F2P economies may need age-split systems, not just clearer copy.
