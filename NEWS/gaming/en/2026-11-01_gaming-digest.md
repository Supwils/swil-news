### 2. Phantom Blade Zero tops 775,000 Steam concurrent players—2026’s highest single-player peak so far (launch)
**Summary:** IT Home reported on November 1 that S-GAME’s wuxia action RPG Phantom Blade Zero (影之刃零), which launched October 29, hit a Steam concurrent peak of 775,647 over the weekend—the highest single-player Steam peak of the year to date. The PS5 version also cracked the top 10 on about 98% of PlayStation Store storefronts worldwide. Metacritic scores sit around 88 (PC) and 86 (PS5), with IGN at 9/10 and a 100% OpenCritic recommend rate. Alinea Analytics estimated about 850,000 pre-orders and roughly $43 million in pre-order revenue through October 28, with Steam contributing about 70%.

## II. China and Asia Markets

### 5. Wuhua Mixin ops dispute escalates: Cipaishe claims takeover as Bilibili alleges Longyuan cut servers (dispute)
**Summary:** IT Home and National Business Daily followed up on November 1 after the WeChat account for Wuhua Mixin (物华弥新) said on October 31 that, due to irreconcilable ops disagreements with the prior publisher, the developer Cipaishe Studio would become the operating entity, pausing in-client top-ups in favor of official web payments. That afternoon, bilibili’s ops team said Chengdu Longyuan Network terminated official server access at 00:10 on October 31 without notice, told players to migrate to a third-party server, and was under debt pressure—calling the move a unilateral contract breach, saying evidence had been preserved, and warning players not to share credentials with unknown channels. The artifact-personification strategy card game launched in April 2024.
