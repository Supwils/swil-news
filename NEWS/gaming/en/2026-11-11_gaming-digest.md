## II. Regulation & Deals

### 6. EU formally proposes Digital Fairness Act curbing minor microtransactions, loot boxes, and pay-to-win; industry warns of a competitiveness hit (regulation)
**Summary:** The European Commission on Wednesday, November 11, unveiled consumer-protection proposals including the Digital Fairness Act. POLITICO reported the package would add sector-specific video-game limits alongside the EU’s mid-September child online protection rules. Draft rules would bar pay-to-progress or pay-to-win microtransactions for minors, restrict paid loot boxes, and require in-app virtual currencies off by default for under-18s; fines could reach 6% of global revenue in cases affecting more than two-thirds of the EU population. The European Game Developer Federation and Video Games Europe (including Sony and Epic) jointly called the pay-to-win definition “fundamentally flawed” and potentially covering “any purchase.” The proposal still requires negotiation with other EU institutions.

**Links:**

- [POLITICO — EU Digital Fairness Act and gaming industry](https://www.politico.eu/article/european-gaming-firms-say-new-eu-consumer-rules-will-cripple-the-industry-digital-fairness-act/)
