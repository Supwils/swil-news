### 3. Remedy Q3 revenue up 226% as Control Resonant becomes its fastest-selling game (earnings)
**Summary:** On November 3, Remedy Entertainment published its Q3 2026 business review: revenue of €39.6 million, up 226% year on year; EBITDA of €6.8 million; and operating profit of €0.6 million after a prior-year loss. ## II. Regulation, M&A, and Streaming

### 6. EU conditionally clears JD.com’s takeover of MediaMarktSaturn parent CECONOMY under foreign-subsidy rules (M&A)
**Summary:** On November 3, the European Commission approved under the Foreign Subsidies Regulation (FSR), subject to commitments, JD.com’s acquisition of German consumer-electronics retailer CECONOMY (MediaMarkt, Saturn, MediaWorld). The probe found preferential financing, tax incentives, and grants from China could distort the internal market—especially by strengthening logistics and technology capabilities and enabling aggressive pricing. Commitments include market-term intra-group financing and offering JD.com warehousing, logistics, and technology services to third-party retailers on equal terms for 10 years, monitored by an independent trustee. CECONOMY said this was the last outstanding regulatory clearance in the offer document, with closing expected within days.

**Links:**

- [CECONOMY — final regulatory clearance](https://www.ceconomy-mms.com/en/article/neues-kapitel-ceconomy-und-jdcom-erhalten-abschliessende-behordliche-genehmigung)
- [IEU Monitoring — EU clears deal with subsidy safeguards](https://ieu-monitoring.com/editorial/eu-commission-clears-jd-com-takeover-of-ceconomy-with-subsidy-safeguards/1253822)

**Commentary:** Europe’s largest consumer-electronics chain changing hands hinged less on classic merger structure than on whether Chinese subsidies can spill through a brick-and-mortar network—FSR is now a hard gate for Chinese buyers in the EU.

### 8. Dutch ACM publishes clearance for Videoland to take sole control of Viaplay Nederland sports streaming (regulation)
**Summary:** On November 3, the Netherlands Authority for Consumers and Markets (ACM) published its October 20 concentration decision allowing DPG Media’s Videoland to acquire sole control of Viaplay Nederland, taking over sports licenses and subscribers covering Formula 1, Premier League football, PDC darts, and other live sport in the Netherlands. ACM said it expects no adverse effects for viewers, sports-rights purchasing, or advertising sales, noting the licenses are time-limited and will return to the market when they expire. NLnow reported the clearance the same day.

**Links:**

- [ACM — Videoland / Viaplay Nederland decision](https://www.acm.nl/nl/publicaties/videoland-mag-uitsluitende-zeggenschap-verkrijgen-over-viaplay-nederland-concentratiebesluit)
