### 2. Xbox Disc-to-Digital Opens to Series X and Xbox One Owners (Platform)
**Summary:** Polygon reported on September 30 that a system update in the early hours rolled Disc-to-Digital out to all Xbox Series X and Xbox One owners. Inserting a disc claims a digital license automatically; if that fails, players can press Start and choose “Claim digital license.” The license can expire or be revoked if the disc is put into another console, and revoked titles appear on a Revoked Digital Games list. The physical disc still works and can be lent or resold. Xbox One players can claim last-generation licenses and play them on a Series S with the same account.

**Links:**

- [Polygon — Xbox disc-to-digital full release](https://www.polygon.com/xbox-disc-to-digital-full-release/)

**Commentary:** The digital license follows the disc, so the physical copy can still change hands, while one disc cannot serve as a standing license on two consoles at once.

## II. Regulation and Licenses

### 6. EU Consumer Authorities Name In-Game Currencies; Industry Rejects a “Value” Label (Regulation)
**Summary:** PocketGamer.biz reported on September 30 that the EU Consumer Protection Cooperation Network opened coordinated actions asking Activision Blizzard, Crytek, InnoGames, King, Mojang, Plarium, PLR Worldwide Sales, Riot Games, Supercell, and Ubisoft to align commercial practices for in-game virtual currencies with EU consumer law. Games under assessment include Hunt: Showdown 1896, Forge of Empires, Candy Crush Saga, Minecraft, Mech Arena, Gardenscapes, Valorant, Clash of Clans, For Honor, Diablo Immortal, and Call of Duty: Mobile. The network said talks after last year’s Key Principles did not produce a satisfactory result. The same day, the European Game Developers Federation and Video Games Europe described the actions as covering nine studios and rejected defining in-game currencies as digital representations of value; they said they have asked for the legal basis since spring 2025 and still have not received it. Justice Commissioner Michael McGrath said national authorities, with Commission support, will enforce the rules.

**Links:**

- [PocketGamer.biz — CPC targets King, Supercell, Mojang and others](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/europes-cpc-targets-king-supercell-mojang-and-others-over-virtual-currencies/)
- [Games Press — Industry response to CPC principles on in-game currencies](https://www.gamespress.com/Industrys-response-to-the-CPC-Networks-non-legally-binding-principles-)

**Commentary:** Enforcement has reached named companies and games, while the dispute is still whether a virtual currency counts as “value.”

### 8. Mexican Lawmakers Propose Telling Buyers Whether a Digital Game Is Owned or Licensed (Regulation)
**Summary:** TweakTown on September 30 and Eurogamer on September 29 reported that Luis Donaldo Colosio Riojas, chair of the Mexican Senate’s Digital Rights Commission, and federal deputy Iraís Reyes presented a consumer-protection initiative, first shown in an Instagram video. It would require digital platforms to say whether a purchase grants ownership or a license, require a way to keep a game playable if servers shut down, and require foreign sellers of digital goods in Mexico to keep a local address or legal representative. TweakTown said there is no bill number, final text, or vote schedule. The pair filed a competition complaint in August over Sony’s plan to end PlayStation disc production by 2028.

**Links:**

- [TweakTown — Mexican lawmakers push digital game ownership initiative](https://www.tweaktown.com/news/113825/mexican-lawmakers-push-new-initiative-to-protect-digital-game-ownership-rights/index.html)
- [Eurogamer — Mexican lawmakers join the fight for digital games ownership](https://www.eurogamer.net/mexican-lawmakers-join-fight-digital-games-ownership)

**Commentary:** The initiative still has no final text, and its scope has already moved from Sony’s discs to license disclosure and shutdown preservation on every digital storefront.
