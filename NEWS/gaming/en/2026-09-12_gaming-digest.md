### 3. Moss developer Polyarc shutting down after 12 years; about 30 staff affected (Studio closure)
**Summary:** PC Gamer reported on Sep 12 that Polyarc, known for the VR platformer series Moss, said on LinkedIn it is winding down after nearly 12 years. ## II. M&A, Labor & Industry Pressure

### 5. Saudi PIF reportedly weighing an EA–Savvy Games merger into a cross-platform giant (M&A)
**Summary:** Bloomberg and Reuters reported on Sep 10 (with follow-ups including Chuapp on Sep 12) that people familiar with the matter say Saudi Arabia’s Public Investment Fund is considering combining Electronic Arts with Savvy Games to coordinate console/PC franchises (such as EA Sports FC, Battlefield, and The Sims) with mobile holdings (including Monopoly Go! and Pokémon GO). A PIF-led consortium took EA private for roughly $55 billion; no final decision has been made, a deal is unlikely before Savvy completes its about $6 billion Moonton acquisition, and any combination would face multi-jurisdiction antitrust scrutiny.

**Links:**

- [Reuters — Saudi wealth fund weighs EA-Savvy merger](https://www.reuters.com/technology/saudi-wealth-fund-weighs-ea-savvy-merger-bloomberg-news-reports-2026-09-10/)

### 6. Nearly 1,900 Blizzard union workers ratify first contracts: AI rules and layoff recall rights (Labor)
**Summary:** Per the Communications Workers of America, Kotaku, and the Los Angeles Times, nearly 1,900 union-represented Blizzard Entertainment workers ratified first contracts with Microsoft-owned Blizzard after about two years of bargaining, covering teams across World of Warcraft, QA, Hearthstone, Overwatch, Diablo, and more. Terms include wage increases, just-cause protections, a three-day in-office hybrid week, disability accommodations, and grievance procedures; an industry first requires Blizzard to discuss, evaluate, and bargain over workplace AI use; laid-off workers gain an extra four weeks of severance and 14-month recall rights into open roles across Blizzard bargaining units. Blizzard president Johanna Faries called ratification a major studio milestone.

**Links:**

- [CWA — Blizzard workers ratify historic contracts](https://cwa-union.org/news/blizzard-entertainment-workers-ratify-historic-video-game-contracts-cwa)
- [Kotaku — Blizzard union contract covers AI and layoffs](https://kotaku.com/blizzard-workers-union-contract-microsoft-2000732422)

**Commentary:** In a layoff-heavy industry, locking AI use and post-layoff recall into hard contract language sets a benchmark for other studio negotiations.
