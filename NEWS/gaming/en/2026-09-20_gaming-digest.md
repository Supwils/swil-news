### 7. Disney+ Updates Terms So Every Plan, Including Premium, Can Show Pre-/Post-Roll Ads (Streaming)
**Summary:** Dexerto and follow-up coverage on September 20 said Disney+ revised its subscriber agreement so every tier may include promotional content, sponsorships, or ads before or after movies and series—including Premium, long sold as the higher-priced, less interrupted option (coverage cited about $18.99 a month in the U.S.). Users must accept the updated terms on login to keep watching. Help-center language still says Standard and Premium on-demand titles can run without mid-program commercial breaks, but may include limited promos before or after playback, while live and linear content can carry regular breaks. Junior Mode profiles remain free of commercial ads; ad blockers may interrupt playback.
