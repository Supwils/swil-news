### 6. Epic revises UE6 plan: no Blueprints from Early Access, betting on AI migration (engine)
**Summary:** PC Gamer and others reported on November 22 that Epic EVP of Development Marcus Wassmer confirmed Unreal Engine 6—including Early Access—will not include Blueprints or the Actor Gameplay Framework; gameplay will use Verse and the new Scene Graph. Epic had previously said Blueprints and Actors would coexist in UE6 Early Access / 6.0 for incremental upgrades; it now cites AI conversion advances to justify an earlier cutover, aiming for large-scale or near-automatic UE5→UE6 migration. Coverage notes Epic used AI assistance to convert more than 20,000 Fortnite Blueprints into roughly 3 million lines of Verse; some studios worry designers lose the visual middle layer.

**Links:**

- [PC Gamer — UE6 jettisons Blueprints for AI coding](https://www.pcgamer.com/gaming-industry/unreal-engine-6-to-jettison-key-scripting-feature-earlier-than-planned-in-favor-of-ai-coding/)

**Commentary:** Replacing visual scripting with LLM-readable code will reshape mid-size teams’ talent mix and toolchains as much as any single engine feature.
