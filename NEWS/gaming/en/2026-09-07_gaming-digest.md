### 1. Capcom’s Onimusha: Way of the Sword tops 1 million on day one; series clears 10 million as dormant-IP revival continues (Sales)

## II. Regulation & Regional M&A

### 5. Philippines’ PhilWeb discloses ~₱4.23B JKS investment; PSE suspends trading from September 8 (M&A)
**Summary:** Per PeraPulse and related September 7 coverage, PhilWeb Corporation said it and subsidiary PhilWeb Capital signed agreements to subscribe for about 10.73 million JKS Tech Solutions shares at ₱394 each—about ₱4.23 billion in total (roughly ₱1.34 billion for PhilWeb and ₱2.88 billion for the subsidiary). The outlay far exceeds PhilWeb’s roughly ₱548.5 million asset base as of June 30, triggering Philippine Stock Exchange substantial-acquisition rules; PSE will suspend PhilWeb trading from September 8 pending fuller disclosure. JKS is described as the systems operator for regulated online platform Epic Game. Reciprocally, JKS plans to buy about 81.38 million PhilWeb treasury shares for roughly ₱1.34 billion (~4.85% stake). The deal follows Lance Gokongwei’s recent ~₱2 billion capital injection and chair appointment.

## V. Esports & Platforms

### 10. CS2 “fake VAC ban” exploit can cancel matches without a fresh real ban (Platform security)
**Summary:** TalkEsport reported on September 7 that Counter-Strike 2 players and dataminers described a vulnerability affecting VAC Live: an attacker can spoof a VAC-banned status, prompting VAC Live to cancel the match immediately. Because the system treats the ban as already present, a new real ban may not be issued. Reports link the issue to how the client and server handle disconnect reasons; the bug has reportedly been filed with Valve. As of publication, Valve had not issued an official statement or patch. Abuse could undermine competitive integrity in Premier and other ranked modes where results matter.

**Links:**

- [TalkEsport — CS2 fake VAC ban exploit lets cheaters cancel losing matches](https://www.talkesport.com/news/cs2/cs2-fake-vac-ban-exploit-cancel-matches/)

**Commentary:** When anti-cheat can be spoofed into ending games, ranked trust collapses faster than any meta shift.
