## II. Streaming & Entertainment

### 7. Reports: YouTube agrees in principle to ~$3 billion school-district addiction settlement (regulation)
**Summary:** Bloomberg and follow-on coverage on November 20–21 said YouTube has reached an agreement in principle to pay about $3 billion to thousands of U.S. school districts that accuse its design of harming student mental health, plus additional teen product safeguards. The deal still needs majority district buy-in, months of paperwork, and approval from U.S. District Judge Yvonne Gonzalez Rogers in Oakland; Alphabet did not immediately comment. Districts have also sued Meta, Snap, and TikTok, with another federal trial eyed for February 2027.

**Links:**

### 12. NVIDIA ships GeForce Hotfix 621.35 to stop idle drivers hammering SSDs
