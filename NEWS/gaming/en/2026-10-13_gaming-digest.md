## I. Publishers and Products

### 1. Microsoft forms Xbox Labs to test decompiled ports and cross-game remixes (publisher)
**Summary:** The Verge and GeekWire reported on October 13 that Xbox CEO Asha Sharma announced Xbox Labs in a memo to employees that day, led by Scott Van Vliet, Xbox chief technology officer since May, and staffed as a small group of game makers, engineers, and scientists. The memo points to games rapidly decompiled onto hardware they were not designed for, Windows builds translated to Apple Silicon, and browser ports through WebAssembly and WebGPU, and says people spend more than 72 billion hours a year playing Xbox games, roughly five times the estimated time spent writing code worldwide. GeekWire notes that the memo never uses the word AI and instead frames the work around agents, while The Verge describes the experiments as AI-powered ports and remixes. Sharma wrote that priorities are not changing, and that Labs explores new technology without abandoning work already underway.

### 2. NVIDIA doubles DLSS 5 model speed and dates Hogwarts Legacy for November 5 (graphics)
**Summary:** NVIDIA's GeForce site posted on October 13 that the DLSS 5 3D-guided neural rendering model is getting 2x faster this week: the October update shrinks the footprint by 24% to roughly 118 million parameters, cuts 4K VRAM use by 20%, and halves GPU overhead versus the September version. The faster model debuts in NBA 2K27 on October 16 on GeForce RTX 50 Series GPUs, RTX Spark laptops, and GeForce NOW, with frame rates up to 1.4x, while Hogwarts Legacy adds DLSS 5 on November 5 and NVIDIA says that game has sold more than 40 million copies. Where Winds Meet is set for late November, with more than 100 million players worldwide and a Steam peak above 250,000 cited by NVIDIA; a same-day Chinese report, citing NetEase, expects November support plus a preview and calls it NetEase's only title with official DLSS 5 support. A new Frame Generation Performance Mode is in the NVIDIA app beta today, up to 35% faster on a GeForce RTX 5060, and RTX Remix gets DLSS 5 on October 23.
