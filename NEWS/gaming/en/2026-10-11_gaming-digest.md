### 1. Cyberleek threatens a story-mode GTA 6 build, tied to a market cap and physical discs (leak)
**Summary:** GamesRadar and VGC both reported on October 11 that an account calling itself Cyberleek said, in a message published over the weekend, that if its cryptocurrency reaches a $30 million market cap it will release a GTA 6 build with "full story mode, played to the end, credits and all." The way out it offered Take-Two is physical discs and a public apology. VGC writes that the account has been posting unauthorized footage for nearly two months, with a 25-minute clip this week. GamesRadar quotes the preservation group Stop Killing Games as saying illegal methods are unacceptable and do nothing to protect access to games people have paid for. Neither piece independently verifies that the account holds a complete, finishable build. VGC describes the release as about a month away.

### 4. Disney confirms an Artemis Fowl animated series, with no air date (streaming)
**Summary:** Polygon reported on October 11 that author Eoin Colfer announced at Sunday's "25 Years of Artemis Fowl" panel at New York Comic Con that Disney has greenlit an animated series for Disney Channel and Disney+. Colfer, Ben Juwono, and Colleen Evanson are executive producers. Polygon cites Disney as saying the show will dig deeper into the characters and lore. Ayo Davis, president of Disney Kids & Family, said in a release that the series is a way to introduce the books to a new generation. The books number 11, have sold more than 35 million copies, and have been translated into 48 languages. The 2020 live-action film went straight to Disney+, drew widely negative reviews, and has since been removed. Disney did not announce a release date.

### 5. Yoozoo outlines three AI-agent directions for games at a Shanghai conference (Asia)
**Summary:** Tonghuashun, at 17:26 on October 11, carried a Yoozoo Network post: the 2026 China Metaverse Conference was held that day in Shanghai, hosted by the Chinese Association for Artificial Intelligence and co-organized by Tongji University's Shanghai Research Institute for Intelligent Autonomous Systems, under the theme of a boundless blend of virtual and real worlds. Yoozoo was the only industry speaker on the main forum. Yu Huasong, head of the Hong'an Lab in the company's AI innovation institute, spoke on how AI agents could move game creation from generating content to generating worlds. The post lists three directions: multimodal content generation, NPCs with memory and decision-making, and one-click creation that turns a written idea into a playable result. The company says its internal AI platforms and tools are already used in hundreds of business scenarios. Yoozoo's own news list carries the same headline dated October 11.
