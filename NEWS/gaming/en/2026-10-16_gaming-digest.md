### 5. Jagex cancels RuneScape Reignited (product)
**Summary:** Jagex creative director Mod Ryan said on the official site on October 15 that RuneScape Reignited is cancelled. He wrote that feedback after the RuneFest reveal showed the plan had missed the mark, and that growth from returning and new players should not come at the expense of the existing community. ### 8. PEGI starts raising age ratings on some released digital-only games (regulation)
**Summary:** GamesMarket reported on October 16 that PEGI has begun, in October, raising age ratings for digital-only games that are already on sale when they use paid random items such as loot boxes. The article treats a balloon event added in June as a likely cause and says PEGI has not confirmed that explanation. The U.S. ESRB Teen rating is unchanged in that report.

### 10. Steam's client beta tests achievement sets (platform)
**Summary:** Valve posted on October 15 that Achievement Sets are in beta in that day's Steam Client beta. Developers can group achievements into base-game sets and DLC sets. Base-game progress shows in blue. DLC achievements stay out of overall progress until the player owns the content, then show in purple. Finishing a set adds a trophy to the profile, and that completion is kept if achievements are added later. Developers can also archive a single achievement or a whole seasonal set so players who missed it are not blocked, while people who unlocked it still see it. Games that already support sets include Portal 2, Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4, and Euro Truck Simulator 2.

**Links:**

- [Steam News — Achievement Sets beta](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/593110/view/710037059469839141)

**Commentary:** What can be checked is the rule set inside the client beta, not a finished switch of the achievement pages for the whole catalog.

### 12. Netflix names Peter Rice chief content officer (streaming)
**Summary:** CNBC reported on October 16 that Netflix said on Thursday that former Disney executive Peter Rice will become chief content officer, overseeing global scripted and unscripted series, film, and live events, replacing Bela Bajaria after about a decade at the company. Bajaria joined in 2016 and later ran a content budget of about $20 billion, including live deals such as WWE and the NFL. CNBC said the appointment comes days after cuts of roughly 1,000 jobs, about 6% of staff. Viewership rose 2% in the first half, according to the July disclosure. Nielsen's July figures put Netflix at 7.8% of TV viewing and YouTube at 14.2%. Netflix is due to report fiscal third-quarter earnings on Tuesday. Rice is also developing a film adaptation of the game Elden Ring.
