## II. Streaming and Deals

### 7. Paramount Closes Its $110 Billion Takeover of Warner Bros. Discovery and Names the Combined Company Skydance (Merger)
**Summary:** The BBC reported on October 6 that Paramount Skydance has completed its $110 billion acquisition of Warner Bros. Discovery. The combined company is named Skydance Corporation, with David Ellison as chairman and chief executive. The deal places HBO, CBS, Nickelodeon, Showtime, Comedy Central, DC Studios, and Food Network in one group, and adds franchises including Harry Potter, Game of Thrones, and The Lord of the Rings. The BBC says a settlement last month between U.S. states, Paramount, and Ellison cleared the way forward. That settlement requires at least 30 films a year, sets guardrails against AI-generated films, and requires 20% of film production in the United States for the first two years, rising to more than 30% over the following three years. Missing the annual quota forces a sale of Paramount's 49% stake in Miramax. The Verge, citing the closing release, says HBO Max and Paramount+ will "unify into a single service over time." No date or new name is given, and HBO chief executive Casey Bloys will oversee it. Ellison told investors in March that a merger would mean a little over 200 million direct-to-consumer subscribers. The Verge sets that beside Netflix's 325 million subscribers reported in January.

### 8. The Primetime Emmys Move to Prime Video in 2027 under a Six-Year Global Deal (Streaming)
**Summary:** Variety reported on October 6 that the Television Academy confirmed on Tuesday a six-year deal for Amazon's Prime Video to carry the Primetime Emmys, effective in September 2027. It is the first time the show leaves broadcast television since the ceremony began in 1949, and it ends the 31-year "wheel" among ABC, NBC, CBS, and Fox. The telecast sits in front of the paywall and will be free in the United States and in the more than 240 countries and territories where Prime Video operates. Variety cites sources for a global license fee of around $15 million a year. The previous wheel paid about $8 million to $10 million a year for domestic rights only. Academy chair Cris Abrego did not confirm the figure but said it was "definitely an increase." Mike Hopkins, head of Prime Video and Amazon MGM Studios, said a three-hour show is plenty and that Amazon does not plan to make it longer. Abrego also said a merger of the Television Academy and the National Academy of Television Arts and Sciences could happen by mid-2027, before the next Emmys.

**Links:**

- [Variety — Emmys move to Prime Video in a six-year deal](https://variety.com/2026/tv/news/emmys-prime-video-six-year-deal-15-million-a-year-1236899957/)

**Commentary:** Free access, a global footprint, and a six-year term are confirmed by the parties; the roughly $15 million annual fee is still a source figure the Academy did not confirm.

## III. Regulation

### 9. Licensed Betting Sites and Apps in Brazil Start Going Offline, with About R$1.7 Billion Estimated for Return (Regulation)
**Summary:** Agência Brasil reported at 9:55 a.m. on October 6 that sports-betting and online-gaming sites and apps begin going offline nationwide on Tuesday under provisional measure 1.394/2026, signed by President Lula on September 25. The deadline for users to withdraw balances themselves was 11:59 p.m. on October 5. Operators must report remaining balances to financial institutions by CPF on October 7 and 8. Banks are to return the money from October 9 through October 14. Where a bank cannot complete the refund, Caixa Econômica Federal may intermediate payments from October 14. The Secretariat of Prizes and Bets estimates that about 23 million bettors still had balances in those accounts. The government calculates that about R$1.7 billion will be returned. By the end of September, the ministries of Justice and Finance had requested blocks on 5,209 domains tied to illegal betting. The measure is in force but still needs congressional approval, or it lapses within the constitutional deadline.

- [Agência Brasil — Betting sites start going offline on Tuesday](https://agenciabrasil.ebc.com.br/geral/noticia/2026-10/bets-comecam-sair-do-ar-nesta-terca-feira)

### 10. Philippine Raids on Suspected Unlicensed Offshore Gaming Detain 244 Foreign Nationals in Mindanao (Regulation)
