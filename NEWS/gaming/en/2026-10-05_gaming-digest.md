### 1. Jagex Pulls the RuneScape 3 Dragon Slayer 2 Trailer and Says It Will Investigate Generative AI (Live Ops)
**Summary:** Kotaku and PC Gamer reported on October 5 that Jagex removed the RuneScape 3 Dragon Slayer 2 trailer shown at RuneFest over the weekend from official channels. Social media manager Alex Sween wrote on Reddit that an external partner produced the trailer and had explicitly confirmed generative AI would not be used. After issues including a six-fingered hand appeared, the studio apologized and said it was investigating urgently. Jagex restated that it does not use generative AI to create its games, artwork, or creative assets, and that it will not knowingly allow partners to use it. This is the second such partner dispute in quick succession, after an Old School RuneScape T-shirt made with 100 Thieves. The studio said it will overhaul review processes after RuneFest and commission community creators to retell Dragon Slayer II.

## II. Regulation

### 6. More Than 480,000 Back Brazil's Betting Ban in a Senate Consultation, and Licensed Sites Go Offline Tomorrow (Regulation)
**Summary:** European Gaming reported on October 5 that, by 07:10 that day, the Senate e-Cidadania portal showed 481,203 votes for Provisional Measure No. 1.394 and 80,727 against, for 561,930 votes in total. The outlet calculated support at about 85.6% from those portal figures. The consultation does not bind senators. The measure Lula signed on September 25 bans the operation, offer, intermediation, and advertising of fixed-odds betting, covering sports betting, online casino games, and foreign operators serving players in Brazil. Under the government's timetable, licensed operators must take sites and apps offline from October 6. All licences end on October 25, with no refund of fees and no compensation. The National Association of Games and Lotteries and the Brazilian Institute for Responsible Gaming asked the Supreme Federal Court on September 28 to suspend the measure. On October 2, Justice Luiz Fux gave the Attorney General's Office 72 hours to respond. No ruling had been published when the article was written. In the October 4 first round, Flávio Bolsonaro took 47.04% and Lula 45.15%. The runoff is on October 25.

**Links:**

- [European Gaming — Brazil Senate consultation on the betting ban](https://europeangaming.eu/portal/latest-news/2026/10/05/215344/brazil-betting-ban-senate-consultation-flavio-bolsonaro/)

### 9. Alberta Says Enforcement Against Unlicensed Gambling Sites Begins After October 13 (Regulation)
**Summary:** SiGMA reported on October 5 that Alberta Minister of Service Alberta and Red Tape Reduction Dale Nally said at G2E in Las Vegas that, after the transition period ends on October 13, the province will use available enforcement tools against unlicensed operators that keep serving players in Alberta. Measures under consideration include cease-and-desist letters and action against payment processors that handle transactions for unlicensed operators. App stores and social media companies could also be used to limit access to unlicensed products. Nally said the number of licensed sites is expected to rise from about 35 to about 60 by the deadline. Provincial figures showed 31 regulated sites operating as of mid-September. The regulated iGaming market opened on July 13. SiGMA said the remarks were reported by RG.org from the G2E appearance.
