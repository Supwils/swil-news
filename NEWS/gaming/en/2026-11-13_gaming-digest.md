## II. Regulation & Deals

### 5. EU Digital Fairness Act draft targets loot boxes and paid progress; paid random rewards banned for minors (regulation)
**Summary:** The European Commission proposed a Digital Fairness Act on November 11. Mid-November analyses from GameDeveloper, heise, and The Verge say the draft would ban paid variable-reward systems (including loot boxes) for minors, require odds and reward-type disclosures for adult purchases, and let players disable such systems. Games aimed at minors would default virtual currency off unless a guardian enables it, and would restrict pay-to-win / pay-to-progress for under-18s. The package also hits dark patterns, easier cancellation, and requiring fresh consent before free trials convert to paid plans; fines could reach about 6% of worldwide annual turnover. It still needs Parliament and Council approval and is not yet law.

**Links:**

- [GameDeveloper — EU Digital Fairness Act and games](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/business/european-union-proposes-digital-fairness-act-which-could-have-major-effects-on-game-development)
- [heise — What the EU draft means for loot boxes](https://www.heise.de/en/news/Digital-Fairness-Act-What-the-EU-draft-means-for-loot-boxes-in-games-11521109.html)

**Commentary:** Europe hard-coding “minors + paid randomness” as a ban zone would force global F2P economies beyond region-only patches if adopted.
