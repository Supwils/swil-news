### 1. Tokyo Game Show Cancels All of September 21; September 20 Still On (Show)
**Summary:** Tokyo Game Show organizers announced on September 19 that the entire public day on September 21, a Japanese national holiday, is canceled because Typhoon No. 25 is approaching. They cited stronger wind and rain expected that afternoon and possible public-transit disruption, and said attendee, exhibitor, and staff safety on the way home comes first. September 20 goes ahead as scheduled, and Sunday tickets will not be refunded, though some facilities or activities may still change with the weather. Monday tickets will be refunded through the original vendor, and some stage events planned for that day may be streamed once venue safety is confirmed. Anime News Network said this was the first five-day TGS; Thursday drew 53,630 people and Friday 52,545.

### 6. After WoW: Forever Overloaded Its Test, Developers Explained Why the Beta Stack Could Not Hold (Beta)
**Summary:** Gamersky's Chinese report on September 19 relayed Blizzard developers' explanation from the day before. Senior game designer Josh Greenfield called the beta the craziest numbers he had seen in 17 years at Blizzard and nearly eight years on Classic. Senior producer Tom Ellis wrote on September 18 that the test environment is nowhere near a production stack and that the whole beta used one regional entry point, so players were disconnected after long queues. The team needed about half an hour to find the bottleneck because the game and Battle.net showed little load. After looting and quest accepts slowed, Oracle engineers manually analyzed tables and set recurring jobs. World servers also ran hot on CPU and memory because empty maps were not shutting down; the fix needed restarts, and Blizzard added more world servers. GamesRadar published its write-up on September 18.

**Links:**

- [Gamersky via NetEase — Forever beta numbers](https://www.163.com/dy/article/L76IK62N0526K1KN.html)
