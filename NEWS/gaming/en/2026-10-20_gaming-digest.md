### 9. France's ANJ publishes a survey of hybrid digital games among ages 15-17: 39.0 percent played in the prior 12 months (regulation)
**Summary:** France's gambling authority, the ANJ, posted results of the 2025 study on a page dated October 19, 2026. In the ENJEU-Mineurs 2025 survey, run by ARPEJ with the ANJ, 39.0 percent of respondents aged 15 to 17 said they had played at least one "hybrid digital game" in the 12 months before the study. The share was 42.4 percent for boys and 35.4 percent for girls. The most common form was video games with casino elements (26.3 percent), then free trial play on casino platforms (18.1 percent) and social casino games (16.6 percent). Among these young people, 15.6 percent said they had spent money on the games. Of hybrid-game players, 58.8 percent also played real-money gambling. On the Canadian adolescent gambling inventory, 26.0 percent of mixed players scored at high severity, against 3.9 percent of those who only played real-money games. Among mixed players who spent money on hybrid games, 40.7 percent scored high severity, against 14.1 percent of those who played without spending. During hybrid-game play, 68.9 percent said they had seen ads urging them to bet on real-money games, mainly on TikTok and Instagram. SBC News reported the study on October 20.

**Links:**

- [ANJ — results page](https://www.anj.fr/pratique-des-jeux-numeriques-hybrides-chez-les-15-17-ans-resultats-de-la-recherche-enjeu-mineurs)

**Commentary:** The 39.0 percent window on the ANJ page is the 12 months before the survey; the qualitative line says some casino-element experiences can be an entry point, and the page does not assign that role to one named game.
