## II. M&A, Labor & Platforms

### 6. Arkane Lyon Union Open Letter: 19-Month Hiring Freeze “Created” Closure Case (Labor)
**Summary:** French game workers’ union STJV published an open letter on November 4 accusing Microsoft of a roughly 19-month group hiring freeze that artificially caused delays and cost overruns on Marvel’s Blade—then citing those same issues to justify closing Arkane Lyon. The union says production is progressing well and that shutting down and cancelling the project would likely cost more than finishing it. About 217 employees plus contractors are at risk if no serious buyer emerges; the buyer-search window has been extended to December 13. Demands include worker voice in choosing a buyer, a 24-month post-sale redundancy protection fund, and about €200,000 per worker if closure proceeds—union asks, not agreed terms.
