## II. Platforms, Streaming, and China Releases

### 5. Steam Tests Pyrowave: Trading Bandwidth for Lower Remote Play Latency (Platform)
**Summary:** Multiple September 22 reports said the September 21 Steam Client beta added an experimental Pyrowave codec for Remote Play. Valve says it uses the GPU to encode and decode for very low end-to-end latency, with optional YUV 4:4:4 and automatic HDR, at roughly five to ten times the bandwidth of H.264, HEVC, or AV1; wired gigabit Ethernet to the router is recommended. The option must be enabled in Remote Play advanced client settings. It currently targets desktop Steam clients, with Steam Link mobile support said to be coming soon, and is aimed at flat-screen streaming rather than VR.

**Summary:** MediaNama reported on September 22, citing The New Indian Express and related coverage, that MeitY told India’s Central Information Commission that as of September 1, 2026, the Online Gaming Authority of India had not formally registered a single game under the Promotion and Regulation of Online Gaming Act, 2025 and accompanying rules, while 201 online-gaming complaints had been received. ### 11. Riot: Vanguard Anti-Boost Actions Hit About 296,000 Accounts (Esports)
**Summary:** EarlyGame, PCGamesN, and others reported on September 22 that Riot said it has actioned 296,416 League of Legends and VALORANT accounts for boosting and rank manipulation since late 2025. Vanguard’s boosting detection expanded from League to VALORANT earlier in the summer and can flag higher-ranked players logging into others’ accounts as well as “hitchhikers.” Penalties include rank removal, temporary suspensions, and permanent bans, though not every action is a permanent ban. Riot said the system is not yet at full power and plans tougher rank removals and barriers against disposable accounts.
