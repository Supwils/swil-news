## I. Big Publishers, Labor & Products

### 1. Rockstar UK staff reject IWGB recognition deal by about 75% (labor)
**Summary:** Multiple outlets reported on November 26 that Rockstar Games’ UK workforce voted down a formal recognition agreement with the IWGB Game Workers Union: about 75.3% against and 24.7% in favor, with 1,544 ballots cast and roughly 93.2% turnout. Voting ran from November 12 through 26, overlapping Grand Theft Auto VI’s launch week. Recognition would have created a framework for collective talks on pay, overtime, bonuses, leave, pensions, and consultation on AI and layoffs; without it, Rockstar is not obliged to meet union reps regularly. IWGB said organizing will continue; Rockstar publishing head Jennifer Kolbe said the result backs direct collaboration. A related unfair-dismissal tribunal remains pending.

**Links:**

- [The Verge — GTA VI developers vote against unionization](https://www.theverge.com/games/1043169/rockstar-games-union-vote-iwgb)
- [The Independent — Rockstar UK staff vote against union recognition](https://www.independent.co.uk/news/business/rockstar-iwgb-edinburgh-glasgow-b3092300.html)

**Commentary:** Record launch heat plus record turnout still left formal recognition short—culture messaging won the ballot, but the workplace debate does not reset to zero.
