### 1. Funcom starts a restructuring and layoffs, with its CEO leaving at year-end (layoffs)
**Summary:** Funcom's press release is dated October 14, 2026, and PC Gamer reported it on October 17. The company says that after Dune: Awakening's PC launch in June 2025, the console versions arrived in September, and the studio will now focus on long-term operation of Dune: Awakening and Conan Exiles. Chief Product Officer Scott Junior will become CEO. Rui Casais, 22 years at the company and 11 as CEO, will leave at the end of this year and will handle the handover with Junior until then. The release says the restructuring has begun, the organization will shrink, and the number of people affected will be shared only after the process ends.

### 6. Bengaluru studio Spill Games raises a $10.5 million Series A (funding)
**Summary:** Indian Startup News reported on October 17 that Spill Games raised $10.5 million in a Series A led by Elevation Capital, with existing investors Centre Court Capital and PeerCapital participating. The studio was founded in 2024 by former PlaySimple executives Om Misra, Tapan Ranjan, and Harsh Garg. It makes free-to-play casual, puzzle, and hybrid-casual games for players outside India, earning from ads and in-app purchases. The company said the number of games showing potential to scale profitably doubled from three to six in less than three months. The report adds the April 2026 seed round of $3.1 million and the December 2024 round of $750,000 to this round for a total of about $14.35 million. Live titles include Cozy Finds, Sticker by Number, Zen Math Crossword, Bird Rescue Jam, and Grill Master.
