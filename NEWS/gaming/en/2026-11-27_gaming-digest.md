## II. Regulation & Market Data

### 5. Argentina’s Senate passes gambling-addiction law banning online betting ads and sponsorships (regulation)
**Summary:** Industry outlets reported on November 27 that Argentina’s Senate unanimously approved the Gambling Addiction Prevention Law on November 26 (72–0), banning online gambling advertising, promotion and sponsorship—including welcome bonuses, stadium naming, influencer marketing and festival brand presence. Operators must use biometric checks via the national ID registry, show responsible-gambling warnings covering about half the screen at session start and end, and face limits on credit cards and social-security accounts for betting. The executive has 120 days to issue implementing rules; unlicensed operators risk roughly three to eight years in prison. Legal critics said restrictions hit the licensed market hardest and may not stop minors on grey platforms.

**Links:**

- [NEXT.io — Argentina approves sweeping gambling ad ban](https://next.io/news/regulation/argentina-approves-sweeping-gambling-ad-ban/)
- [Gaming Americas — Argentina passes online gambling law](https://gamingamericas.com/latest-news/2026/11/27/127188/argentina-banning-gambling-advertising-and-sponsorships/)

**Commentary:** Another Latin American “ads to zero” experiment—the open question is whether bans curb addiction or push traffic to unlicensed sites.
