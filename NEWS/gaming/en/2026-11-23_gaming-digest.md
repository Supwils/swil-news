## II. Deals & Regulation

### 5. Plug In Digital acquires Twin Sails, forming an indie publishing and distribution group (M&A)
**Summary:** French publisher/distributor Plug In Digital announced on November 23 that it has acquired Paris indie publisher Twin Sails Interactive (News Tower and others), combining into Plug In Digital Group. Twin Sails’ former CEO Nicolas Godement becomes Group CEO; founder Francis Ingrand becomes Group Chairman. ### 7. EU opens consultation on Digital Fairness Act targeting loot boxes, virtual currency, and kids’ IAPs (regulation)
**Summary:** The European Commission has opened public feedback on its proposed Digital Fairness Act (deadline around January 14, 2027 Brussels time, extendable as language versions appear). The draft hits game monetization: paid loot boxes would need odds disclosure and an off switch for all players, with paid loot boxes and pay-to-win / pay-to-progress banned for minors; virtual currencies would show real-money value and stay off by default for children until a guardian approves; plus rules on addictive design toggles, dark patterns, and subscription cancellation. Trade groups including Ukie and Germany’s game have warned about cost and age-rating systems. Parliament and Council still must negotiate; industry timing estimates full application no earlier than about 2028.

**Links:**

- [GameDev.net — EU opens consultation on Digital Fairness Act](https://gamedev.net/news/7295-eu-opens-consultation-on-digital-fairness-act/)
- [HCR Law — What the proposed DFA means for game development](https://www.hcrlaw.com/news-and-insights/what-the-proposed-eu-digital-fairness-act-means-for-game-development/)

**Commentary:** Writing “minors + chance-based spend” into a directly applicable draft Regulation is harder and more predictable than scattered national rules.
