### 4. Nikkei: Sony’s PlayStation disc phaseout sparks global consumer-rights backlash (Regulation)
**Summary:** Nikkei Asia reported on September 8 that Sony Group’s plan to stop selling PlayStation 5 game discs has drawn backlash from consumer advocates worldwide, including voices in the UK and Mexico who say the move will curtail consumer choice. Sony previously confirmed on the PlayStation Blog in July that physical disc production for all new PlayStation console games will end starting January 2028, after which new titles will be digital-only via PlayStation Store and retailers, with no impact on games already released or releasing on disc before that date. Debate is shifting from format preference toward digital ownership and the future of second-hand markets.

### 5. Cambodia to suspend casino-based online gambling from October amid scam crackdown (Regulation)
**Summary:** Cambodianess reported on September 8 that Interior Minister Sar Sokha reaffirmed Cambodia will suspend online gambling operations at all casinos from October, aiming to tighten controls over tech-enabled scams, suspicious financial flows, and misuse of casino facilities. The temporary halt follows Prime Minister Hun Manet’s decision to stop online gambling livestreamed from casinos; during the pause, ministries are to build stronger legal frameworks, digital monitoring, and trained staff before any restart, with violators facing license revocation. Crackdown figures cited for July 2025–August 31, 2026 include inspections of about 793 suspected sites, dismantling of 86 scam centers, and action against 27 casinos (18 licenses revoked, nine suspended).
