### 8. Netflix, Amazon, and YouTube Form Streaming Access and Choice Alliance to Lobby on Sports Rights (Regulation)
**Summary:** On Monday, September 14, Netflix, Amazon, and YouTube launched the Streaming Access and Choice Alliance (SACA), led by tech trade group TechNet, to push “technology-neutral” policies that support streaming investment—including live sports. The move comes as U.S. lawmakers and the FCC scrutinize sports migrating from free TV to paid streamers and debate revisiting the Sports Broadcasting Act of 1961 antitrust exemption. The alliance said sports content volume on major global subscription streamers has risen about 52% since January 2024.
