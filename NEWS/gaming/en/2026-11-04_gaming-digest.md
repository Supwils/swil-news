## I. Majors & Products

### 1. Nintendo Losing a Switch Cartridge ROM Supplier as Macronix Exits the Business (Supply Chain)
**Summary:** On November 4, The Verge reported that Macronix, a major ROM supplier for Nintendo Switch cartridges, plans to gradually exit ROM production and shift capacity toward data-center NOR and NAND flash. Citing Taipei Times coverage of Macronix’s Q3 2026 earnings call, the report said ROM revenue fell 39% year over year while NOR rose 158% and NAND surged 768%; chairman Miin Wu said data-center NOR demand is robust and customers are chasing capacity. Nintendo recently reiterated it has “no plans” to abandon physical games, and said in August that physical titles accounted for about 38.5% of its game sales—higher than some peers.

**Links:**

- [The Verge — Nintendo is losing a Switch cartridge supplier](https://www.theverge.com/games/1026426/nintendo-switch-cartridges-rom-supplier-data-center)

**Commentary:** AI data-center demand is pulling memory capacity away from game cartridges, forcing physical-first holdouts to reshuffle suppliers and cost models.

### 2. Valve Tightens Steam Review Checklist: Five Minutes of In-Steam Playtime Required (Platform)
**Summary:** In a Steamworks post, Valve acknowledged a significant rise in store-page and build-review submissions; reviews that usually take 3–5 days can now take up to 12 business days, much of it from re-reviews of basic functional issues. The updated release checklist requires developers to launch via Steam’s green Play button and log at least five minutes of playtime (within the past seven days, or since the last build upload). Valve also added checks for achievement icons, Steam Cloud save paths, and redistributables such as DirectX and Visual C++. The Verge and Digital Trends followed on November 3–4; an independent tally cited in coverage suggested nearly half of October 2026 Steam releases disclosed some AI-generated content.

**Links:**

- [The Verge — Valve changes Steam review process](https://www.theverge.com/games/1025722/valve-steam-game-increase-submissions)
- [Digital Trends — Steam tougher restrictions amid AI games](https://www.digitaltrends.com/gaming/steam-is-drowning-in-new-games-and-valve-is-dropping-tougher-restrictions/)

**Commentary:** Making “did you actually launch it on Steam?” a hard gate is Valve’s automated answer to an AI-accelerated flood of low-quality submissions.

### 6. Global Games Industry Has Cut Nearly 11,000 Jobs This Year While Korean Firms Hire AI-Savvy Juniors (Labor)
**Summary:** Seoul Economic Daily’s English edition on November 4 cited nonprofit ASGC figures showing 10,977 confirmed games-industry job cuts from January 1 through October 20, 2026—already above 2025’s full-year 9,197 and higher than 2022 and 2023. Meanwhile Krafton opened a 2027 internship-to-hire program for more than 100 juniors with AI-skill assessments, and Nexon’s Nextorial program replaced traditional coding tests with AI interviews and AI-skill evaluation.
