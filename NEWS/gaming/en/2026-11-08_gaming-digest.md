## II. Streaming, Regulation & Deals

### 6. Fox Closes About $22 Billion Roku Acquisition, Pledges an Open Platform (M&A)
**Summary:** Fox Corp. said on November 6 that it had closed its roughly $22 billion acquisition of Roku—well ahead of the previously expected first half of 2027—gaining a platform that reaches more than 100 million streaming households. Fox pledged to keep Roku an “open ecosystem,” and reporting on DOJ clearance cites multi-year commitments to carry non-Fox content, avoid search discrimination, supply third-party data products on reasonable terms, and maintain content-data firewalls. A new Fox Digital Platforms unit pairs Roku with Tubi and Fox One; Fox expects about $400 million in annualized cost synergies within two years.

**Links:**

- [Variety — Fox closes $22B Roku takeover](https://variety.com/2026/tv/news/fox-closes-roku-acquisition-1236941275/)

### 7. UK ASA Bans Misleading Mobile-Game Ads From King, Scopely and Others (Regulation)
**Summary:** The UK Advertising Standards Authority ruled against ads for King’s Farm Heroes Saga, Scopely/Loom’s Pixel Flow, Omnilojo’s Dark War Survival, and Vistrex’s Idle Zombie Miner: Gold Tycoon, finding that non-interactive cinematics, unavailable mechanics, or brief bonus stages presented as core gameplay were misleading and must not run again in that form. One King ad and one Scopely ad were partly upheld despite “Not actual gameplay” disclaimers; Omnilojo and Vistrex complaints were fully upheld.

**Links:**

- [Mobilegamer.biz — ASA bans misleading mobile ads](https://mobilegamer.biz/asa-bans-misleading-mobile-game-ads-from-king-scopely-dark-war-survival-publisher-and-more/)

**Commentary:** Fake-gameplay user acquisition is getting more expensive—disclaimers no longer save big publishers, and cinematic bait hits smaller shops first.
