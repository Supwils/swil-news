## II. Regulation and Corporate Stance

### 8. Korea's National Assembly Passes a Game Industry Act Amendment on Domestic Agents (Regulation)
**Summary:** DigitalToday reported that the National Assembly passed the merged amendment in a plenary session on October 1. Sponsors included Cho Seung-rae of the Democratic Party and Kim Jae-won of the Rebuilding Korea Party, with the Culture, Sports and Tourism Committee combining the bills. If an overseas game company has a Korea-incorporated entity it established, or one it can dominate, that entity must be appointed first as the domestic agent, and the company must keep training and supervising the agent while it offers service. Failing to appoint an agent as required, or failing to supervise one, can draw an administrative fine of up to 20 million won; failing to submit materials or submitting false ones, without justifiable grounds, can draw up to 10 million won. The amendment takes effect six months after promulgation following a Cabinet meeting. The current enforcement decree still requires an agent when previous-year sales are at least 1 trillion won, or when average daily mobile installs in Korea are at least 1,000.

**Links:**

- [DigitalToday — Overseas game firms' domestic agent rules](https://www.digitaltoday.co.kr/en/view/110009/overseas-game-firms-domestic-agent-liability-tightened-user-protection-duties-expanded)

**Commentary:** The agent role moves from "any address in Korea" to a local entity first, and complaint handling plus document production become statutory duties.
