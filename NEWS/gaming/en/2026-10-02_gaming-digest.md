### 3. Reigns Developer Nerial Says It Is Entering Hibernation (Studio)
**Summary:** Gematsu published a statement on October 1 from founders Francois and Tamara Alliot, and Gamekult followed on October 2: the studio and Devolver jointly decided to end development at Nerial, which the founders call hibernation. The statement says the studio operated for 13 years and published 11 games, the latest being Reigns: The Witcher, released February 25 with CD Projekt RED. The team will look for other jobs, and the founders plan to rest before returning to game development. Devolver acquired the studio in 2021. Gamekult also notes that a little over a year before this closure, Devolver had already cut about 40% of Nerial's staff.

## II. Regulation and Mergers

### 5. The Paramount–Warner Merger Will Be Named Skydance (Merger)
**Summary:** Variety reported on October 2 that the soon-to-merge Paramount and Warner Bros. Discovery will operate as Skydance. Chairman and CEO David Ellison announced the name that day on X. The deal is set to close on October 6, combining two movie studios, HBO Max and Paramount+, and television businesses including CBS, CNN, MTV, TBS, Comedy Central, and Food Network. The franchise list spans Harry Potter, The Lord of the Rings, Game of Thrones, the DC Universe, Yellowstone, Mission: Impossible, Top Gun, and Nickelodeon. Class B common stock is planned to move the same day from Nasdaq to the New York Stock Exchange, with the ticker changing from PSKY to SKYD, and the corporate name is intended to become Skydance Corporation. Former Mattel chief executive Ynon Kreiz joins as co-CEO on October 5. Variety describes an approximately $111 billion deal: Ellison outbid Netflix, which had an agreement to buy Warner's streaming and studio businesses, and settled an antitrust suit by 12 states; the judge approved that settlement on September 30. The report also says debt after the merger is projected to exceed $80 billion. Paramount is issuing about $42.4 billion in bonds and taking out $8.5 billion plus €850 million in new loans. Larry Ellison has personally guaranteed $46.7 billion in equity financing, and commitments of about $24 billion from the sovereign wealth funds of Saudi Arabia, Qatar, and the United Arab Emirates will, according to Paramount, own 38.5% of the combined company.

### 6. EU Consumer Authorities Open a Coordinated Action on In-Game Currency (Regulation)
**Summary:** Ireland's Competition and Consumer Protection Commission said in an October 1 press release that it and consumer authorities across Europe, through the European Commission's Consumer Protection Cooperation Network, have launched a coordinated action against nine online gaming companies over potential breaches of consumer protection law. The review covers in-game purchases, virtual currencies, consumer rights at checkout, cost transparency, and the fairness of terms. The companies are Crytek GmbH, InnoGames GmbH, King.com Operations (Malta) Limited, Mojang AB, Plarium Europe, PLR Worldwide Sales Limited, Riot Games Limited, Supercell Oy, and Ubisoft EMEA SAS. Named games include Hunt: Showdown 1896, Forge of Empires, Candy Crush Saga, Minecraft, Mech Arena, Gardenscapes, Valorant, Clash of Clans, and For Honor. The points under review include real-money pricing, virtual currencies that obscure prices, pressure to buy more currency than needed, clear information before purchase, a 14-day withdrawal right for unused virtual currency, and safeguards for children and vulnerable consumers. Separately, the network is engaging Microsoft-group company Activision Blizzard UK over Diablo Immortal and Call of Duty, including personal-data collection, designs that may promote addiction, parental controls, direct marketing to children, and account blocking.

**Links:**

- [CCPC — Joint actions into online gaming practices](https://www.ccpc.ie/news-and-media/news/article/2026/10/01/ccpc-and-eu-consumer-authorities-launch-joint-actions-into-online-gaming-practices)

### 11. Steam Frame OS Beta Claims Battery Gains of Up to 12% (Platform)
**Summary:** GamingOnLinux on October 2 summarized Valve's notes for SteamOS 0.4.3 Beta on Steam Frame. Users opt in through Settings, System, System Update Channel.
