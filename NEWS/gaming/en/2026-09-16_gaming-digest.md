### 7. Devolver Digital Leaves London's AIM: Delisting Effective Sept. 16 (Delisting)
**Summary:** Indie publisher Devolver Digital's shareholders approved AIM cancellation with about 90% of votes in favor; per company notices, the last trading day was September 15 and cancellation took effect at 7:00 a.m. London time on September 16. An oversubscribed tender at 16 pence per share bought back up to about 23.32 million shares, with a second tender of up to about $5 million planned within 12 months after delisting, plus a matched-bargain facility for limited liquidity. Management argued public-market pricing created a “valuation disconnect” versus games' long-tail revenues and that going private better supports long-term publishing.

### 10. Cambodia to Halt Casino Online Gambling in October, Reviews 100+ Licences (Regulation)
