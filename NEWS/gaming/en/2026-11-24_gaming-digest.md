### 2. Terraria 1.4.6 opens Crossplay Phase One: same-network play without subscriptions (platform)
**Summary:** Re-Logic shipped Terraria 1.4.6 on November 24 with Crossplay Phase One: Xbox, PlayStation, Switch, Android, iOS, PC, and Mac clients on v1.4.6 can, in most cases, see and join each other on the same Wi-Fi or wired LAN without a console multiplayer subscription. Phase Two will target easier online cross-platform joins. Caveats remain: PlayStation and Xbox need an online subscription to reach online PC dedicated servers, and Switch cannot join privately hosted dedicated servers under Nintendo’s security rules (player-hosted worlds still work). The game has sold more than about 70 million copies.

## II. Engines, Data & Deals

### 5. Epic: UE6 will ship without Blueprints from early access, betting on Verse, Scene Graph, and AI migration (engine)
**Summary:** Coverage on November 24 followed Epic EVP of Development Marcus Wassmer’s update: Unreal Engine 6, including Early Access, will not include Blueprints or the Actor Gameplay Framework; gameplay will be built in Verse on Scene Graph. That reverses June’s plan for coexistence in Early Access and 6.0. Epic argues AI advances should slash—or automate—UE5-to-UE6 conversion costs, citing an AI-assisted prototype that ported more than 20,000 Fortnite Battle Royale Blueprints into about 3 million lines of Verse. UE5 keeps getting targeted features and fixes, so teams can stay put.

**Links:**

- [PocketGamer.biz — Epic to drop Blueprints from Unreal Engine 6](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/epic-games-to-drop-blueprints-from-unreal-engine-6-as-ai-reshapes-migration-plans/)
- [Game Developer — UE6 to remove Blueprints and Actor framework](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/programming/unreal-engine-6-to-remove-blueprints-and-actor-gameplay-framework-from-the-beginning)

**Commentary:** Cutting visual scripting early on an “AI will migrate you” thesis makes migration certainty—not slogans—the real risk for mid-size studios.

### 6. AppsFlyer: UK mobile IAP down 10.5% overall; action & strategy down 24.5% after GTA VI (data)
**Summary:** AppsFlyer reported on November 24 that in the first days after GTA VI’s November 19 launch, UK mobile-game IAP revenue fell about 10.5% versus the prior two-week average. Action & Strategy—the mobile genres closest in audience and play style—saw IAP drop about 24.5%, with sessions and installs down about 10.5% and 10.4%. Sports & Racing and Casino IAP fell about 12.5% and 10.4%. Overall sessions slipped only about 2.8%; product lead Adam Smart said even a few high spenders diverting budgets to an £80–100 console release can hit mobile revenue disproportionately. Broader UK app IAP rose about 15.8%, likely aided by early Black Friday shopping.

### 8. Lottomatica shareholders approve CIRSA absorption with 99.99% of votes cast (M&A)
**Summary:** Italy’s Lottomatica held an extraordinary meeting in Rome on November 23; results reported November 24 show about 99.99% of votes cast backed absorbing Spain’s CIRSA Enterprises. Shareholders also approved expanding the board from 11 to 13 directors, with two seats nominated by CIRSA’s Blackstone-backed controlling shareholder. Terms are 0.668 new Lottomatica shares per CIRSA share, leaving roughly 67.5% for current Lottomatica holders and 32.5% for CIRSA holders, with Blackstone expected near 24%. CIRSA’s shareholder vote is set for November 30; antitrust, FDI, EU foreign-subsidy, and gaming clearances remain, with completion eyed for Q2 2027.

### 13. Riot bans Team BONK coach and five players for 48 months (esports enforcement)
