### 2. More than 1,600 Rockstar UK staff vote on formal union recognition (labor / regulation)
**Summary:** During the GTA VI launch window, more than 1,600 Rockstar employees in the United Kingdom are voting through November 26 on whether the company should formally recognize the Rockstar Game Workers Union, part of the IWGB. ### 7. EU Digital Fairness Act draft targets loot boxes, pay-to-win, and child monetization (regulation)
**Summary:** The European Commission's Digital Fairness Act proposal, published in November, continues to drive industry analysis: it would tighten rules on showing real-money values for virtual currencies, disclosing and disabling paid variable-reward systems such as loot boxes, and restricting pay-to-win / pay-to-progress offers to children. The draft would replace the Unfair Commercial Practices Directive with a directly applicable regulation, but it still requires negotiation with the European Parliament and member states and would typically apply only after about a 12-month transition if adopted.

**Links:**

- [Pinsent Masons — DFA proposal and game monetisation](https://www.pinsentmasons.com/out-law/analysis/digital-fairness-act-proposal-puts-video-game-monetisation-models-in-focus)
- [The Hype Magazine — EU targets pay-to-win and influencer ads](https://www.thehypemagazine.com/2026/11/19/eu-digital-fairness-act-pay-to-win-influencer-ads/)

**Commentary:** Even if real enforcement is closer to 2028, product and compliance teams must treat child monetization design as a hard constraint, not a PR talking point.
