## II. Regulation, Litigation, and Labor

### 6. Denuvo Sues Anonymous Cracker voices38 Over Anti-Tamper Bypasses on at Least 26 Games (Lawsuit)
**Summary:** VGC reported on September 18 that Denuvo filed suit on Monday, September 14, in the U.S. District Court for the Northern District of California against "voices38, an unknown individual or entity," alleging bypasses of Denuvo Anti-Tamper on at least 26 games, including Resident Evil Requiem, Black Myth: Wukong, Hogwarts Legacy, and Doom: The Dark Ages. The company seeks damages under the DMCA anti-circumvention rules and an injunction covering "any past, present, or future" game using the technology. TorrentFreak noted that more cracks, including Persona 3 Reload and Star Wars Outlaws, appeared after the list was drawn up. voices38 replied on Reddit that everything would continue as normal.

**Links:**

- [VGC — Denuvo sues cracker over 26 games](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/denuvo-sues-game-hacker-for-releasing-cracks-for-26-games-that-remove-its-drm-protection/)
- [TorrentFreak — Denuvo sues voices38](https://torrentfreak.com/denuvo-sues-game-cracker-voices38-for-bypassing-its-anti-tamper-drm/)

**Commentary:** The DRM vendor is the plaintiff, and the requested injunction reaches "future" games—this is a case against a person and a bid to preset jurisdiction over the next crack.

### 7. Swedish Unions Widen the Strike Notice at King; Walkout Set for September 25 if Talks Fail (Labor)

### 8. India's MeitY Tells the Information Commission: No Game Registered, 201 Complaints (Regulation)

### 12. Riot Says Anti-Boost Has Penalized 296,416 League and VALORANT Accounts (Esports)
**Summary:** Dexerto reported on September 18 that Riot Games confirmed on September 17 that Vanguard's anti-boost system has penalized 296,416 accounts across League of Legends and VALORANT since late 2025. The system started on League last year and expanded to VALORANT this summer. Not every penalty is a permanent ban; outcomes can include rank rollbacks, stripped season rewards, and temporary suspensions, while purchased accounts and accounts without a legitimate owner can be removed for good. Riot said the system is not yet at full capacity.

**Links:**

- [Dexerto — Riot penalized 296,416 accounts](https://www.dexerto.com/gaming/riot-banned-nearly-300000-valorant-and-league-of-legends-players-for-rank-manipulation-3410177/)
