### 2. Samsung's fourth-quarter phone plan is down about a fifth as glass fiber shifts to AI (phone materials)
**Summary:** DigiTimes on October 19 carried a ZDNet Korea report from October 15 that Samsung Electronics has cut its fourth-quarter 2026 plan for mid-range and low-end smartphones because memory prices have jumped and glass fiber for the copper-clad laminate in printed circuit boards is tight. ZDNet Korea wrote that Nittobo is shifting capacity from E-glass used in phone boards to higher-margin T-glass for AI chips. An industry source said E-glass supply is no longer smooth and prices have risen, so Samsung reduced orders for the related parts. The monthly production plan in that report moved from 14.3 million units in October, 9.5 million in November, and 7.6 million in December, or 31.4 million in total, as given in mid-September, to 9.9 million, 7.6 million, and 6.7 million, or 24.2 million, as given in early October, a drop of 23 percent. Component-order outlooks were said to be 20 percent to 30 percent below the figures given before the Chuseok holiday. The full-year shipment figure communicated to suppliers was a little above 230 million units, below the 240 million the parts industry had expected. The report said flagship plans remain high and next year's business plan has not yet been shared with suppliers. The Mobile Experience and Networks division lost 735 billion won in the second quarter, and brokerages forecast a 400 billion won loss for the year. Samsung did not confirm the cut in these reports.

## II. Factory Capacity and Manufacturing Investment

### 3. Siemens uses a digital twin in Erlangen to shorten circuit-board ramp-up (factories)
**Summary:** Siemens said in a press release on October 19 that it had shown new AI applications with NVIDIA at its electronics factory in Erlangen, Germany. For the printed-circuit-board assemblies used in drives and frequency converters, virtual production preparation can cut engineering effort by up to 85 percent and time to market by up to 40 percent. Quality inspection used to wait several weeks for physical prototypes; photorealistic images from the digital twin now train the vision systems. A planning agent generates production scenarios against defined indicators and checks them on a digital twin of the whole plant. The release says this end-to-end agent planning can save up to 20 percent of cost. The plant employs about 1,000 people and already runs more than 100 AI algorithms across production, logistics, quality inspection, and maintenance. Siemens plans to invest 500 million euros in the site through 2030. Board member Cedrik Neike said in the release that Erlangen raises productivity by 7 percent a year. Reuters, carried the same day by CNA, quoted him at the plant saying manufacturers need annual productivity gains of 7 percent to 9 percent to stay competitive, and that Siemens wants AI to lift factory automation from 30 percent to 40 percent today to 70 percent to 80 percent.

**Links:**

- [Siemens — Siemens partners with NVIDIA to advance AI-driven, autonomous factories](https://press.siemens.com/global/en/pressrelease/siemens-partners-nvidia-advance-ai-driven-autonomous-factories)
- [CNA (Reuters) — Siemens targets up to 80% factory automation with AI](https://www.channelnewsasia.com/business/siemens-targets-up-80-factory-automation-ai-board-member-says-6472376)

**Commentary:** The 85 percent, 40 percent, and 20 percent figures are upper bounds in the release for circuit boards and planning scenarios at Erlangen. The 70 percent to 80 percent range is a board member's automation target, not a rate the whole plant has already reached.

### 4. China's third-quarter factory utilization is 73.1 percent, and autos hit a 10-quarter low (capacity)
**Summary:** The National Bureau of Statistics said on October 19 that industrial capacity utilization at firms above a designated size was 73.1 percent in the third quarter of 2026, up 0.1 percentage point from the second quarter and down 1.5 percentage points from a year earlier. Manufacturing was 73.3 percent. Auto manufacturing was 66.2 percent, down 7.1 percentage points year on year. Computer, communications, and other electronic equipment was 80.2 percent, up 1.2 percentage points. Utilization for the first three quarters was 73.2 percent, after 73.6 percent in the first quarter. Caixin's headline said auto manufacturing was the lowest in 10 quarters, and the visible lead said solar and battery-related industries have rebounded from their lows while autos and steel kept falling.

**Summary:** The Los Angeles Business Journal reported on October 19 that the ports of Los Angeles and Long Beach together handled more than 2 million TEU in September, and that each port set a monthly record. Los Angeles handled more than 1.04 million TEU, the highest month since the port opened and the third time it has passed 1 million.
