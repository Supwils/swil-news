### 2. TSMC, Memory and the Cost of the Chip Supercycle (Semiconductors)

**Summary:** IDC notes TSMC's Q3 2026 revenue rose 41.2% to $46.72 billion at a 66.4% gross margin, with Q4 guidance of $52.3–54.3 billion. IDC says memory costs are up about 350% year on year and expects increases to continue through at least 2028, while Q3 smartphone shipments fell 22%. SK hynix expects demand to exceed supply through 2030, with over half of its 2027–28 capacity already covered by long-term agreements.

**Links:**

- [IDC — Who pays for the chip supercycle](https://www.idc.com/ap/resource-center/semicon/2026-october-who-pays-for-the-chip-supercycle/)

**Commentary:** Supply, not demand, is the binding constraint, and the cost is being passed down to end devices.

### 3. China's Rare Earth Export Control Pause Reaches Its Expiry (Rare Earths)

We could not find confirming reports from after Nov 10, so the current status should be checked against MOFCOM announcements.

**Links:**

New orders edged up only to 55.5, and some commentary says the gain was partly driven by inventories and slower deliveries.

## III. Policy, Geopolitics & Logistics

### 6. USTR Extends Section 301 Exclusions and Maritime Fee Pause to Jan 9, 2027

**Summary:** Per Flexport's Nov 12 update, USTR extended certain 2018 China Section 301 exclusions (HTSUS 9903.88.69 and 9903.88.70) by 60 days to Jan 9, 2027, and the pause on fees for vessels tied to Chinese shipbuilding and on added duties on Chinese cranes also runs to that date. The US-China trade truce is scheduled to end Jan 10. The decision deadline in the Vietnam IP case moved from Nov 29 to Feb 28, 2027, with tariffs still a possible outcome.
