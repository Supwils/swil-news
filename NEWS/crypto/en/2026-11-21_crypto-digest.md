## III. DeFi & Protocols

### 4. DeFi: Aave Executes Avalanche V4 Onboarding of Anchorage-Issued USAT (Borrow-Only)

**Summary:** Aave proposal #543 — “Onboard USAT to Aave V4 Core Instance on Avalanche” — executed on Nov 21 with 100% support. USAT is a dollar stablecoin issued by Anchorage Digital Bank under the U.S. federal stablecoin framework. The approved configuration makes USAT a borrow-only reserve (not usable as collateral), with an add cap of about 60 million USAT and a draw cap of about 55 million, contingent on seeding roughly $5 million of DEX liquidity on Avalanche. Forum risk notes still flag bridge and minting-privilege risks.

**Links:**

- [Aave Governance — Onboard USAT to Aave V4 Core Instance on Avalanche](https://governance.aave.com/t/direct-to-aip-onboard-usat-to-aave-v4-core-instance-on-avalanche/25880)
- [Kvorum — Aave proposal #543 executed](https://app.kvorum.watch/daos/aave/proposals)

**Commentary:** Bringing a bank-issued stablecoin into a major lending market is progress, but “borrow-only” shows the DAO is still hedging issuance and bridge risk with parameters.

## IV. Security Incidents

### 6. Security: Cross-Chain DEX Chainflip Sees ~$4.86M Ethereum Vault Drain; FLIP Falls ~30%–34%

**Summary:** Around 2:10 a.m. ET on Nov 21, an attacker apparently used a valid aggregate validator signing key to drain Chainflip's Ethereum vault of about 520.2 ETH, 3.25 million USDC and 758,000 USDT — roughly $4.86 million — with most proceeds routed through Tornado Cash within about 10 minutes. The protocol entered full safe mode, disabling deposits, withdrawals, swaps and quoting; FLIP fell about 30%–34%, and market cap dropped from about $41.6 million to about $27.1 million. Security firms pointed to key/signing compromise rather than a smart-contract bug; Bitcoin and Solana vaults were not reported drained in the same way.
