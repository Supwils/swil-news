### 3. FBI Confirms North Korea’s TraderTraitor Behind Binance Theft; Urges DeFi and Swaps to Block Addresses (Security)
**Summary:** In statements circulating from Oct. 31 into Nov. 1, the U.S. FBI confirmed that the theft of more than $6 billion from Binance on or about Oct. 29 was North Korea–linked “TraderTraitor” activity—the same actors tied to last year’s ~$1.5 billion Bybit hack. The FBI said it has observed the actors converting stolen assets primarily into ether and expects further laundering via DeFi and instant-swap services; together with the Secret Service and IRS-CI, it urged THORNode operators, RPC nodes, instant exchanges, DeFi services, analytics firms, and other VASPs to block listed addresses. The notice listed over 600 Ethereum addresses, just under 20 Bitcoin addresses, and one Solana address; Chainalysis warned the footprint could expand to “tens of thousands” of addresses in coming weeks.

## III. Regulation & Policy

### 5. Canada’s OSFI Crypto Capital and Liquidity Guideline Takes Effect Today for Oct. 31 Year-End Banks (Regulation)
**Summary:** OSFI’s Capital and Liquidity Treatment of Crypto-asset Exposures (Banking) Guideline (2027) took effect Nov. 1, 2026 for institutions with a fiscal year ending Oct. 31—including Canada’s Big Six banks—while firms with a Dec. 31 year-end apply from Jan. 1, 2027. The final text keeps the 5% of Tier 1 net capital Group 2 exposure limit and punitive treatment if breached, while delivering two easings: matched long/short positions in the same crypto-asset with the same maturity on regulated exchanges can fully offset in market-risk capital; qualifying client-clearing derivative exposures through a QCCP are excluded from the Group 2 limit. OSFI still refuses to treat Group 2a crypto-assets as eligible collateral, so crypto-backed loans do not get hedge relief.

**Links:**

- [OSFI — Crypto-asset exposures capital and liquidity guideline (2027)](https://www.osfi-bsif.gc.ca/en/guidance/guidance-library/capital-liquidity-treatment-crypto-asset-exposures-banking-guideline-2027)
- [OSFI — Implementation letter: Nov. 1 / Jan. 1 phased effective dates](https://www.osfi-bsif.gc.ca/en/guidance/guidance-library/capital-liquidity-treatment-crypto-asset-exposures-banking-guideline-2027-letter)

**Commentary:** “Limits stay, hedge and clearing math ease a notch” slightly widens room for bank market-making and client clearing, but collateral and HQLA red lines are untouched—bank entry remains cautious.

### 6. USDC About 11 Days From EBA Dual Supervision as the EU’s First Significant EMT (Regulation)
**Summary:** The European Banking Authority has classified USDC, issued in the EU by Circle Internet Financial Europe SAS, as the first “significant” e-money token under MiCA among 45 notified EMTs at the time. From Nov. 12, 2026, direct supervision of own funds, reserve assets, liquidity requirements, and recovery and redemption plans transfers from France’s ACPR to the EBA; ACPR retains governance, qualifying holdings, fit-and-proper assessments, outsourcing, and conflicts of interest. The EBA also signed an MoU with the New York Department of Financial Services on cross-border stablecoin oversight. On Nov. 1, USDC still traded near one dollar; attention centers on the supervisory handoff rather than any product redesign.

**Links:**
