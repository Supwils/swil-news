## III. Regulation & Policy

### 4. Regulation: Hong Kong Gazettes Bill Expanding Licenses to Crypto Dealing, Advisory, Management and Custody

**Summary:** On Nov 20 the Hong Kong government gazetted the Virtual Asset Services Legislation (Amendment) Bill 2026, proposing amendments to the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorist Financing Ordinance so that businesses providing virtual-asset dealing, advisory, management or custody services in Hong Kong must be licensed by or registered with the SFC and meet fit-and-proper and related requirements. HKMA-supervised banks and stored-value facility licensees would also need SFC registration to offer VA services. The bill is scheduled for first reading in the Legislative Council on Dec 2. Weekend coverage also highlighted proposed penalties for falsely claiming a license application (up to two years' imprisonment and a HK$400,000 fine on indictment, subject to legislative changes).

### 5. Regulation: Bank of Russia Rules Take Effect — Exchanges May Start Crypto Trading From Nov 22 Once Rules Are Approved

**Summary:** A Bank of Russia ordinance effective Nov 22 authorizes exchanges to begin organized cryptocurrency trading once the regulator concurs with their trading rules. Venues must register participants such as brokers, asset managers and central counterparties, keep order and trade registers, monitor activity, and calculate current, volume-weighted average, market and closing prices for listed coins while reporting to the central bank. SPB Exchange has already been test-trading bitcoin, ether and USDT and has submitted rules; Moscow Exchange plans a Dec 1 pilot in the same three assets. Separately, Finance Ministry officials said second-reading amendments would soften limits on using digital currency for certain payments, allow miners to hedge, and make some foreign-wallet declarations voluntary.

### 6. Regulation: SEC Crypto Custody Comment Deadline Still Listed as Dec 7; Trade Groups' 45-Day Extension Request Unanswered

**Summary:** The SEC proposed crypto custody rules for advisers and regulated funds on Oct 1, 2026 (File No. S7-2026-35; Federal Register publication Oct 6). In a Nov 4 letter, AIMA, the ABA, SIFMA and other groups asked for 45 more calendar days, citing more than 300 specific comment requests and overlapping investment-management proposals. As of Nov 22, the SEC rule page still listed a Dec 7, 2026 deadline and no Federal Register extension notice was found. Key elements include conditional self-custody when no permitted custodian is available and a pathway to use qualifying state trust companies as custodians.

### 8. Stablecoins: DBS and Circle Expand Direct USDC Minting and Redemption for Asian Institutions

**Summary:** On Nov 18 in Singapore, DBS and Circle announced that eligible institutional clients can mint and redeem USDC directly through DBS, with near-instant 24/7 USD–USDC settlement intended to reduce reliance on exchange secondary-market liquidity and spreads.
