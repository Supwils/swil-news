## I. Regulation and Policy

### 1. EBA classifies USDC as the EU's first significant e-money token, with dual supervision from November 12 (Regulation)
**Summary:** On October 14, the European Banking Authority classified USDC, issued by Circle Internet Financial Europe SAS, as a significant e-money token under the Markets in Crypto-Assets Regulation. It is the first of 45 notified e-money tokens in the European Union to receive that label. From November 12, 2026, direct supervision of own funds, the reserve of assets, liquidity requirements, and recovery and redemption plans moves from France's ACPR to the EBA. The ACPR keeps governance, qualifying holdings, fit-and-proper assessments, outsourcing, and conflicts of interest. The EBA did not say which of the seven significance criteria USDC met. Finance Magnates, citing Circle's quarterly results, put worldwide circulation at $73.3 billion at the end of June, up 19% from a year earlier. The decision covers USDC only, not euro token EURC.

**Links:**

- [EBA press release mirror — USDC classified as the EU's first significant e-money token](https://www.pubt.io/view/1401DE5A02E6D2D9F49FA77E6A54EA0AB38EF804)
- [Finance Magnates — EU banking regulator takes over key oversight of Circle's USDC](https://www.financemagnates.com/cryptocurrency/eu-banking-regulator-takes-over-key-oversight-of-circles-usdc-stablecoin/)

**Commentary:** From November 12, the reserves behind USDC listed in the EU sit with an EU-level supervisor, while the French license still covers the company itself.

### 2. French finance committee rejects the budget's revenue section, so stablecoin-swap tax amendments must be refiled (Regulation)
**Summary:** CoinDesk reported on October 14 that an amendment adopted by the National Assembly's finance committee on October 7 would, from January 1, 2027, tax swaps from crypto such as bitcoin into MiCA electronic-money tokens at France's 31.4% flat crypto capital-gains rate. Crypto-to-crypto swaps are generally not taxable today; tax applies when holders cash out or spend. Chainalysis France chief executive Francois Volpoet said the amendment leaves USDT, DAI, and PAXG outside its scope. A separate amendment backed on October 9 would require disclosure of self-hosted wallets holding at least 100,000 euros (about $116,000), with fines up to about $11,600, and would extend the exit tax to crypto holdings worth roughly $900,000. The committee then rejected the revenue section of the 2027 budget by 31 votes to 3, so the amendments must be submitted again. A vote on the revenue section is scheduled for October 20, and the budget must pass by December 10. None of the measures is law.

**Links:**

- [CoinDesk — Proposed French tax on regulated stablecoins could drive traders offshore](https://www.coindesk.com/policy/2026/10/14/proposed-french-tax-on-regulated-stablecoins-could-drive-crypto-traders-offshore)

**Commentary:** The rate would hit the MiCA stablecoin swaps authorities can see, and the rejected revenue section means those clauses have to re-enter the full Assembly before they can take effect.

### 3. Dutch central bank grants an e-money license to 37-bank consortium Qivalis (Regulation)
**Summary:** CoinDesk reported on October 14 that Amsterdam-based Qivalis said the Dutch central bank had granted it an electronic-money institution license, moving it closer to issuing a euro e-money token. The company must still meet remaining MiCA requirements, including publication of a white paper, and plans to distribute the token in the coming months through banks, crypto exchanges, and payment providers rather than directly to consumers. The consortium has grown to 37 European banks since it was founded in September 2025, including BBVA, BNP Paribas, ING, and DZ Bank, and has selected Fireblocks for digital-asset infrastructure. Under MiCA, e-money tokens must be redeemable at par at any time and cannot pay interest. Qivalis has not said which members will use the token or how much they expect to settle.

**Links:**

- [CoinDesk — Dutch central bank grants Qivalis an e-money license under MiCA](https://www.coindesk.com/policy/2026/10/14/dutch-regulator-approves-qivalis-as-electronic-money-institution-under-mica-rules)

**Commentary:** The license covers the right to issue; the white paper and member-bank rollout are still open, so the euro token is not yet a live settlement asset.

### 10. Standard Chartered pushes its $2 trillion stablecoin-supply forecast from 2028 to 2030 (Stablecoins)
**Summary:** The Block on October 14 cited a Wednesday report from Geoffrey Kendrick, Standard Chartered's global head of digital assets research. The bank kept a $2 trillion stablecoin-market estimate but now expects that level in 2030 rather than 2028. Supply has stayed between $300 billion and $320 billion over the past 12 months and stands at about $309 billion. Kendrick called the slowdown cyclical because crypto-market activity has been weak. Usage is still rising: raw onchain transfer value hit a monthly record of $19 trillion in May, and monthly volume has averaged just under $10 trillion so far this year, up from $4 trillion in 2025. Average monthly stablecoin velocity has doubled to about 30 times from about 15 times in 2025. USDT's monthly velocity has stayed near seven times, while USDC's rose to about 80 times from 45 times in 2025. He said the GENIUS Act is due to take effect in January 2027, and whether other countries follow it is a main risk to the forecast.

## IV. DeFi, Protocols and Security

### 12. StarkWare opens OffMarket so Polymarket orders are not publicly tied to a main account (DeFi)
**Summary:** The Block reported on October 14 that StarkWare opened OffMarket to the public after a limited release in August. Users deposit USDC into an open-source privacy pool on Starknet, then trade on Polymarket's existing order book at the same prices through a fresh Polygon address derived from their signature. Deposits are capped at $1,000 USDC per account. The service is non-custodial, with keys kept in the browser. A StarkWare spokesperson said trade size and timing can still identify a trader, and that markets, prices, and executions stay public. L2BEAT figures cited by StarkWare put the shared privacy pool at about $1.5 million across 17,000 deposits. OpenZeppelin's June audit reported no critical or high-severity findings. OffMarket is not a Polymarket product, has no formal partnership or special access, sits in Polymarket's verified builder program, and applies Polymarket's geographic limits, including the ban on U.S. users. Private orders take about a minute, and withdrawals take a few minutes.

**Links:**

- [The Block — StarkWare launches OffMarket for more private Polymarket trading](https://www.theblock.co/news/ecosystems/2026-10-14-starkware-launches-offmarket-to-let-polymarket-users-trade-more-privately-418567)

**Commentary:** What opened is a privacy front end with a $1,000 cap that does not hide the trade itself, and Polymarket can still disable the builder code.

### 13. Uniswap turns on a VolatilePair Hook on Ethereum mainnet, with separate buy and sell fees for ETH/USDC (DeFi)
**Summary:** CryptoBriefing reported on October 14 that Uniswap switched on its VolatilePair Hook on Ethereum mainnet, starting with an ETH/USDC pool. The hook replaces a single fee with two dynamic fees, one for buys and one for sells, and recalculates both on every swap from recent price moves. Trades that correct a price gap after a large move pay a temporary surcharge, which Uniswap describes as protection for liquidity providers against arbitrage losses under a static fee. Trading in volatile pairs on Uniswap has topped $500 billion so far in 2026. The StablePair Hook, introduced in September for pairs such as USDC/USDT, reached $3.1 billion of volume in its first four weeks. Hook parameters and fee logic can be recalibrated through Uniswap governance. The report did not give the new pool's starting liquidity.

**Links:**

- [CryptoBriefing — Uniswap launches an ETH/USDC pool with separate buy and sell fees](https://cryptobriefing.com/uniswap-volatilepair-hook-eth-usdc-pool/)

**Commentary:** The fee is now a function of direction and recent price action, and there is no figure yet for how much liquidity leaves the old pools.
