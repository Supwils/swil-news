### 4. CFTC previews a retail crypto trading framework, complementing the SEC's Regulation Crypto Assets

**Summary:** The CFTC's October 13 ANPRM contemplates a federal framework for leveraged, margined or financed retail crypto transactions (CTXs), through Regulation CTX and Regulation CAM, including a new "crypto asset market" DCM subcategory; comments are due December 14. It contains no rule text and does not claim broad authority over spot markets. It is presented as complementary to the SEC's August 2026 proposed Regulation Crypto Assets.

**Links:**

- [Mayer Brown — CFTC previews a federal framework for some retail crypto asset transactions](https://www.mayerbrown.com/en/insights/publications/2026/11/cftc-previews-a-federal-framework-for-some-retail-crypto-asset-transactions)

**Commentary:** With Congress stalled, the two agencies are laying groundwork through rulemaking, but without new legislation the spot-market gap remains.

### 5. Russia opens retail Bitcoin, Ether and USDT trading on November 17

**Summary:** The central bank finalized rules letting licensed exchanges and brokers sell BTC, ETH and USDT to retail investors. Non-qualified investors may buy up to 300,000 rubles (about $3,700) a year per intermediary after passing a test; qualified investors have no cap. Moscow Exchange plans to start crypto trading on December 1, peer-to-peer trading will be banned from July 1, 2027, and transfers to self-custody and crypto payments for goods remain prohibited.

**Links:**

- [Cointelegraph — Russia finalizes crypto trading rules for November rollout](https://cointelegraph.com/news/russia-finalizes-crypto-trading-rules-november-rollout)

**Commentary:** Russia is taking a controlled-gateway route: open purchases, closed payments and self-custody, to keep crypto inside a monitored financial system.

## IV. Security

### 9. Binance hit by $6.3B hack: FBI blames North Korea's TraderTraitor, the largest crypto theft on record

**Summary:** About $6.3–6.36 billion was stolen across ten blockchains in roughly 20 minutes on October 29; the FBI attributed it to North Korea's TraderTraitor (Lazarus) on October 31, matching TRM Labs and Chainalysis. Withdrawals resumed in phases from November 1, with $3.64 billion of net outflows in the first 9.5 hours (about 2.1% of its October 28 proof of reserves).
