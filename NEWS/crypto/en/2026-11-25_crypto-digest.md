## II. Regulation & Policy

### 3. Regulation: Ukraine's Parliament Passes Virtual-Asset Regulation and Tax Law — About 23% Combined Rate, July 2027 Start

**Summary:** On Nov 25, Ukraine's Verkhovna Rada adopted on second reading and as a whole draft law No. 10225-d amending the Tax Code and other acts on virtual-asset regulation, with about 271 votes. Individuals would generally owe 18% personal income tax plus a 5% military levy — about 23% combined — on annual net crypto profits; crypto-to-crypto swaps would be exempt and losses carry-forwardable. Assets bought before the rules take effect could qualify for a lower combined ~10% rate if sold for fiat by end-2028. Core provisions are set for July 1, 2027, pending presidential signature. Oversight splits between the securities commission and the National Bank, which would supervise single-currency stablecoin issuers.

### 4. Regulation: Sens. Warren and Van Hollen Urge SEC to Scrap Regulation Crypto Assets and the Innovation Exemption

**Summary:** Disclosed Nov 25, Democratic Sens. Elizabeth Warren, Chris Van Hollen and allies sent Tuesday letters to SEC Chair Paul Atkins seeking withdrawal of proposed Regulation Crypto Assets — which would create startup exemptions up to about $5 million and fundraising exemptions up to about $75 million — and the tokenized-securities “innovation exemption.” They argued the offering regime would give crypto preferential, lighter treatment versus traditional securities, weaken state authority and harm investors, while the innovation exemption could enable a shadow stock market with weaker fair-pricing and transparency safeguards and fragment liquidity. The SEC did not immediately comment.

### 5. Regulation: French Senate Rejects Crypto Exit Tax, Stablecoin-Swap Tax and Large Self-Custody Wallet Reporting

**Summary:** According to the Senate record and Nov 25 reporting, France's Senate rejected three crypto amendments while debating the revenue title of the 2027 budget: extending the exit tax to crypto above an €800,000 threshold, taxing conversions into stablecoins treated as e-money tokens, and requiring declaration of self-custodied wallets worth more than €100,000. The finance committee and government opposed all three; Public Accounts Minister David Amiel said stablecoins are neither legal tender nor a means to settle debts. Existing deferral of tax on crypto-to-crypto trades, including stablecoin swaps, remains in place for now.

### 6. Regulation: EBA Report Urges Commission to Weigh Extra Rules for CASPs Funneling Clients into DeFi Lending

**Summary:** On Nov 25, the European Banking Authority published a report on stablecoins — including MiCA e-money tokens (EMTs) — in DeFi and reiterated its MiCA-review recommendation that the European Commission assess additional requirements for crypto-asset service providers that facilitate access to DeFi, especially lending protocols. The EBA flagged yield via DeFi as a bypass of MiCA's ban on interest on EMTs, plus regulatory-arbitrage risks around unauthorized stablecoins. Suggested tools include suitability tests, richer risk disclosures, leverage limits, a ban on routing clients to unauthorized stablecoins, and a certification regime for DeFi lending protocols. EU consumer uptake remains limited, but CASPs and AI apps are lowering entry barriers.

**Links:**

### 9. DeFi: Aave Labs Publicly Launches Savings App After Early Balances Top About $63 Million

**Summary:** On Nov 25, Aave Labs opened the Aave App to the public on iOS and Android. Users can open self-custodial accounts with email or phone — no seed phrase or self-paid gas. Bank funding is available in the U.S. (ex-New York), the UK and the EEA via Bridge, Astra and Push; elsewhere users can deposit USDC, USDT or GHO from eight networks including Ethereum, Arbitrum and Base. Verified users can earn a variable ~6% base rate for 90 days on up to $100,000, liftable to about 7.25% via referrals and recurring deposits. Funds are allocated to Aave Protocol overcollateralized lending markets; a discretionary Balance Protection program is not FDIC/SIPC insurance. Early access drew more than about $63 million in balances in under three months.

### 11. Infrastructure: Ethereum Foundation Recommends Keccak-256 as the Post-Quantum Hash; Internal Target Around 2029

**Summary:** In a Nov 24 Ethereum Foundation blog post widely covered on Nov 25, researchers recommended Keccak-256 (and Keccak-f[1600]-derived primitives) as the hash for Ethereum's planned move to hash-based post-quantum cryptography. Reasons include nearly two decades of cryptanalysis, consistency with existing addresses and execution-layer state, and zkVMs now proving standard hashes efficiently enough. The Foundation keeps an internal ~2029 deadline for quantum-resistant upgrades, with a timetable review in January 2027; the recommendation is not yet an adopted network upgrade.
