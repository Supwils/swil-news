## I. Regulation & Policy

### 1. SEC crypto broker-dealer financial-responsibility draft enters White House review (Regulation)
**Summary:** The U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission's proposed rule titled “Amendments to Broker-Dealer Financial Responsibility and Recordkeeping and Reporting Rules Regarding Crypto Assets” (RIN 3235-AN48) was filed with the White House Office of Information and Regulatory Affairs on November 3 and remained pending review as of November 8. The Unified Agenda abstract points to amendments of net-capital Rule 15c3-1, customer-protection Rule 15c3-3, and recordkeeping Rules 17a-3 and 17a-4 for crypto-asset activity; the draft text is not public and the item is labeled economically significant. OIRA review is not approval or effectiveness—Commission proposal, public comment, and a final rule would still follow. NullTX and CoinEditorial covered the filing on November 8.

**Links:**

- [NullTX — SEC Targets Net Capital, Customer Protection and Recordkeeping Rules for Crypto Brokers](https://nulltx.com/sec-targets-net-capital-customer-protection-and-recordkeeping-rules-for-crypto-brokers)
- [OIRA / Reginfo — RIN 3235-AN48 agenda entry](https://www.reginfo.gov/public/do/eAgendaViewRule?RIN=3235-AN48&pubId=202510)

**Commentary:** Moving from staff FAQs to enforceable rules will reshape custody and capital costs for crypto-active brokers, but the direction stays opaque until the text is published.

### 3. Binance to close some EU accounts after MiCA transition, with balance fees from Nov. 29 (Regulation)
**Summary:** CoinDesk and Cointelegraph report that after the EU Markets in Crypto-Assets (MiCA) transition period ended, Binance is progressively restricting services for affected users; a spokesperson confirmed affected accounts will close effective December 31, 2026. Users in France, Spain, Italy, and Poland said they received emails: deposits blocked, withdrawals still open; from November 29 a monthly fee of 0.025% of remaining crypto or 10 USDC (whichever is higher) applies, with another charge after December 24; leftover balances after year-end may be treated as inactive and converted or transferred. France's AMF shows Binance France's registration lapsed automatically on July 2; Binance is still absent from ESMA's authorized crypto-provider register and says it continues to pursue licensing.

## IV. Security Incidents

### 7. Web3Auth says it frontran attackers by moving about $5.6M from exposed Keplr wallets (Security)
**Summary:** The Block reported on November 8 that Consensys-owned Web3Auth said a flaw in its systems exposed private keys for some Keplr wallets created with Google or Apple login, and that it used the same path as the original attacker to move remaining funds into 12 recovery addresses holding about $5.6 million onchain—including roughly 2.49 million ATOM (~$3.3 million) on Cosmos Hub. Keplr's co-founder estimated about 20% of social-login accounts were affected; seed-phrase and hardware-wallet users were not, and Google/Apple logins were paused. Bitquery had tracked roughly $6.01 million in transfers across more than about 44,000 wallets; Web3Auth said some flows were mistaken for theft and that Bitquery's collector addresses sit on its recovery list. Irrecoverable losses are not yet finalized; the firms are working on a return path.

### 9. Ethereum Foundation: Glamsterdam Hoodi hard fork delayed until eight contract risks are resolved (Infrastructure)
**Summary:** In a November 6 post, the Ethereum Foundation said auditor ChainSecurity found projects that rely on gas/state-accounting invariants broken by EIP-8037 and EIP-8038 under Glamsterdam; All Core Devs decided not to hard-fork Hoodi until the issues are resolved, with no new activation date. The Foundation called on contract developers and auditors to check eight listed invariants while internal audits continue; work on the subsequent Hegota upgrade proceeds in parallel. The delay extends the late-October decision canceling the prior ~October 26 Hoodi activation; mainnet timing remains unset. Cointelegraph and crypto.news continued weekend coverage.

**Links:**
