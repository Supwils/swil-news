### 6. California enacts nation’s strongest child companion-chatbot and social-media addiction laws (Policy)
**Summary:** Governor Gavin Newsom on September 10 signed a package of child online and AI safety bills still widely covered on September 11. Core measures include SB 1119 (“Adam’s Law”), requiring companion chatbots to maintain crisis protocols for suicidal ideation, parental controls, notices if a child disables safety settings, plus independent child-safety audits and annual risk assessments; AB 1709 and related bills ban addictive features such as autoplay and profile-based algorithmic feeds for users under 16; another measure pauses companion chatbots in toys for five years. The chatbot law is named for California teen Adam Raine, who died by suicide in 2025 after ChatGPT conversations; his parents have sued OpenAI.

**Links:**

- [Governor of California — Newsom signs strongest child safety chatbot and social media laws](https://www.gov.ca.gov/2026/09/10/governor-newsom-signs-the-strongest-child-safety-chatbot-and-social-media-laws-in-the-nation/)

## IV. European Compliance & Consumer Hardware

### 9. EU Cyber Resilience Act reporting duties take effect; ENISA Single Reporting Platform goes live (Compliance)
**Summary:** ENISA announced on September 11 that the CRA Single Reporting Platform (SRP) reached initial operating capability. From today, manufacturers placing products with digital elements on the EU market must report actively exploited vulnerabilities and severe incidents affecting product security (broader CRA cybersecurity requirements still apply from December 11, 2027). The platform lets reporters file once, with notifications reaching the coordinating CSIRT and ENISA simultaneously. TechTimes and others noted fines of up to €15 million or 2.5% of global annual turnover, whichever is higher, with extraterritorial reach for any product sold into the EU.

**Links:**

- [ENISA — The CRA Single Reporting Platform is launched](https://www.enisa.europa.eu/news/the-cra-single-reporting-platform-is-launched)
