## II. Policy, Regulation, and Geopolitics

### 3. House report urges removal of PRC open-weight models from federal systems; Cursor weighs a phase-out (Policy)

**Summary:** On November 20, the House Homeland Security Committee and the Select Committee on China released a joint investigative report on U.S. adoption of PRC open-weight models, citing engagement with Airbnb, Cursor (Anysphere), and DoorDash. Cursor’s Composer 2 was built on Moonshot AI’s Kimi K2.5. According to CNBC, Cursor told the committees it would explore phasing out PRC-developed open-weight models because of geopolitical risk. The report recommends a national U.S. open-weight initiative and broadening restrictions on PRC-developed models in federal systems and federal contracting.

### 4. TSMC tightens advanced-node foundry access for Chinese designers after new U.S. rules (Chips)

**Summary:** Reuters, citing people familiar with the matter, reported that after new U.S. Commerce Department rules took effect on November 19, TSMC is tightening advanced manufacturing for affected Chinese chip designers: orders at 16nm and below are generally halted without a U.S. export license, and customers are typically limited to older 28nm/40nm processes without HBM or advanced packaging. The rules remove a prior technical-verification path that sometimes avoided individual licenses. TSMC said it will comply with applicable BIS regulations and does not comment on market rumors.

**Links:**

- [Investing.com / Reuters — TSMC tightens China AI chip curbs](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/tsmc-tightens-china-ai-chip-curbs-as-new-us-rules-close-manufacturing-loophole-sources-say-5038806)
- [CNA — TSMC pauses China orders at 16nm and below, Reuters reports](https://www.cna.com.tw/news/afe/202611200021.aspx)

**Commentary:** Closing the foundry verification loophole further narrows China’s external path to high-performance AI silicon and raises the premium on domestic accelerators and heterogeneous scheduling.

### 7. Hong Kong convenes first AI risk-governance coordination working group (Regional governance)

**Summary:** On November 20, Chief Secretary Chan Kwok-ki chaired the first meeting of Hong Kong’s Working Group on Coordination of AI Risk Governance. The group is coordinating seven action tracks, including fighting AI-enabled crime, protecting minors, promoting ethical market assessment, ensuring orderly industry adoption, examining liability for automated-product accidents, introducing agent-management guidelines, and evaluating automation’s employment impact. It will meet regularly to review cross-bureau delivery.

**Links:**

## III. Models and Products

### 8. OpenAI launches MedChemBench: 99 tasks testing medicinal-chemistry reasoning (Science AI)

**Summary:** On November 20 OpenAI introduced MedChemBench, drawn from 117 papers and patents and 761 molecular structures across 99 problems that score molecular-structure reconstruction, scientific reasoning, compound ranking/selection, and property prediction. At the xhigh reasoning setting, GPT-6.2 Astra earned about 84.5% of available credit, ahead of GPT-6 Astra (83.3%) and GPT-6.1 Sol (82.0%). OpenAI stresses the benchmark does not show that models can discover new drugs or improve real lab decisions, and prior exposure to published sources cannot be ruled out.

**Links:**

- [OpenAI — Introducing MedChemBench](https://openai.com/index/introducing-medchembench/)

**Commentary:** Frontier competition is migrating from general leaderboards into vertical scientific workflows—higher scores still stop short of clinical value without an experimental loop.

### 9. OpenAI hardens defenses against adversarial distillation on GPT-6.2 (Safety)

**Summary:** In a same-day post, OpenAI described adversarial distillation: unauthorized use of frontier-model outputs to train a similar model that can then be deployed without comparable safeguards. The company says it strengthened defenses in GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol; in an internal adaptive evaluation of protected-reasoning extraction, average attack success fell from about 54.0% against GPT-6 Astra with original safeguards to about 0.2% against GPT-6.2 Astra with updated safeguards.

**Links:**

- [OpenAI — Combatting adversarial distillation](https://openai.com/index/combatting-adversarial-distillation/)

**Commentary:** As open weights and distillation become industry defaults, closed-model moats increasingly depend on anti-extraction engineering rather than parameter count alone.

## IV. China’s Industry and Scientific AI

### 11. Alibaba’s Wu Yongming: 2027–2028 will be China’s tightest compute years; T-Head to lead training supply (Industry)

**Summary:** On Alibaba’s November 19 earnings call, CEO Wu Yongming said AI remains early and that models and applications must co-evolve; apps sharing the same underlying model risk high substitutability. Flagship chips will prioritize Alibaba’s own model training and core partners.
