### 2. Meta expands AI age estimation globally to place more teens in Teen Accounts (Safety · Product)

**Summary:** Meta said it is expanding Instagram’s AI age-estimation technology worldwide to identify likely teen users—even those who registered with adult birth dates—and move them into Teen Account protections: private by default, limits on stranger DMs and content types, and constraints on time spent. Facebook continues rolling out the approach, with Threads planned later this year. Meta says “millions more” teens will receive age-appropriate protections, building on commitments with U.S. attorneys general; in Australia it is also using Apple’s age-range signal. Al Jazeera, The Verge, and others note Meta has not published accuracy metrics or independent audits, leaving privacy and false-positive concerns open.

### 4. Google publicly ships Gemini 4 Carbon and previews Antigravity “swarm” multi-agents (Product)

**Summary:** Google DeepMind said on November 10 that Gemini 4 Carbon is available to developers, enterprises, scientists, and consumers. Google calls it its strongest model yet for cybersecurity, long-horizon agentic workflows, complex software engineering, and scientific reasoning. Paying users get API access via Google AI Studio and Gemini for business at the same introductory price as Gemini 4 Argon—about $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens—with rollout to Google AI Ultra subscribers in Antigravity, the Gemini app, and AI Mode in Search. The Verge notes Carbon first went to cybersecurity defenders about two weeks earlier; Google is also previewing a “swarm” feature on Antigravity that turns one prompt into a coordinated agent team. Argon may remain the default for most software and knowledge work, with Carbon aimed at tasks that stress the intelligence frontier.

## III. Security, Open Source, and Infrastructure

### 8. Fireworks AI discloses a second October breach with wider billing and unfinished-weight exposure (Security)

**Summary:** Fireworks AI updated its October 2026 incident bulletin, and media amplified the findings on November 10. After an earlier early-October credential abuse case, a third-party cloud host of Fireworks R&D machines was compromised on October 23; attackers reached development systems on October 24 and ran an unauthorized workload that expanded customer impact. Affected categories include resource metadata, billing and payment history (not card numbers, which Fireworks says it does not store), and, for some customers, incomplete model weights and datasets. Fireworks says Inference, Training SDK, and Nexus—zero data retention by default—showed no detected impact; it rotated credentials, isolated networks, and is working with Palo Alto Networks Unit 42. Customers such as Notion also posted impact notices.

**Links:**

- [Fireworks AI — Update on October 2026 security incident](https://fireworks.ai/security/bulletins/october-2026-security-incident-update)
- [The Register — Fireworks AI gets burned twice](https://www.theregister.com/cyber-crime/2026/11/10/fireworks-ai-gets-burned-twice-as-customer-data-exposure-widens/5308722)

### 9. NVIDIA donates OpenShell to CNCF and opens AI-factory Kubernetes building blocks (Open Source · Infrastructure)

NVIDIA says hundreds of organizations already use OpenShell; CNCF separately welcomed the project.

### 11. Ant Lingbo open-sources LingBot-Touch, a unified tactile foundation model for fine manipulation (Open Source · China)

**Summary:** Shanghai Ant Lingbo Technology open-sourced LingBot-Touch on November 10. Pretrained on nearly 5,000 hours of real tactile data spanning magnetic, visuotactile, and piezoresistive sensors, it maps diverse signals into a shared tactile token space for reuse across dexterous hands and action models. In 26 real-robot tests on Sharpa Wave and XHand 1, four action models gained about 14–26 percentage points in average success versus no-touch baselines; a bottle-cap task rose from about 56% to about 90% with LingBot-Touch. The company ships Tiny (~5.3M parameters) and Small variants, with report, models, and code on Hugging Face, GitHub, and ModelScope.

**Links:**

- [China Securities Journal — Ant Lingbo open-sources LingBot-Touch](https://www.cs.com.cn/ssgs/01/2026/11/10/detail_2026111010051207.html)

**Commentary:** Embodied AI still needs more than bigger vision-language models—unified touch representations are the scarce input for fine closed-loop manipulation.
