### 7. Google Nature paper: smartwatch AI flags hypertension signs without cuff calibration (Research · Health)

**Summary:** A November 5 Nature study from Google Research describes an AI system that uses consumer smartwatch PPG and accelerometer waveforms to detect signs of hypertension without cuff calibration. In a prospective multicenter study of 196 adults benchmarked against 24-hour ambulatory blood-pressure monitoring, seven-day real-world use yielded about 65.8% sensitivity and 90.0% specificity, versus about 55.3% sensitivity for initial office screening. Authors stress opportunistic screening only—not a replacement for clinical cuff diagnosis. Google’s research blog says the work informed blood-pressure trend features in the Google Health app while labeling the methods early feasibility research.

**Links:**

- [Nature — Opportunistically Detecting Signs of Hypertension on a Consumer Smartwatch](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-11336-3)
- [Google Research — Detecting signs of hypertension using wearable sensors](https://research.google/blog/detecting-signs-of-hypertension-using-wearable-sensors/)

**Commentary:** Wearable AI is shifting from fitness scores to passive chronic-disease sentinels—false positives and regulation will decide whether it screens or scares.
