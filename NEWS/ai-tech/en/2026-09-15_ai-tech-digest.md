## II. Enterprise Products, Data & Voice AI

### 4. Salesforce and NVIDIA launch CRM reasoning model Koa: Nemotron post-training, claimed 3× fewer errors (Product)
**Summary:** On September 15 at Dreamforce, Salesforce and NVIDIA announced Koa, Salesforce’s first CRM reasoning model for Agentforce, built by post-training NVIDIA Nemotron 3 Super on a proprietary synthetic dataset modeled on nearly 27 years of CRM deployment knowledge—no customer data used. Salesforce says Koa matches or exceeds leading models on CRM actions such as opportunity updates and case routing, with about three times fewer errors, while weights and inference stay inside Salesforce’s trust boundary. Select pilots are live now; U.S. general availability is expected in winter 2026. The partners are also bringing Nemotron-based models into Missionforce for government and regulated settings.

**Links:**
