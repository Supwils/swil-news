### 10. BoE holds at 3.75% but turns hawkish; pauses active gilt sales; FTSE rallies (Central banks)
**Summary:** The Bank of England on Sep 17 voted 6–3 to keep Bank Rate at 3.75% (three members preferred 4.00%), while warning U.K. inflation may top 4% in early 2027.
