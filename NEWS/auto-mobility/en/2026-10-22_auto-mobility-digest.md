### 2. MIIT drafts changes to NEV market-access rules covering software updates and cloud configuration (Policy / China)
**Summary:** Jiemian reported on October 22 that the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology's department of industrial policy and regulation has drafted a decision revising parts of six rules, including the provisions on access for new-energy vehicle makers and products. The auto-related clauses would allow provincial industry departments to carry out administrative inspections of vehicle makers and products, require exterior labeling on relevant vehicles, and let small-batch products apply for conditional access when they do not meet the general conditions. The draft would also set supervision rules for software updates, cloud-configuration changes, and hardware upgrades, and tighten the link between company monitoring platforms and the road-vehicle safety monitoring platform.

### 5. EPP Group says today's votes advance a reversal of the 2035 combustion-engine ban (Policy / Europe)
**Summary:** The European People's Party Group in the European Parliament said on October 22 that, with a broad centrist majority, the day's votes wrote technological neutrality into auto climate rules and that the group and its partners agreed to reverse the combustion-engine ban while adding incentives for green steel made in the EU. Transport spokesman Jens Gieseke said about 13.8 million jobs in Europe and about 7% of EU GDP depend on the auto industry. The group said the Environment Committee is expected to vote on November 4, with a plenary vote likely in December if that vote passes. EnergyNews reported the same day that the October 20 compromise among the EPP, the Socialists, and Renew still requires an 87.5% cut in vehicle CO2 by 2035 versus 2021, and lets EU-made low-carbon steel count for up to 7.5% of compliance. Eurofer described the credit as 3% at first, rising to 7.5% from 2035, and the report said the text is not yet law.

**Links:**

- [EPP Group — Votes described as advancing a reversal of the combustion-engine ban](https://www.eppgroup.eu/newsroom/get-europe-s-car-industry-back-in-the-race)
- [EnergyNews — Parliament compromise includes a low-carbon steel credit of up to 7.5%](https://energynews.biz/low-carbon-steel-credit-eu-parliament-car-co2-compromise/)

**Commentary:** Reversing the combustion ban is the EPP Group's political description. The Environment Committee votes on November 4, and the reported compromise still keeps an 87.5% CO2 cut by 2035.

### 13. Stellantis halts Jeep Wrangler production in Toledo; dealers hold about 156 days of stock (Supply chain / United States)
**Summary:** Kelley Blue Book reported on October 22 that Stellantis temporarily halted Jeep Wrangler production at its Toledo, Ohio, assembly plant after glycidyl methacrylate escaped a storage tank at a nearby MC Tank Transport facility. The report said the automaker sent workers home on Tuesday night and canceled Wednesday shifts. Emergency crews issued evacuation and shelter-in-place orders. The chemical irritates the eyes, nose, mouth, and skin. Dealers were reported to hold about 156 days of Wrangler inventory, above the 85-day supply they typically target, so a short stop is unlikely to create an immediate shortage for buyers. No restart date was given.
