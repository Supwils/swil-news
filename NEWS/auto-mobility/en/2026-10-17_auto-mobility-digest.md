### 2. BMW raises the iX5 EPA range estimate from 435 miles to 450 miles, with certification still pending (New model / United States)
**Summary:** InsideEVs reported on October 16 that John Kelly, a BMW product manager for luxury vehicles, said at the U.S. press launch of the fifth-generation X5 that the iX5 60 xDrive, using Gen 6 batteries, is expected to reach up to 450 miles (about 724 km) on the EPA cycle, above the 435-mile figure given this summer. He said the official EPA number should arrive in the coming weeks. Michael Ahlers, head of the X5 project, said the company treats 400 miles as the point at which customers stop worrying about range, and aims for about 450 miles to cover highway driving and air conditioning. The 144 kWh pack is the largest BMW has fitted to a production vehicle, using 46120 cylindrical cells 25 mm taller than those in the iX3 and i3. Charging is quoted at up to 460 kW, with 10% to 80% in 22 minutes. Dealers are scheduled to receive the car in the first quarter of 2027. BMWBlog restated the estimate on October 17. BMW's U.S. model page still showed a preliminary estimate of up to 435 miles when checked.

## II. Markets, Governance, and Exports

### 4. Xinhua: gasoline cars fell below one-third of sales in September, and NEV penetration reached 67% (Market / China)
**Summary:** National Business Daily on October 17 carried a Xinhua report of figures recently released by the China Passenger Car Association. In September 2026, gasoline cars' share of the Chinese market fell below one-third for the first time, and new-energy vehicle penetration rose to 67%. Five years earlier, gasoline cars still held more than 90% of the passenger-car market. Tesla chief executive Elon Musk reposted the figures and commented that China's dependence on oil is falling rapidly. International Energy Agency data in the same report say that in the second quarter of 2026, new-energy vehicles alone displaced the equivalent of 1.5 million barrels of crude oil a day in China, about France's daily oil consumption. The agency expects China's oil demand to peak in 2027, about two years earlier than its previous forecast.

**Summary:** ITV reported on October 17 that about 20 people gathered near Wayve's headquarters in north London on Saturday over safety, jobs, and congestion. TfL granted minicab licences for the vehicles in August. Uber has said more than 140,000 Londoners have opted in to show a preference for self-driving vehicles in the app.

**Summary:** CGTN reported on October 17 that Verne, a Croatian firm backed by Uber and powered by Pony.ai, has run a commercial robotaxi service in Zagreb since April, until now with a safety operator in the driver's seat. Chief executive Marko Pejkovic said fully driverless testing began in August on the roughly 22 km route between headquarters and the airport. Rides can be ordered in the Verne or Uber app, and the price is locked at 1.99 euros (about $2.22). The company says thousands of commercial rides have an average rating of 4.8 out of 5.

### 10. Kakao: a Seoul robotaxi drives child-safety zones and a tunnel, with a manager still aboard (Autonomous driving / South Korea)
**Summary:** Aju Press on October 17 described a 20- to 30-minute ride in a Kia EV6-based "Seoul Autonomous Vehicle" around Maebong Station in Gangnam. Kakao Mobility's AV-Kit uses three LiDAR units, five radars, and seven cameras, and a rear display shows a 3D view of the surroundings. Since August the operating area has included child-safety zones, where speed is capped at 80% of the limit, so a 30 km/h zone is driven at no more than 24 km/h. In the Maebong Tunnel, where GPS is weak, the safety manager did not take the wheel. The company has run a late-night service in Gangnam since March and expanded data collection to daytime in June, using edge cases to train the model. The car now combines an AI planner with a rule-based planner. Kakao plans to raise the share of the end-to-end model by year-end, with a rule-based safety evaluator still checking the final plan. If a passenger changes the destination mid-ride, autonomous driving stops and the manager intervenes.

### 11. Mercedes-Benz and NVIDIA extend their partnership toward urban assistance and future Level 4 (Autonomous driving / Germany)
**Summary:** Mercedes-Benz Group announced on October 16 that it will extend its strategic partnership with NVIDIA to develop the next generation of driver assistance and automated driving. MB.OS handles new functions and over-the-air updates and works with NVIDIA Hyperion's in-vehicle computing and sensor architecture. The Alpamayo Stack supplies full-stack driving software with AI reasoning for point-to-point urban assistance, active safety, and automated parking. Halos adds a parallel safety stack. The companies say the technologies support advanced assistance and development of future SAE Level 4 uses, including robot limousines. The work also covers engineering simulation and Mercedes-Benz's MO360 digital production system. Board member and chief technology officer Jörg Burzer called NVIDIA Mercedes-Benz's most important strategic partner in automated driving.
