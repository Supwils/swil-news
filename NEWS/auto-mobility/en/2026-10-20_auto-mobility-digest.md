## II. Electric-Vehicle Products

### 3. Li Auto launches Lianshan: lifetime cover for in-house batteries, and 500 km in 10 minutes under stated conditions (EV / China)
**Summary:** Li Auto launched the Lianshan electric technology system at its Beijing Power Day on October 20, covering batteries, drive units, and the charging network. CnEVPost reported two guarantees: if the original battery self-ignites because of a quality problem and the vehicle meets scrapping criteria, Li Auto replaces it with a new vehicle of the same configuration; if a battery fire after a collision or chassis scrape leads to scrapping, the company covers the gap between insured vehicle damage and the actual payout. Both are subject to eligibility conditions. Batteries across the lineup are to retain at least 75% health within 8 years or 160,000 kilometers. The new Li L8 offers 430 kilometers of CLTC pure-electric range, and the 2026 Li i6 up to 780 kilometers, more than 8% above the 2025 versions. Sina Finance, citing Dingjiao One, said a 5C battery can add 500 kilometers in 10 minutes under applicable conditions. The company has 4,200 supercharging stations and more than 23,000 stalls, and plans 8,300 5C stalls before the 2027 Spring Festival. The 2026 i6 launches on October 28, with deliveries in early November.

## III. Batteries, Swapping, and Charging

### 5. CATL's heavy-truck dual battery targets more than 95% depth of discharge, with 900 swap stations by year-end (Battery / China)
**Summary:** CnEVPost reported that CATL on October 20 in Beijing unveiled a dual-battery system for heavy trucks: a chassis-mounted swappable main pack plus a 77-kWh backup battery behind the cab that supports 3C charging. CATL said conventional swap trucks often return with about 30% charge left; with the backup pack, depth of discharge on the main battery can rise from about 70% to more than 95%, so trucks can return with under 5% in the main pack. New standardized blocks, the 260# and 300#, are rated at 432 kWh and 513 kWh. A new-generation Qiji station swaps a pack in about 5 minutes, cuts construction cost by 10%, needs no pit, and can be deployed in as little as 5 days. Basic battery-as-a-service plans bill net energy charged rather than energy discharged, which CATL says can cut per-kWh rental by up to 20%. The year-end target is 900 heavy-truck swap stations, 2,000 in 2027, and 10,000 longer term with partners. Agreements covering 13,000 vehicles were signed, including orders from SF Holding and China Postal Express & Logistics. Reuters separately described a long-range Tianxing II pack of 760 kWh with more than 600 kilometers of range, and said heavy-truck electrification globally remains below 10%.

## IV. Autonomous Driving and the Supply Chain

### 9. Waymo: fully autonomous ride-hailing in the Madrid region in 2027, with supervised driving in the coming days (Autonomous driving / Spain)
**Summary:** Reuters reported on October 20 that Alphabet's Waymo plans a fully autonomous ride-hailing service in Spain's Madrid region in 2027, its first deployment in the country. Electric Jaguar I-PACE vehicles will appear on the streets in the coming days with trained specialists behind the wheel before autonomous operations begin, under the testing framework of Spain's traffic agency, the DGT. Waymo's site says the initial area includes, and is not limited to, Madrid city, Alcobendas, and Getafe; the vehicles are still in testing and preparation, commercial rides are not yet offered, and testing typically runs 24 hours a day, seven days a week. The same wire note said the DGT last month issued its first national testing permits to WeRide and Uber.

### 10. Unifor authorizes a Stellantis strike by 97.3%; supplier Dakkota will close its Detroit plant (Supply chain / Canada, United States)
**Summary:** CBT News reported on October 20 that Unifor members voted on October 17 and 18, with 97.3% authorizing strike action at Stellantis plants in Canada, covering about 9,000 unionized employees on an expired agreement. The union is not yet in a legal strike position: neither side has requested the "No Board" report required under Ontario labor procedure, and an authorization vote does not itself stop work. The report says talks are paused over Stellantis's proposed sale of the idled Brampton, Ontario, plant to armored-vehicle maker Roshel, after a memorandum of understanding on September 11, and that Windsor production has been canceled for three weeks through the week of November 2. A notice filed with the state of Michigan on October 19 says supplier Dakkota Integrated Systems will close its Detroit plant in two stages and cut 397 jobs: one line stops on December 31, cutting 205 jobs, and the other on March 31, cutting 192. The plant builds instrument panels for the Jeep Grand Cherokee and Dodge Durango assembled nearby.
