## I. China & Europe: Markets and Policy

### 1. China's October NEV sales reach 1.94 million; exports up about 140%

**Summary:** According to CAAM October data (released November 11), NEV sales were 1.94 million, up 13.1% year on year, with 1.334 million sold domestically and 606,000 exported. NEV penetration hit a record 64.3%, the fourth straight month above 60%; exports rose roughly 140% while domestic sales fell 8.6%. BYD sold more than 300,000 BEVs in a single month for the first time.

**Commentary:** Growth has shifted from the domestic market to exports, amplifying the tension between weak home demand and growing overseas friction.

### 2. CPCA: NEV retail 219,000 in Nov 1–8, down 7% year on year

**Summary:** CPCA data show passenger-car NEV retail of 219,000 for November 1–8, down 7% year on year and up 27% from the same period in October, with NEV penetration at 74.9% versus October's record 67.5%. Overall passenger-car retail fell 21% year on year; CPCA cited a high 2025 base, said a year-end rebound is unlikely, and expects full-year 2026 vehicle exports of about 12 million.

**Links:**

- [CnEVPost — China NEV retail first 8 days Nov 2026](https://cnevpost.com/2026/11/11/china-nev-retail-first-8-days-nov-2026/)

**Commentary:** Record penetration alongside shrinking volume suggests NEVs are displacing combustion cars while overall domestic demand stays soft.

### 3. EU plans to cap Chinese hybrid imports at 350,000 a year from December 1

**Summary:** Multiple outlets report the EU plans an annual cap of 350,000 Chinese-built hybrids and plug-in hybrids from December 1, rising 6% a year from the second year over four years, with EU-built models exempt. The cap is expected to cut sales by BYD, Chery and MG in Europe by more than half. Reports differ on the measure's final legal status and we found no official EU document, so details and the start date should be checked against the Commission.

**Links:**

- [electrive — China's electric car exports surge by 140 per cent (policy context)](https://www.electrive.com/2026/11/11/chinas-electric-car-exports-surge-by-140-per-cent/)
- [InsideEVs — China Agrees To Limit Hybrid Imports To EU To 350,000 Annually](https://insideevs.com/news/811656/china-eu-trade-limit-350000/)

**Commentary:** Hybrids were the main route for Chinese makers around EU EV tariffs, and a quota narrows that route too.

## II. Autonomous Driving

### 4. Tesla launches unsupervised robotaxi service in San Antonio, its eighth city

**Summary:** Tesla began driverless rides without a safety driver in San Antonio using modified Model Y vehicles, its eighth unsupervised market and fifth in Texas. It has not disclosed fleet size; tracking sites estimated only about seven vehicles in the early hours. Austin's service area was expanded to roughly 443 square miles the same day.

## III. US Policy & Automakers

### 8. Democrats win both chambers of Congress, but impact on auto tariffs and policy looks limited

**Summary:** An industry analysis says Democrats won both chambers in the November 3 midterms, but a presidential veto limits their ability to reshape auto policy. Section 232 tariffs rest on presidential authority, so Congress's levers are oversight, the USMCA review and trade-talk pressure. USMCA-compliant vehicles are exempt, and deals with the EU and Japan set auto tariffs at 15%.

**Links:**

- [Automotive Logistics — What a Democratic Congress means for US automakers](https://www.automotivelogistics.media/ev-and-battery/what-a-democratic-congress-means-for-us-automakers/2765416)

**Commentary:** Policy direction is unlikely to reverse soon, so automakers must still plan capacity and products around tariffs and the end of EV subsidies.

### 9. Volkswagen recalls 22,524 ID.4 over battery fire risk; owners told to cap charging at 80%

**Summary:** Volkswagen is recalling 22,524 ID.4 vehicles in the US over a high-voltage battery defect linked to thermal incidents, with owners advised to limit charging to 80% and notification letters expected on November 27. Follow-up reports say the probe may extend to the ID.3, ID.7, ID. Buzz and Cupra Born, but we could not confirm the date of that expansion.
