## I. Policy & Markets

### 1. EU provisional hybrid quotas take effect Dec. 1; China allotted ~192,000 units over 200 days; MOFCOM responds (Policy)
**Summary:** On Nov 24 the European Commission published provisional safeguard measures on hybrid passenger cars (HEVs, PHEVs and mild hybrids): from Dec 1, country-specific import quotas apply and volumes above quota may not enter free circulation; the provisional measure lasts up to 200 days through June 18, 2027. Quotas are set roughly at 2025 import levels plus a little over 15%. Caixin and National Business Daily report China’s allotment at about 191,800–192,000 vehicles for the provisional window (about 350,000 on an annualized basis), the largest among origins. At a Nov 26 briefing, MOFCOM spokesperson He Yadong said China and the EU had reached an understanding on hybrid trade under principles of compliance, non-discrimination and respect for facts, urged the EU to honor that consensus and investigate fairly under WTO rules, and said Beijing will closely watch follow-up steps while defending legitimate industry interests.

**Commentary:** Quotas close the hybrid “tariff-work-around” channel—and raise the strategic value of European local plants another notch.

### 2. U.S. Connected Vehicle Security Act nears lame-duck passage, targeting adversary-linked connected cars (Policy)
**Summary:** POLITICO reported on Nov 26 that the Connected Vehicle Security Act (S.4429), led by Sens. Bernie Moreno and Elissa Slotkin, is closing in on passage this session after winning over key automakers. Sponsors aim for a December Senate voice vote and are coordinating House fast-track action. The bill would bar U.S. sale or interstate commerce of connected vehicles tied to components or software/hardware from foreign adversaries such as China and Russia, hardening Commerce Department connected-vehicle rules into statute. The Alliance for Automotive Innovation is “very supportive”; GM and the UAW have endorsed it. Reporting says negotiators moved to exclude batteries from the restrictions to protect arrangements such as Ford’s CATL-related battery work. Chinese vehicles already face steep tariffs; the bill is meant to lock in long-term supply-chain certainty.

**Links:**

- [POLITICO — China car ban at the 'one yard line' in Congress](https://www.politico.com/news/2026/11/26/congress-china-cars-autos-ban-lame-duck-01183667)
- [Congress.gov — S.4429 Connected Vehicle Security Act of 2026](https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/senate-bill/4429)

**Commentary:** Tariffs hit price; this statute hits “connected identity” and supply-chain provenance—U.S. barriers on Chinese autos are shifting from trade tools to a national-security toolkit.

## II. Autonomous Driving & Robotaxi

### 3. Waymo pauses driverless freeway service nationwide after CA ban, closed-lane entries and recalls (Autonomy)
**Summary:** The Verge’s Nov 26 account says that after California’s DMV barred Waymo from state interstates last week, the company paused commercial driverless freeway service across all five of its freeway markets. Between October and November its vehicles entered closed or coned-off freeway lanes 11 times (including three after a Nov 6 software change), and it also filed a recall tied to responses to officers directing traffic by hand. NHTSA filings cited in follow-on coverage show two voluntary recalls covering more than 4,400 fifth- and sixth-generation vehicles; Waymo says there were no collisions or injuries. Surface-street ride-hailing continues—over half a million trips a week, per a company spokesperson—while Waymo works with California officials on restoring freeway operations.

### 6. VW’s Elli launches BiDi V2G charger in Germany, paying owners by plugged-in hours (up to ~€720 in year one) (V2G)
**Summary:** TechRadar and related coverage on Nov 26 say Volkswagen Group charging unit Elli has launched its first vehicle-to-grid offer in Germany with the Elli BiDi charger: up to about 11 kW bidirectional power for VW, Cupra, Škoda and selected VW commercial models, with further European markets planned from 2027. In the first contract year Elli pays a plug-in bonus of about €0.24 per hour connected (sessions must last at least three hours), which at roughly 250 hours a month can reach about €60 a month or up to about €720 in year one. The BiDi unit costs about €1,995; grid offtake is compensated separately at about €0.40/kWh. An app lets owners set departure time and target state of charge so charge/discharge stays within those bounds.

### 7. Suzuki and ELIIY Power win METI storage-battery security certification for Kosai LFP production from 2029 (Battery)
**Summary:** On Nov 26 Suzuki and ELIIY Power said Japan’s Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry certified their plan to develop and mass-produce stationary lithium-ion cells and modules as a “Plan for Securing the Supply of Storage Batteries” under the Economic Security Promotion Act. A domestic base in Kosai, Shizuoka, is slated to start in October 2029 at about 0.48 GWh/year; investment is about ¥44.7 billion with a maximum subsidy of about ¥18.7 billion. The LFP chemistry uses no nickel or cobalt, targets both stationary durability and automotive energy density, and is designed for post-vehicle stationary repurposing, with gradual adoption in residential, C&I and automotive uses from FY2030. Nikkei frames the move as reducing reliance on Chinese battery supply and export-control risk.

### 8. Subaru prices UK E-Outback from £49,995 with early-2027 deliveries (New model)

### 9. Uber launches Assist in Brazil: reserved extra-help rides for riders with disabilities, older adults and pregnant passengers (Mobility)
**Summary:** Exame and related Nov 26 coverage say Uber launched Assist in Brazil for riders who may need extra boarding help—including people with disabilities, older adults and pregnant passengers—with driver assistance getting in/out and stowing foldable mobility aids. The pilot rolls out via Uber Reserve (book 30 minutes to 90 days ahead) across ten capitals: São Paulo, Rio de Janeiro, Brasília, Fortaleza, Salvador, Belo Horizonte, Manaus, Curitiba, Recife and Goiânia, at fares similar to Comfort Reserve. Selected drivers must meet trip and rating thresholds and take accessibility training; vehicles are roomier conventional cars without ramps and cannot take non-folding powered wheelchairs.
