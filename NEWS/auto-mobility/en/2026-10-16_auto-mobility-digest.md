## I. Debuts

### 1. Tesla completes the McGregor demo of the new Roadster and puts production in 2028 (New model / United States)
**Summary:** Reuters reported that Tesla showed a redesigned two-seat Roadster on Thursday at SpaceX's test site in McGregor, Texas, and said production would start in 2028. Built with SpaceX, the car drove upside down on a ramp using a suction system, then turned in the air and landed on built-in thrusters, and later jumped a truck and other vehicles. The reservation page after the event listed a $50,000 deposit for the Performance version, a $250,000 deposit for the Thrust version with acceleration thrusters, and an invite-only single-seat Rocket with a "full flight system." Vehicle engineering chief Lars Moravy said 0-60 mph takes less than one second and top speed exceeds 230 mph (370 km/h). Range is more than 300 miles (480 km), less than half of the 2017 promise. No price was given at the event. Tesla reports third-quarter results on October 21, and the shares were up 5% in Friday premarket trading.

## III. Autonomous driving

### 8. Waymo says it is heading to Toronto and aims to carry the first riders in 2028 (Autonomous driving / Canada)
**Summary:** Waymo's blog on October 16 said the company is laying the groundwork for fully autonomous ride-hailing in Toronto, with a goal of welcoming the first riders in 2028. In the coming days, vehicles will be driven manually to build high-definition maps, then tested with trained autonomous specialists behind the wheel. Fleet operations go to Toronto-based Element Fleet Management, and Waymo said it will invest over time in hundreds of roles in maintenance, validation driving, and customer service. The company said data from more than 400 million fully autonomous kilometres show 95% fewer crashes involving serious injury or worse than human drivers in the cities where it operates. TechCrunch added the same day that Waymo has approval for Ontario's automated-vehicle pilot, runs service in 15 U.S. cities, and is testing in London and Tokyo.

### 11. BMW starts series production of sixth-generation high-voltage batteries in Shenyang, with cylindrical-cell energy density up more than 20% (Battery / China)
**Summary:** A BMW Group release dated October 15 said series production of Gen6 high-voltage batteries has begun in Shenyang, the third site after Debrecen in Hungary and Irlbach-Straßkirchen in Germany, supplying Neue Klasse models built in China.
