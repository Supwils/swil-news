## I. EVs & Market Policy

**Summary:** The China Passenger Car Association on Nov 3 estimated nationwide passenger NEV wholesale at about 1.8 million units in October 2026, up roughly 12% year on year and 8% month on month—on track for a new monthly wholesale record—with January–October cumulative wholesale around 13.22 million (+about 9.9% YoY). ### 2. China’s October NEV penetration clears 50% for the first time; vehicle exports hit 666,000 (+22.9%) (Market / China)
**Summary:** EV industry coverage on Nov 3 citing CPCA said October NEV sales were about 1.715 million with penetration crossing 50% for the first time—roughly one in two new cars sold was a new-energy model. Narrow passenger-car retail was about 1.6 million, down about 28.9% YoY and 1.1% MoM. Growth leaned overseas: vehicle exports reached about 666,000 (+about 22.9% YoY), with Chery, Geely, BYD and SAIC posting strong export prints. The dealer inventory warning index was about 62.2% (higher in the north), with many dealers still reporting weaker close rates and prices.

### 6. Tesla’s Europe driver assist: Dutch RDW confirms speed margin cut from 50% to 10%; system renamed Tesla Assisted Driving (ADAS / Europe)
**Summary:** Electrek reported on Nov 3 that the Netherlands’ RDW confirmed approval of a Tesla update capping the European system’s speed-limit margin at +10% (from as high as +50%) and officially renaming “FSD Supervised” to “Tesla Assisted Driving.” The margin resets to 0% at each start, and some speed-related functions are disabled by default. RDW called the changes minor, folded them into the existing type approval without extending it, and stressed that the rename does not alter the driver-assistance classification or driver responsibility. It notified member states that recognize the Dutch approval; an EU-wide TCMV vote is still eyed for December at the earliest.

**Links:**

- [Electrek — Tesla cuts FSD’s speed margin from 50% to 10% over the limit in Europe](https://electrek.co/2026/11/03/tesla-assisted-driving-rdw-speed-cap-10-percent-europe/)

**Commentary:** The rename and tighter margin are de-risking ahead of an EU-wide vote—regulatory narrative may matter more than demos for Europe’s green light.

### 7. BMW finishes real-world testing of Level 2 city assist, targeting early-2027 German approval for turns, roundabouts and right-of-way (ADAS / Europe)
**Summary:** Motor1 and related coverage on Nov 3 said BMW has finished real-world testing of a driver-assistance system that can handle turns, roundabouts and right-of-way decisions in city traffic, aiming for regulatory approval in early 2027 with Germany first and other European markets to follow. The upgrade builds on BMW’s City Assistant (already supporting traffic-light stop/go), adds navigation-guided maneuvers, and remains SAE Level 2 with an attentive driver required. Functions need both an in-cabin attention camera and active BMW Maps route guidance; BMW groups the suite under “Symbiotic Drive” and has not described it as hands-off. Type approval is being sought under updated DCAS (UN R171) rules.

**Links:**

- [Motor1 — BMW Brings Level 2 City Assistant To Urban Traffic](https://www.motor1.com/news/814170/bmw-brings-level-2-city/)

**Commentary:** European OEMs are answering the Tesla narrative with a compliance-first Level 2 city pack—the contest is the type-approval path, not the level number in the press release.

## III. Batteries & Charging

### 8. Nio cumulative battery swaps pass 130 million: >110,000 daily average over 88 days; 4,236 China swap stations (Energy / China)
**Summary:** CnEVPost reported on Nov 3 that Nio said cumulative swaps passed 130 million at about 18:53 Beijing time, only 88 days after crossing 120 million on Aug 7. Daily swaps averaged more than 110,000 over that span—about one completed swap every 0.77 seconds. As of Nov 3, Nio had 4,236 swap stations in China (up 230 from 4,006 on Aug 7), plus 5,556 charging stations and 32,261 piles, for 9,792 combined sites. About 84.5% of users had a swap station within 3 km of home or work; cumulative swap electricity reached about 6.89 billion kWh, estimated to have saved users about 110 million hours.

**Links:**

- [CnEVPost — Nio adds 10 million battery swaps in 88 days, taking total past 130 million](https://cnevpost.com/2026/11/03/nio-battery-swaps-130-million/)
