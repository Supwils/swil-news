## IV. Batteries, Charging & Passenger Vehicles

### 9. UL Solutions launches AC bidirectional (V2G-AC) charger certification program (Charging / U.S.)
**Summary:** pv magazine USA reported on September 14 that UL Solutions opened testing and certification for AC bidirectional EV supply equipment under the new UL 1741 Supplement SC at labs in Fremont, California, and Northbrook, Illinois. The pathway covers wallboxes that supervise AC connections to vehicles with SAE J3072 onboard bidirectional inverters, without requiring the external high-power inverters common in today’s DC V2G products. That could cut hardware cost and improve interoperability, giving utilities, automakers, and vendors a clearer compliance path to treat EV fleets as distributed energy resources.

**Links:**

- [pv magazine USA — UL Solutions launches AC bidirectional EV charger certification program](https://pv-magazine-usa.com/2026/09/14/ul-solutions-launches-ac-bidirectional-ev-charger-certification-program/)

**Commentary:** Standards unlock procurement; cheaper AC bidirectional gear may decide V2G scale more than flashier DC demos.

### 10. GM exits Indiana battery JV as Samsung SDI takes full plant ownership (Supply chain / U.S.)
**Summary:** Industry reports on September 14 said Samsung SDI is ending its SynergyCells battery-plant venture with General Motors in New Carlisle, Indiana, by acquiring GM’s roughly 49.99% stake and taking sole ownership—its first wholly owned North American battery plant, with plans for a dedicated ESS line.
