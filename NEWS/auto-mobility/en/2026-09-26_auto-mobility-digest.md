## I. Batteries & Circular Supply Chain

### 1. GM completes closed-loop battery recycling pilot with 100% recycled Ni/Co/Mn cathode material (Batteries / Supply chain)
**Summary:** Cirba Solutions and trade coverage report that General Motors, Cirba Solutions and partners finished a closed-loop EV battery recycling pilot: about 80 end-of-life GM packs were processed into black mass at Cirba’s Ohio plant and refined into more than 12 metric tons of cathode active material (CAM) made with 100% recycled nickel, cobalt and manganese. Ultium Cells (GM–LG Energy Solution) built new cells from that CAM; GM assembled modules and packs at Factory ZERO and Spring Hill. First production vehicles—including Cadillac LYRIQ / VISTIQ / Escalade IQ models plus Chevrolet Silverado EV and GMC Sierra EV variants—are rolling off the line to customers.

**Links:**

- [Cirba Solutions — Closing the Loop: 100% recycled critical minerals power new GM EVs](https://www.cirbasolutions.com/closing-the-loop-battery-recycling-pilot-uses-100-recycled-critical-minerals-to-power-new-gm-evs/)
- [Electric Cars Report — GM Completes Closed-Loop EV Battery Recycling Pilot](https://electriccarsreport.com/2026/09/gm-completes-closed-loop-ev-battery-recycling-pilot-with-100-recycled-metals/)

**Commentary:** “100% recycled” still covers only three cathode metals—but the loop has left the lab and reached production vehicles.

### 2. Mercedes deepens ProLogium partnership for priority Gen4 solid-state cell testing (Solid-state batteries)
**Summary:** Carscoops reports Mercedes-Benz has strengthened its long-running tie-up with Taiwan’s ProLogium, gaining priority access to Gen4 lithium-ceramic solid-state cells for electrical, thermal and safety testing at Mercedes and third-party labs. The cells use ceramic separators and a non-flammable inorganic electrolyte aimed at fast charging and high power. ProLogium already runs a Taiwan plant and plans a Dunkirk, France site of up to about 44 GWh for European customers. Mercedes is also still testing Factorial Energy lithium-metal packs; an EQS with Factorial cells previously covered about 1,205 km on one charge.

**Links:**

- [Carscoops — Mercedes Signs Huge Deal To Help Make Solid State Batteries A Reality](https://www.carscoops.com/2026/09/mercedes-signs-huge-deal-to-help-make-solid-state-batteries-a-reality/)

**Commentary:** Dual-supplier testing hedges solid-state scale-up risk; European capacity promises matter as much as lab range feats.

### 6. Ford recalls ~86 Mach-Es with replaced battery modules: repeated DC fast charging may risk power loss and HV fire (Safety)
**Summary:** Morning Overview reports Ford told NHTSA that roughly 86 model-year 2023–2025 Mustang Mach-Es (Ford’s own count ~83) that previously received a replacement Battery Energy Control Module under unrelated repairs may report a full state of charge incorrectly; after repeated DC fast charging, that can lead to unexpected loss of propulsion and elevated high-voltage fire risk. Ford says it has no reports of crashes, injuries or fires tied to the condition. Owner warning letters are targeted for about October 26, 2026, with a free software reflash remedy aimed for December 2026.

**Links:**

- [Morning Overview — Ford’s Mustang Mach-E can catch fire after repeated fast-charging, a new recall warns](https://morningoverview.com/fords-mustang-mach-e-can-catch-fire-after-repeated-fast-charging-a-new-recall-warns/)

**Commentary:** Small population, big signal: EV safety risk can ride in on service parts, not just the factory line.
