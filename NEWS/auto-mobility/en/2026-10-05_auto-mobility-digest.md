## I. EVs and Markets

### 1. Germany registered 256,774 cars in September; battery EVs took 34.5%, and Tesla rose 268.7% (EVs / Germany)
**Summary:** Germany's Federal Motor Transport Authority (KBA) on October 5 published press release No. 41/2026. New passenger-car registrations in September were 256,774, up 9.0% from a year earlier. The January-September total was 2,221,798, up 5.3%. Private registrations rose 28.5% and accounted for 40.3%; commercial registrations fell 1.2%. Battery-electric cars numbered 88,599, or 34.5% of the market, more than one in three. Hybrids numbered 98,871, up 1.7%, for a 38.5% share; plug-in hybrids were flat versus a year earlier, at a 10.8% share. Petrol cars numbered 44,632, down 29.2%, for a 17.4% share. Diesel cars numbered 23,058, down 20.1%, for a 9.0% share. Average CO2 emissions of new cars were 82.7 g/km, down 19.6%. Among import brands with more than 4,000 registrations in the month, Tesla gained the most: 12,552 vehicles, up 268.7%, for a 4.9% share. Tesla's nine-month total was 44,810, up 201.9%. BYD registered 6,040, up 85.6%, for a 2.4% share. Volkswagen registered 42,461, down 6.1%, and remained the largest German brand with a 16.5% share. Tagesschau reported the same day that the government said an EV purchase subsidy in place since May had already been paid to 100,000 applicants.

### 2. Bloomberg: U.S. EV share is near 6% this year, and dealers are asking for electric inventory again (EVs / United States)
**Summary:** Claims Journal on October 5 carried a report by Kara Carlson and Gabrielle Coppola. A year after the $7,500 federal EV tax credit ended, U.S. EV demand is stabilizing, but it has not returned to the pre-expiry peak. Cox Automotive puts EVs at close to 6% of the U.S. new-car market this year. In last year's third quarter, the rush before the credit expired pushed the share to nearly 11%. Dealer EV inventory fell from about a 180-day supply in early 2026 to 78 days in August, roughly in line with combustion-vehicle stocks. Rivian delivered 19,248 vehicles in the third quarter, above the roughly 17,600 analysts expected. The report ties that to the first full quarter of the midsize R2 SUV, priced around $50,000. Tesla sold 486,532 vehicles in the quarter, down 2.1%, and ahead of the roughly 464,000 average analyst estimate. Randy Parker, chief executive of Hyundai's North American business, said more dealers are asking for EV inventory as gas prices rise, but the company is not planning to raise production. Ford's U.S. sales chief Rob Kaffl said Friday that Mustang Mach-E is seeing returning customers, while volumes have not rebounded significantly from last year.

**Links:**

- [Claims Journal — Gas-price anxiety points to stabilizing U.S. EV demand](https://www.claimsjournal.com/news/national/2026/10/05/340541.htm)

**Commentary:** The evidence in today's piece is a share stuck near 6%, thinner dealer stocks, and two EV makers beating delivery estimates.

## II. Autonomous Driving and Mobility Platforms

### 4. Volvo and Waabi start customer freight for Warp in Texas, with an observer still in the driver's seat (Autonomy / United States)
**Summary:** Volvo Group said on October 5 that Volvo Autonomous Solutions and Waabi have started their first customer operation in Texas. A Volvo VNL Autonomous powered by the Waabi Driver is hauling freight directly to third-party facilities on the Dallas-Houston corridor for Warp, a network that handles both less-than-truckload and full-truckload freight. The companies said autonomous hauls currently run with an observer in the driver's seat. The VNL Autonomous is built with redundancies for six safety-critical systems. Nils Jaeger, president of Volvo Autonomous Solutions, said experience on the Dallas-Houston lane will be used to add routes. Waabi chief operating officer Lior Ron called the work a first step toward using the truck inside less-than-truckload networks and delivering to third-party sites.

## III. Batteries and Charging

### 7. Honda and Taisei publish the base technology for in-motion wireless charging, with public-road tests from fiscal 2027 (Charging / Japan)
**Summary:** Honda said on October 5 that Honda R&D, Taisei Corporation, and Taisei Rotec have developed the underlying technology for a magnetic-coupling wireless power transfer road system that can charge electric vehicles while they are moving. Demonstration testing on public roads is planned from Japanese fiscal 2027, which starts on April 1, 2027. Honda has worked on dynamic wireless power transfer since 2021, and the three companies have researched together since 2025. The system is aimed at vehicles from passenger EVs to large commercial EVs, and the in-road units and pavement are designed for traffic that includes vehicles of about 20 tons gross weight. Honda said integrating the inverter and coil into one in-road unit with DC distribution was the first such unit publicly disclosed as of the end of September 2026, based on Honda's own research. From late 2026, a test roadway at the Taisei Group's Tamura future-technology field will be used for a durability load equivalent to one million wheel passes at 49 kilonewtons per wheel, power-transfer tests, and tests of measures against electromagnetic leakage, plus verification of output up to about 150 kilowatts. The companies will also join a NEXCO East demonstration, the Tateyama Project, on the Tateyama Expressway in Chiba from fiscal 2027. Honda plans to present the work at Japan Mobility Show Bizweek on October 13-16 and at the ITS World Congress 2026 in Gangneung, South Korea, on October 19-23.

**Commentary:** What was published is the hardware that goes under the pavement; the public-road demonstration is scheduled for fiscal 2027, not a road that is live today.

### 8. CCTV: some highway service areas tell drivers to leave at 80% charge during the return peak (Charging / China)
**Summary:** CCTV News reported on October 5 that electric vehicles queued at many highway service areas during the National Day holiday, and that some sites now require drivers to leave once the battery reaches 80%. Qin Jian, director of the charging-service center at State Grid's smart vehicle-network company, said lithium-ion batteries fast-charge from 0% to 80% and then taper, so the last 20% takes about as long as the first 80%. The report says an 80% stop makes sense at peak times, while drivers can still fill the battery when sites are quiet. It cited the Ministry of Transport's view that outbound trips are spread out and return trips bunch up. October 5 and 6 are the core return days, with charging peaks from 11:00 to 21:00 on the 5th and from 10:00 to 22:00 on the 6th. A list of especially busy service areas was drawn up before the holiday, with mobile chargers and high-power fast chargers added. The same report restated figures from the national charging-facility monitoring platform, covering 62,700 highway charging connectors: from 00:00 on October 1 through 24:00 on October 3, there were 2.9686 million charging sessions, up 48.41%, and 71.6193 million kilowatt-hours, up 49.39%. Drivers can check whether a connector is free in the e-Lu Changtong mini program.

**Links:**

- [CCTV News via Sina Finance — Some highway service areas adopt an 80% charge-and-leave rule](https://finance.sina.com.cn/wm/2026-10-05/doc-iniucuem4604810.shtml)

**Commentary:** What is new today is the leave-at-80% rule and the return-peak window; the first three days' session and energy totals are the backdrop in the same report.

### 9. A Volvo electric truck completes Finland's first public megawatt charge in Kotka (Charging / Finland)
**Summary:** Plugit said on October 5 that Finland's first public Megawatt Charging System session is complete. On October 2, Volvo's new all-electric FH Aero Electric charged at Plugit's PRO station in Kotka. The company said the truck can charge at up to 700 kilowatts and has a range of up to 700 kilometers. The session was completed both through Volvo Open Charge and with Plugit's own cable, and both methods worked. Janne Silvonen, product manager for Volvo's electric trucks, said the combination of long range and MCS charging can make electric trucks practical for longer hauls. Plugit said public MCS charging in Finland is available in Kotka and Lieto. The company has about 350 public charge points, nine of them for heavy vehicles, and a tenth heavy-vehicle public point is under construction in Eskilstuna, Sweden.

## IV. Manufacturing and Supply Chain

### 10. Toyota halts three Thai plants through October 10 after flooding (Supply chain / Thailand)
**Summary:** Zigwheels on October 5 cited a statement from Toyota Motor Thailand. Production at the Samrong, Banpho, and Gateway plants is suspended from that day through Saturday, October 10. Flooding disrupted parts deliveries in Samut Prakan, Chachoengsao, and Chonburi. The company said the timetable can change depending on conditions and whether parts deliveries resume. It is coordinating with suppliers, managing parts inventory, and adjusting production and logistics plans. Incoming-parts inspection, assembly process controls, and quality checks at each stage will continue under the existing standards. If a customer's delivery date is affected, dealers will be asked to tell the customer.
