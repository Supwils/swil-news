### 2. Europe’s BEV share hits 25% for the first 10 months of 2026; October alone reaches 31% (Market)
**Summary:** Automotive World reported on Nov 27, citing the International Council on Clean Transportation (ICCT), that battery-electric cars took 25% of new registrations across the EU, Norway and Iceland in the first 10 months of 2026—up seven points year on year—and 31% in October alone. Germany and France reached year-to-date shares of about 29% and 32%; Italy and Spain about 8% and 12%; Poland about 5%. Combustion-only cars fell to about 28%. Most manufacturer pools already meet 2025–2027 CO₂ targets; Volkswagen Group remains about 5 g/km over but is closing fast. ICCT Europe director Peter Mock cautioned that once targets are met, incentives to keep pushing EV sales may weaken, risking a slower adoption pace.

**Links:**

- [Automotive World — Europe’s battery electric share reaches 25% for 2026](https://www.automotiveworld.com/news/europes-battery-electric-share-reaches-25-for-2026/)

**Commentary:** European electrification is shifting from “sprint to compliance” to “will momentum hold after targets are met”—record shares meet softer post-compliance incentives as the next variable.

## IV. Batteries, Charging & Mobility Platforms

### 9. CATL unveils 100 kWh “30#” Chocolate swap block and intelligent replenishment with Huawei Qiankun (Battery)
**Summary:** At the Nov 26 Huawei Qiankun Ecosystem Conference, CATL’s Chocolate swapping unit introduced the first 100 kWh version of its standardized 30# swap block; international coverage followed on Nov 27. CATL claims about 700 km CLTC range, about 600 kW peak discharge, roughly 99-second swaps, and about 400 km of range from 10 minutes of conventional fast charging, with a locking mechanism rated for a theoretical ~27 years of daily swaps and a roadmap toward sodium-ion and solid/liquid-solid chemistries. Huawei Qiankun and the swap network share cloud AI scheduling for in-trip prediction and one-tap station arrival; the passenger-car swap network targets about 6,000 stations across some 297 Chinese cities in 2027. Today’s stations still lean on 25# packs (about 56 kWh LFP); the 30# is meant for larger energy needs.

### 11. BYD adds storage cabinets at busy flash chargers: peak speed depends on buffers; grid capacity still caps throughput (Charging)
**Summary:** CarNewsChina reported on Nov 27 that energy-storage cabinets are appearing at some of BYD’s busiest flash-charging stations. Each gun can peak near 1,500 kW at about 1,000 V from a ~2,100 kW host; a typical two-gun site uses about two cabinets (~370 kWh nameplate) behind a ~300–400 kVA transformer. Storage supplies short megawatt bursts while the grid slowly refills the cabinets; once empty, sites must still deliver at least about 300 kW, so the marketed “nine-minute” experience can fall back to ordinary DC fast charging. Analysis says extra cabinets help short peaks, but under all-day high demand the grid connection still sets how many true flash sessions a site can sustain.

**Links:**

- [CarNewsChina — BYD spotted adding storage to busy flash chargers](https://carnewschina.com/2026/11/27/byd-spotted-adding-storage-to-busy-flash-chargers-but-it-still-may-not-hold-up/)

**Commentary:** After minute-scale charging ads hit the road, the fight instantly moves to distribution and storage dispatch—vehicle power ratings cannot outrun the transformer nameplate.
