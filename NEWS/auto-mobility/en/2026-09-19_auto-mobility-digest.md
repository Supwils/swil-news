## I. European Automakers

### 1. VW board papers propose about 4,100 further Porsche job cuts to close a €700 million overhead gap (Jobs / Europe)
**Summary:** Handelsblatt reported on September 19 that recent Volkswagen supervisory-board documents propose cutting about 4,100 more employees in the Sport Luxury brand group, meaning Porsche, to address an overhead shortfall of about €700 million. Reuters, citing the paper the same day, said the reduction would be "in addition to existing agreements." Volkswagen declined to comment, and a Porsche spokesperson declined to comment on the board papers. Because Porsche has been more independent since its listing, the parent can recommend such steps but cannot order them. In July, management and labor agreed to 5,000 further cuts on top of about 4,000 already set, bringing the currently agreed program to roughly one in five jobs by 2035. The report follows Volkswagen's September 18 cut of its full-year operating return on sales outlook to 1% at best, from 4.0%–5.5%, driven largely by a Porsche writedown after weaker China sales and a reversal of the EV strategy.

## II. China Models & Deliveries

### 2. Dongfeng Forthing Xinghai V6 launches from a 99,900-yuan promo price with Huawei Qiankun ADS 5 SE (New model / China)
**Summary:** Autohome and ifeng Auto reported on September 19 that Dongfeng Liuzhou launched the battery-electric Xinghai V6 at its autumn event in Liuzhou. Three trims carry list prices of 104,900–124,900 yuan and launch promo prices of 99,900–119,900 yuan. The SUV measures 4,835 × 1,900 × 1,755 mm with a 2,900 mm wheelbase and offers three-row six- or seven-seat layouts. Battery packs of 45.5 kWh or 60.5 kWh correspond to 400 km or 510 km CLTC range; a 30%–80% charge takes about 26 minutes, and the car supports 6.6 kW external discharge. ifeng separately cited average consumption of about 11.8 kWh/100 km. An Explorer variant was also previewed.

## III. Recalls

### 4. BYD files a recall of 183,211 Tang and Qin vehicles; NIO firefly and Stellantis hybrids are in the same batch (Safety / China)
**Summary:** A State Administration for Market Regulation defect-recall notice says BYD Auto Industry and BYD Auto filed plans covering 58,026 Tang vehicles built from March 28, 2015, to December 21, 2018; 77,846 new-generation Tang DM vehicles built from May 19, 2018, to January 14, 2022; and 7,023 new-generation Tang EV vehicles built from December 27, 2018, to October 25, 2021, under S2026M0116V. BYD Auto is also recalling 40,316 Qin vehicles built from June 27, 2014, to June 9, 2019, under S2026M0117V. The combined total is 183,211. A batch defect in the brake-pedal stop pad can crack after long use and, in extreme cases, leave the brake lights on when the pedal is not pressed. Dealers will replace the pad at no charge. Dongguan Daily on September 19 relayed the administration's September 18 cluster of notices: NIO is recalling 686 firefly EVs built from March 20 to April 2, 2026 (S2026M0114V), because a steering-column torque-sensor harness may interfere with the rotor and, in extreme cases, power steering can fail; Stellantis Shanghai is recalling 979 imported Wrangler hybrids and 1,178 new Grand Cherokee 4xe hybrids (S2026M0115V) over sand contamination during engine casting that can, in extreme cases, cause a loss of power or an engine-bay fire.

**Links:**

## V. Charging & Supply Chain

### 7. New South Wales adds A$16.5 million for fleet chargers and kerbside ports (Charging / Australia)
**Summary:** The Driven reported on September 19 that the New South Wales government will invest another A$16.5 million in EV charging: A$11.5 million to help businesses, non-government organisations, and councils install chargers for their fleets, and A$5 million in co-funding for kerbside charging. Kerbside grants are up to A$6,000 per AC port and A$30,000 per DC fast-charge port. For businesses and councils with at least 21 passenger vehicles, SUVs, vans, or light trucks up to 4.5 tonnes, support is up to A$8,000 per DC port and A$2,000 per AC port. Funded sites must use 100% renewable electricity on an ongoing basis. Energy Minister Penny Sharpe said more than 155,000 EVs are registered in the state, saving more than 200 million litres of fuel a year; the government said owners can save as much as A$3,000 a year in running costs. Fleet funding to date has supported purchases of almost 5,000 EVs and more than 1,500 charge points, and the state has funded about 3,300 chargers overall. Kerbside eligibility expands from 32 to 37 local government areas, adding the Blue Mountains, Camden, Campbelltown, Hawkesbury, and Wollondilly for the first time. Commercial carparks qualify alongside council carparks.
