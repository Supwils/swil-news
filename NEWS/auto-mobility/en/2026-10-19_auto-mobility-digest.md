### 2. EIA: hybrids were 16% of U.S. new light vehicles in the third quarter, battery-electrics 6% (Market / United States)
**Summary:** The U.S. Energy Information Administration's Today in Energy note on October 19 says that in the third quarter of 2026, hybrids, battery-electrics, and plug-in hybrids together were 23% of new light-duty vehicle sales, down slightly from 24% a year earlier. Hybrids reached 16%. Omdia estimates put battery-electrics at 6%, down from 10%, and plug-in hybrids at 0.7%, down from 1.8%. Battery-electrics were 6% of new-vehicle sales in the first nine months of 2026, down from 8% a year earlier, and 12% of luxury light-vehicle sales, down from 31% in the third quarter of 2025. The New Clean Vehicle Credit and the Qualified Commercial Clean Vehicle Credit expired on September 30, 2025, and hybrids were not eligible; Kelley Blue Book data show the average new-vehicle transaction price passed $50,000 this quarter for the first time this year.

## II. Electric Vehicles and Factories

### 4. Subaru shows an in-house EV mule at about 510 horsepower, with some technology aimed at 2029 (EV / Japan)
**Summary:** Subaru showed reporters an in-house EV-platform prototype on October 18 at a proving ground north of Tokyo. Car and Driver and Electrek reported it on October 19, citing Automotive News. The mule uses an Outback body with a longer wheelbase and dual-motor all-wheel drive: Car and Driver puts the front motor at about 201 horsepower and the rear at about 308, about 510 combined, with 0-62 mph in under four seconds; Electrek writes the total as 380 kW, about 509 horsepower. The motors were developed with Aisin and, engineers told Automotive News, are 30% more efficient than the industry average. The pack, developed with Panasonic Energy, uses 4,000 cylindrical cells; Car and Driver says it is 25% lighter than the previous pack, Electrek says 24%, and Subaru is targeting energy density 20% above the industry average. No capacity, range, or charging figures were released, and no production model was confirmed, though some of the technology is due to reach customers from 2029; the platform is said to be about 100 kg lighter than current ones.

## III. Automated Driving and Mobility

### 6. Mercedes-Benz widens its Nvidia partnership toward urban point-to-point assistance and future Level 4 (Automated driving / Germany, United States)
**Summary:** Nvidia's newsroom says Mercedes-Benz and Nvidia announced on October 16 an expanded partnership to develop the next generation of driver assistance and automated driving; Just Auto followed the story on October 19. The work pairs Mercedes-Benz engineering, safety practice, and MB.OS with Nvidia Hyperion in-vehicle computing and sensors, the Alpamayo Stack driving software, and the Halos safety system. Alpamayo supplies full-stack software with AI reasoning for urban point-to-point assistance, active safety, and automated parking; Halos sits alongside Mercedes-Benz systems as a parallel safety stack with added redundancy and guardrails. The companies say the technologies will support advanced driver assistance and future SAE Level 4 uses, including robolimousines. Mercedes-Benz also uses Nvidia technology for engineering simulation and in MO360, its digital production system.

### 7. Santa Monica authorizes talks that could park up to 150 Zoox robotaxis (Robotaxi / United States)
**Summary:** The Santa Monica Daily Press reported on October 19 that the City Council voted 6-0 on Tuesday, October 13, to let staff keep negotiating with Amazon-owned Zoox and, if terms are reached, sign a license to stage and store up to 150 robotaxis in city parking facilities. Primary sites under consideration are the first underground level of the Main Library structure and parts of its surface lot; the kiss-and-ride lot south of the Downtown Expo station is being assessed as a short-term interim site. City Manager Oliver Chi described a pilot of up to one year, with charging elsewhere and city sites used only for parking, and said the city expects about $500,000 or more a year in new recurring revenue, paid per space. Councilmember Dan Hall recused himself because he may hold Amazon stock. Chi said Zoox is running a San Francisco pilot, includes Santa Monica in a Los Angeles-area rollout, and that the vehicles are fully electric.

### 8. An eight-seat driverless shuttle ran on a Margate public road at up to 16 mph (Automated driving / United Kingdom)
**Summary:** The BBC reported that on Friday an eight-seat Ohmio Lift electric shuttle gave free rides on a public road in Margate, Kent, between the railway station and Turner Contemporary, as part of the Self Driving Industry Awards held at the gallery. East Thanet MP Polly Billington and Margate mayor Leo Britcher both backed making the link permanent in the tourist season.

### 11. The Alliance for Automotive Innovation endorses BAAJA to speed permits for critical minerals and the grid (Policy / United States)
**Summary:** Automotive World reported on October 19 that the Alliance for Automotive Innovation has backed the Bipartisan American Affordability and Jobs Act, a Senate bill to speed federal permitting for energy, grid, and critical-mineral projects.
