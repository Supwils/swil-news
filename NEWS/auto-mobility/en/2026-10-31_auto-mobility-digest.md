### 2. European Commission opens hybrid-import safeguard probe after ~49% surge to ~1.42 million vehicles (Policy / EU)
**Summary:** On Oct 30 the European Commission published notice C/2026/5780 in the Official Journal, opening an ex-officio safeguard investigation into new hybrid passenger cars from all origins—plug-in and non-plug-in vehicles that combine a combustion engine with electric propulsion motors and seat up to nine people. The notice cites imports rising from about 957,965 in 2023 to about 1,423,691 in July 2025–June 2026 (+49%), arriving at prices “significantly below” EU producers and pressuring share and profitability. Interested parties have roughly 15–21 days for registrations, questionnaires and submissions; a decision is due within nine months (extendable by two). The notice itself does not set the previously reported ~350,000-vehicle quota or a Dec 1 start date.

**Links:**

- [Teslant — EU opens safeguard investigation into hybrid imports](https://teslant.com/en/news/eu-safeguard-investigation-hybrid-imports-49-percent-2026-10)
- [EU Law Live — Commission opens safeguard investigation into hybrid passenger vehicles](https://eulawlive.com/commission-opens-safeguard-investigation-into-imports-of-new-hybrid-passenger-vehicles/)

**Commentary:** After BEV duties on China, Brussels is now formally fencing the hybrid path around those tariffs—and Japanese and Korean hybrids sit inside the same net.
