## I. Markets and Automakers

### 1. CPCA: October NEV retail forecast at about 1.07 million, with penetration of 66.9% (Market / China)
**Summary:** The China Passenger Car Association on Friday forecast October retail sales of about 1.6 million passenger cars, down 1.1% from September and 28.9% year on year. New-energy vehicle retail sales are forecast at about 1.07 million, down 16.5% year on year and about 1.3% from September, for a retail penetration rate of about 66.9%, 0.1 percentage point below September's record 67.0%. The association said showroom traffic faded after the National Day holiday, most automakers had completed less than 60% of their annual targets in the first three quarters, and efforts to curb excessive competition limited price cuts. Previously released figures showed 520,000 NEV retail sales from October 1 to 18, down 13% year on year, with penetration at 65.9%. September was the ninth straight month of lower NEV retail sales.

## III. Autonomous Driving

### 8. NHTSA proposes optional cyan marker lamps to show that an automated system is driving (Policy / United States)
**Summary:** The National Highway Traffic Safety Administration on October 23 proposed revisions to Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard No. 108. The draft would modernize manual turn-signal, hazard-warning, and beam-switching requirements for vehicles driven only by an automated driving system, and would set color, mounting, activation, and visibility rules for optional cyan marker lamps. Those lamps would tell law enforcement and other road users when an automated driving system is operating the vehicle. Manufacturers would not have to install them, but any installation would have to follow one specification. The proposal would also relax the rigid 12.8-volt test for integral-beam headlamps and remove the old rule on whether high beams sit above or below low beams. A 30-day comment period opens after publication in the Federal Register, under docket NHTSA-2026-1519. This revision is separate from a negotiated rulemaking on other parts of Standard 108 announced earlier this month.

**Links:**

- [NHTSA — Lighting-standard updates for automated vehicles](https://www.nhtsa.gov/press-releases/vehicle-lighting-updates-automated-vehicles)
- [The Verge — Regulators propose cyan lights to mark driverless operation](https://www.theverge.com/transportation/1017435/nhtsa-autonomous-vehicle-cyan-lighting-fmvss)

**Commentary:** The cyan lamp is a proposed and optional marker; the comment clock starts only after Federal Register publication, so it is not yet a required road signal.

### 10. Pony.ai signs a memorandum with inDrive and Nvidia for a Level 4 pilot in Astana (Robotaxi / Kazakhstan)
**Summary:** Pony.ai on Friday signed a memorandum of understanding with ride-hailing platform inDrive and Nvidia, making Kazakhstan its first pilot market in Central Asia. After an agreement with the Kazakh government, the partners plan to deploy seventh-generation robotaxis and Level 4 driving first in the capital, Astana.
