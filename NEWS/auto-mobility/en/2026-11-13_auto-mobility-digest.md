### 2. MIIT studies commercial-vehicle carbon credits; MW charging and swap standards to follow (Policy / China)
**Summary:** Shanghai Securities News and related outlets reported on Nov 13 that Yao Zhenzhi, deputy director of MIIT’s Equipment Industry Development Center, said at a Wuhan commercial-vehicle conference that China is studying a commercial-vehicle carbon-credit system to steer decarbonization through markets, while accelerating megawatt-class ultra-fast charging and battery-swap standards for heavy-duty NEVs in trunk logistics and urban delivery. CAAM data show more than about 800,000 domestic NEV commercial-vehicle sales in the first three quarters, up more than about 40% and about 35.2% of domestic commercial-vehicle sales, with September alone near 46.6%. A nine-ministry “15th Five-Year” plan targets about 40% NEV share of domestic commercial-vehicle new sales by 2030.

### 3. Zoox wheel-less robotaxis hit Atlanta streets; public rides eyed for 2027 (Autonomy / US)

### 4. D.C. AV bill dies on 3–3 tie; commercial robotaxi rollout postponed again (Policy / US)
**Summary:** NBC4 Washington reported that the D.C. Council’s Transportation and Environment Committee defeated a revised autonomous-vehicle bill on a 3–3 tie, so the measure does not advance. ### 5. New Orleans and Jefferson Parish align on robotaxi rules ahead of Waymo driverless push (Policy / US)
**Summary:** The Times-Picayune reported on Nov 13 that New Orleans and Jefferson Parish are coordinating a shared robotaxi regulatory framework before companies such as Waymo launch driverless testing. A draft ordinance would treat for-hire AVs like taxicab companies—requiring registration and per-ride fees, insurance and on-call support, a way for public-safety personnel to disable vehicles, and bans on idling in residential areas or bike lanes. Waymo has been testing about a dozen human-supervised cars locally this year and has not commented on the ordinance; it now operates in about 16 cities after rapid 2026 expansion.

**Links:**

- [NOLA.com — New Orleans, Jefferson Parish coordinating on robotaxi rules](https://www.nola.com/news/business/waymo-new-orleans-jefferson-parish-ordinance-draft/article_169b20e6-f165-4437-8a1a-e8dc625b2d63.html)

**Commentary:** Cities are moving from chasing ride-hail after the fact to writing robotaxi rules before driverless fleets arrive.
