## I. Policy and markets

### 1. Four ministries open a consultation: innovative designs face tighter filing from 2027, and fully concealed handles and folding screens are named (Policy / China)
**Summary:** On October 10, the Equipment Industry Development Center I of China's Ministry of Industry and Information Technology published a draft notice, prepared with the Ministry of Public Security, the Ministry of Ecology and Environment, and the State Administration for Market Regulation, on stronger control of innovative vehicle design and development testing. Comments are due by November 10, 2026, at qiche@miit.gov.cn. From January 1, 2027, newly declared announcement products that use innovative design must file extra technical parameters and complete a safety and environmental assessment under the innovative-design technical guide; products with inadequate testing or false materials will not be announced. Products already on the announcement must supplement their filings by July 1, 2027, and those that still pose a safety hazard after that date must stop production and be recalled. The Paper reported on October 10 that the guide bans fully concealed door handles and the use of folding or flexible displays. The testing specification requires a reliability run of at least 30,000 km and an environmental-adaptation cycle of at least one year, including a cold-hot-cold sequence described as two winters and one summer.

### 2. U.S. EV share has settled near 6% this year; Tesla's third quarter was down 2.1%, and Rivian beat delivery estimates (Market / United States)
**Summary:** The Seattle Times published a Bloomberg story on October 11 saying U.S. EV demand is stabilizing after the boom and bust that followed the end of the federal purchase credit. Cox Automotive data put EV sales close to 6% of the U.S. market this year, after a peak near 11% in last year's third quarter, just before the credit expired. Dealer EV inventory fell from about a 180-day supply in early 2026 to 78 days in August, in line with combustion-vehicle stocks. Rivian delivered 19,248 vehicles in the third quarter, above the roughly 17,600 analysts expected; the report ties that to the first full quarter of the midsize R2, priced around $50,000. Tesla sold 486,532 vehicles in the quarter, down 2.1% and ahead of an analyst average near 464,000, against last year's third-quarter rush of nearly half a million. Hyundai's North America chief Randy Parker said dealers are asking for more EVs, but the company is not raising production. Ford has canceled several models and announced $19.5 billion in related charges, while its U.S. sales chief said returning Mustang Mach-E buyers are still there.

**Links:**

- [The Seattle Times — Anxiety over gas prices points to a looming EV comeback in the U.S.](https://www.seattletimes.com/business/anxiety-over-gas-prices-points-to-looming-ev-comeback-in-u-s/)

**Commentary:** Six percent is this year's stable share, not a new sales peak; the Tesla and Rivian figures are third-quarter deliveries, not October 11 sales.

## III. Batteries and charging

### 4. CATL and Seres: plant-in-plant phase two is 10 to 12 GWh, and both phases together exceed 20 GWh a year (Battery / China)
**Summary:** CnEVPost reported on October 11, citing a CATL statement, that CATL and Seres signed a long-term strategic agreement in Chongqing on October 10 and that Aito will keep using CATL batteries across the lineup. Aito has used CATL batteries in every model since the brand began, and cumulative deliveries have passed 1.2 million vehicles. The companies will speed construction of phase two of the plant-in-plant project inside the Seres factory, with planned annual capacity of 10 to 12 GWh; once phase two is finished, combined annual capacity of both phases will exceed 20 GWh. On June 30, 2025, CATL started two CTP 2.0 pack lines at the plant, its first production base in Chongqing and its first use of the plant-in-plant model. The agreement also covers supercharging and services across the battery life cycle, plus joint work in Europe, the Middle East, and Central Asia. The announcement did not state the contract term, when phase two would start production, or which models the overseas work covers.

As of October 11, Nio had 9,584 charging and swap stations in China, including 4,176 swap stations, of which 1,073 were on highways; the highway swap network covers 9 north-south routes, 11 east-west routes, and 16 urban clusters, connecting more than 550 cities.
