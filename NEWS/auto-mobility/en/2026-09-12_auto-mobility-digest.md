### 2. China to End Some NEV and Fuel-Efficient Vehicle Tax Breaks From 2027 (Policy / China)
**Summary:** China’s Ministry of Finance, State Taxation Administration, and MIIT issued an announcement, with Q&A circulated by provincial finance outlets around Sep 11–12: from Jan 1, 2027, half-rate vehicle and vessel tax for fuel-efficient cars ends, as does the exemption for pure-electric commercial vehicles, plug-in (including range-extended) hybrids, and fuel-cell commercial vehicles—including units purchased before the change. Pure-electric and fuel-cell passenger cars remain outside the tax’s scope. Officials cited 2025 NEV sales of about 16.49 million units, a domestic new-car share above 50%, and an average PHEV passenger-car price of about 218,000 yuan as reasons to restore property-tax fairness.

**Links:**

**Commentary:** Purchase-tax and volume targets still backstop the market, while annual vehicle tax starts splitting pure-electric passenger cars from commercials and PHEVs—incentive design is getting more granular.

### 8. Tesla Rolls Out Automatic Collision Evasion in FSD V14.3.9, Engaging Without Driver Consent (AV / U.S.)
**Summary:** autoevolution reported on Sep 12 that Tesla is shipping Automatic Collision Evasion (ACE) with FSD V14.3.9 (around software 2026.27.6) on AI4/HW4 vehicles. When the system detects an imminent front collision that braking alone may not prevent—and FSD is available/enabled but not active, speed is at or below about 85 mph (about 137 km/h), and the road is not wet/slippery—it can take over steering, braking, or acceleration. During intervention the accelerator is muted; braking can slow the car without necessarily canceling the maneuver. ACE is not in FSD V14.2 Lite on the same build, leaving HW3 owners out. The feature intensifies debate over automated takeover without explicit driver approval.

**Links:**

- [autoevolution — Tesla's Automatic Collision Evasion Is the Most Controversial Safety Feature Tesla Launched in Years](https://www.autoevolution.com/news/tesla-s-automatic-collision-evasion-is-the-most-controversial-safety-feature-tesla-launched-in-years-275489.html)

**Commentary:** ACE shifts assistance from “driver must opt in” toward “the car may force a takeover”—the more aggressive the safety claim, the sharper the liability and hardware-tier fights become.

### 11. European Commission Keeps Battery Recycling Targets, Citing Treatment Capacity Gaps (Policy / Europe)
**Summary:** 2EU.brussels reported on Sep 12 that the European Commission will not change EU recycling-efficiency and material-recovery targets for waste batteries. Lithium recovery remains at least 50% by end-2027 and 80% by end-2031; lithium-based battery recycling efficiency stays at 65% from end-2025, rising to 70% by end-2030. JRC modeling suggests moving lithium recovery rates by ten percentage points would shift EU supply coverage by only about two points in 2027 and three in 2035; a baseline case sees European production (primary plus recycling) potentially meeting about 18% of battery lithium demand in 2027 and 42% in 2035—before accounting for available treatment capacity. The Commission says industrial-scale processing, not higher percentages, is the binding constraint; mandatory recycler reporting begins around 2027. Separately, Fraunhofer FFB and RWTH Aachen launched NRW-DiRecLab to compare five direct-recycling routes for electrode scrap.

**Links:**

- [2EU.brussels — Commission keeps battery recycling targets, pointing to the need for more EU treatment capacity](https://2eu.brussels/en/news/commission-keeps-battery-recycling-targets-pointing-to-the-need-for-more-eu-treatment-capacity)
