## I. Autonomous Driving & Robotaxis

### 1. Waymo Files Two NHTSA Safety Recalls on Closed Freeway Lanes and Manual Traffic Control (Autonomy)
**Summary:** Fox Business reported late Nov 21 that Alphabet-owned Waymo filed two voluntary recalls with NHTSA on Thursday covering its automated driving system, aiming to improve how the Waymo Driver handles closed freeway lanes and intersections under manual traffic control. The closed-lane recall addresses rare cases in which a vehicle may enter and continue in a closed freeway lane; Waymo said no related collisions or injuries have been reported and a fix is still in progress. The manual-control recall followed a crash in which a vehicle initially obeyed an officer who later became obscured; Waymo said the related software update was completed Nov 6. Vehicles need not be taken off the road. The filings come after California DMV barred Waymo robotaxis from all state freeways effective Nov 11; street operations continue, and NHTSA has a separate query on school-bus stop behavior.

### 2. Qatar Starts Phase-Two Electric Robotaxi Trials: Two L4 Vehicles on Fixed Doha Routes for 12 Months (Autonomy)
**Summary:** The Peninsula reported on Nov 22 that Qatar’s Ministry of Transport, with Mowasalat (Karwa), launched the second phase of electric robotaxi testing. Two Level-4 electric robotaxis will run for 12 months in Doha on MOT-mapped routes covering Old Doha Port, the Museum of Islamic Art, Souq Waqif, West Bay, the Sheraton Doha Hotel, and Diplomatic Street along the Corniche. Phase one logged nearly one million autonomous kilometers, exceeding the 500,000-kilometer target. Vehicles use LiDAR, radar, and cameras; MOT and Karwa monitor from a control center with remote operators, and a supervisor sits in the front passenger seat ready to intervene.

**Links:**

- [The Peninsula — Qatar begins phase two of electric Robotaxi test runs](https://thepeninsulaqatar.com/article/22/11/2026/qatar-begins-phase-two-of-electric-robotaxi-test-runs)

**Commentary:** Gulf markets are advancing L4 with small fleets, fixed corridors, and remote oversight—cautious by design, but doubling the phase-one mileage goal shows local safety metrics now support expansion.

### 3. Zoox Begins Purpose-Built Robotaxi Rides in Santa Monica: Employees First, Public Waitlist Next Year (Robotaxi)

## II. China Market & Global Trade

### 4. CPCA Forecast: November NEV Retail Penetration Around 70.6%, First Month Above 70% (Market)
**Summary:** Chinese outlets on Nov 22 cited a Nov 19 CPCA forecast that November narrow passenger-car retail may reach about 1.63 million units, down roughly 1.1% month on month, while NEV retail of about 1.15 million would imply about 70.6% penetration—potentially the first month above 70%. ### 6. South Korea’s Auto Trade Deficit With China Hits Record on Shanghai-Made Tesla Imports (Trade)
**Summary:** Yonhap reported Nov 22 that Korea’s January–October car exports to China fell 34.9% to about $168.77 million while imports from China rose 79.2% to about $4.39 billion, producing a record deficit of about $4.23 billion, per KITA and MOTIR data. The gap is driven largely by Tesla EVs built at the Shanghai Gigafactory: KAIDA data show 98,313 Tesla sales in Korea in the period, up 105% year on year. BYD sold more than 20,000 cars in Korea over the same span, with China-made Polestar models also contributing. China overtook Germany as Korea’s largest car-import source in the first half.

## III. Batteries, Charging & Vehicle-to-Grid

### 7. Ohio’s Latest NEVI Round: About $31 Million in Federal Funds for 40 Fast-Charging Sites (Charging)
**Summary:** Kurrant reported Nov 22 that the Ohio Department of Transportation named 40 sites in its latest NEVI round on Nov 19, with about $31 million in federal funds plus about $10 million from private developers for a combined budget near $41 million. Each site needs at least four ports at 150 kW or more at existing travel centers, fuel stops, and restaurants. With FHWA corridor build-out certification, Ohio can site chargers off interstates where demand is rising. The state’s NEVI pipeline reaches about 120 locations (roughly 99 in design, two under construction, 19 open); construction is due in 2027, with many openings targeted by year-end 2027.
