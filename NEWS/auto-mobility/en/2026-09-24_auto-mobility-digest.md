## I. Markets & Policy

### 1. EU August BEV registrations jump 62.7% YoY; Chinese-owned groups reach ~10.8% share (Market / Europe)
**Summary:** Euronews reported on September 24, citing ACEA, that EU battery-electric passenger-car registrations rose 62.7% year on year in August, with BEV share of new registrations climbing from 17.8% to 27.7%; overall EU new-car sales rose 4.5% YoY. EY said subsidies and an extra working day helped drive the rebound, warning sales could drop sharply if incentives fade. Five Chinese-owned groups tracked by ACEA saw sales up about 71%, lifting combined market share from 6.6% to 10.8%; Leapmotor, Chery and BYD posted triple-digit growth, while Volkswagen, BMW and Mercedes-Benz combined volumes fell 1.1% and share slipped from 41.3% to 39.1%. EU average petrol and diesel prices hit about €2.092 and €2.226 per litre in the week of September 21—record highs in the Commission’s series since 2005.

**Commentary:** High fuel prices and subsidies lift Europe’s EV share, but the gains are flowing more to Chinese brands and Tesla than to German groups.

### 2. Guardian: Chinese hybrid sales to the EU explode; Brussels seeks voluntary curbs as German industry first floats hybrid tariffs (Policy / EU–China)
**Summary:** The Guardian reported on September 24 that Eurostat data show Chinese-made full-hybrid passenger cars sold in the EU surged from 659 units in 2022 to 160,662 in the first seven months of 2026; Chinese-made plug-in hybrids rose from 56,706 in 2022 to 217,764 from January to July this year. After the EU’s 2024 anti-subsidy tariffs on Chinese BEVs, hybrids have become a workaround; Brussels has asked China to voluntarily cut hybrid exports or face safeguards such as quotas. Germany’s VDA indicated for the first time it would consider WTO-compatible trade defence, including assessing tariffs on Chinese hybrids. ACEA data put hybrids at nearly 37% of the overall EU market, with BYD, Chery and Leapmotor among the fastest growers.

**Links:**

- [The Guardian — Alarm bells sound in Brussels as EU sales of Chinese hybrid cars rocket](https://www.theguardian.com/business/2026/sep/24/eu-sales-chinese-hybrid-cars-rocket)

**Commentary:** Tariffs hit pure BEVs, so trade friction is now spreading across the full hybrid spectrum.

### 8. Waymo: 271 million driverless miles through June; ~95% fewer serious-injury-or-worse crashes vs. humans (AV)
**Summary:** Electrek and Carscoops reported on September 24 that Waymo released new safety figures: about 271 million fully driverless miles through end-June 2026—roughly 50 million more than its March update. Versus a human baseline, Waymo claims about 82% fewer injury-causing crashes and about 95% fewer crashes causing serious injury or worse; injury crashes involving pedestrians and cyclists were about 93% and 86% lower, respectively. Scaled across those miles, Waymo says that equates to about 841 avoided injury crashes, including about 175 involving vulnerable road users and about 55 that would have been serious or worse.

## IV. Batteries & Charging

### 10. BYD hits 2,000 highway flash-charging stations—about three months early, before holiday travel (Charging)
**Summary:** CnEVPost reported on September 24 that BYD commissioned its 2,000th Chinese highway flash-charging station, meeting the annual highway target about three months early ahead of Mid-Autumn and National Day travel. The milestone is part of the March 5 “Flash Charging China” plan: 20,000 stations by end-2026, including 2,000 on highways. As of September 24, BYD counted 11,586 flash stations nationwide across 341 cities; highway sites cover nearly one-third of expressway service areas with average spacing just over 100 km. New-generation connectors peak near 1,500 kW; paired with second-gen Blade Battery, BYD cites about five minutes from 10% to 70% at normal temperatures.
