## I. Autonomous Driving & Driver Assistance

### 1. Tesla Rolls Out FSD v15: Upgraded Vision Encoder, Claims ~25x Better Critical Intervention Rate (Autonomous Driving)
**Summary:** Electrek reported on Nov 21 that Tesla has begun rolling out Full Self-Driving v15, with release notes describing a full upgrade of the end-to-end driving network and a new vision encoder shared across FSD, Robotaxi, and Optimus. Tesla claims about a 25x improvement in critical intervention rate, plus better handling of road signs, potholes, school-zone speeds, and weather, and a new end-to-end visualization (Tesla Augmented Reality). The system can also slow for speed cameras and adds Bike Rack Mode; Tesla did not name the first vehicles, though it has said AI4 (HW4) is sufficient for v15.

**Links:**

- [Electrek — Tesla launches FSD v15, targets its biggest weaknesses](https://electrek.co/2026/11/21/tesla-fsd-v15-rollout-25x-safety-reality-view/)

**Commentary:** v15 aims squarely at long-standing weak spots such as signs and school zones, but the “25x” figure remains a relative multiplier without a disclosed absolute intervention rate.

### 2. Italy Grants Provisional Type Approval for Tesla Assisted Driving, First Among the EU’s Four Largest Countries (Policy)
**Summary:** Italy’s Ministry of Infrastructure and Transport recognized the Netherlands’ provisional approval for Tesla Assisted Driving (Europe’s name for FSD Supervised) on Italian territory after a technical review; ANSA and Engadget follow-ups on Nov 21 note Italy is the 12th EU member state to green-light the system and the first among the four most populous ahead of Germany, France, and Spain. The feature will arrive via OTA at about €99 per month and remains Level 2 with continuous driver supervision, not autonomy. Germany, France, and other large markets are still evaluating or testing.

- [Engadget — Tesla's Assisted Driving System Gets The Green Light For Rollout In Italy](https://www.engadget.com/2324122/teslas-assisted-driving-system-gets-the-green-light-for-rollout-in-italy/)

## II. Batteries & Charging Infrastructure

### 3. 2026 China Solid-State Battery Blue Book: Hybrid Solid-Liquid Cells Seen at 11.4 GWh Shipments This Year (Battery)
**Summary:** Securities Daily reported on Nov 21 that the *2026 China Solid-State Battery Industry Development Blue Book*, released at the Nov 20 International Summit on Power Battery Applications, says hybrid solid-liquid (semi-solid) batteries now have commercial delivery capability, with full-year shipments expected at about 11.4 GWh, while all-solid-state cells have completed automotive-grade sample validation but are not yet at mass-production readiness. The report expects margin divergence in 2027–2028, with cost control key for hybrid cells and yield ramp critical for all-solid-state; Gotion High-tech has cited >90% yield on a 0.2 GWh all-solid pilot line, and CATL has pointed to possible small-batch production in 2027.

**Links:**

- [Securities Daily — Hybrid solid-liquid batteries enter commercialization](http://www.zqrb.cn/finance/hangyedongtai/2026-11-21/A1795160991702.html)
- [China Economic Net — Hybrid solid-liquid batteries enter commercialization](http://www.ce.cn/cysc/newmain/yc/jsxw/202611/t20261121_3301272.shtml)

**Commentary:** The industry story is shifting from “all-solid-state hype” to “semi-solid that ships,” while all-solid timelines still hinge on yield and cost, not lab metrics alone.

### 4. NEA: China’s EV Charging Guns Reach 25.391 Million by End-October, Up 36.2% YoY (Charging)
**Summary:** Xinhua reported on Nov 20 that China’s National Energy Administration said the country’s EV charging infrastructure (guns) totaled 25.391 million by the end of October 2026, up 36.2% year over year. Public guns numbered 5.283 million (+16.5% YoY) with about 271 million kW of rated power and average per-gun power of about 51.37 kW; private guns reached 20.108 million (+42.5% YoY) with about 171 million kVA of registered capacity.

**Links:**

- [Xinhua — China’s EV charging guns reach 25.391 million](https://www.news.cn/politics/20261120/234c1ff1bc7c40a79f5ff8bb4f992ab2/c.html)

**Commentary:** Private chargers are still growing much faster than public ones, reinforcing a home-first, public-top-up pattern—while public power density remains the next experience bottleneck.

### 5. India Advances Unified Charging Platform and Utility Coordination: Bharat e-Charge Pilots Five States; SIAM Urges Faster Grid Hookups (Charging)
**Summary:** The Economic Times reported on Nov 21 that India’s Ministry of Heavy Industries said the Unified Bharat e-Charge platform is in pilot across five states, with about 12 charging-network operators connected so users can find, access, and pay across networks; NPCI BHIM Services and BHEL co-developed the platform. At SIAM’s National EV Charging Day the same day, the association’s president called for tighter DISCOM–operator timelines, noting high “behind-the-meter” connection costs for sites rated 100 kW and above; a prime minister’s adviser said substantial unused PM E-DRIVE budget could support charging build-out.
