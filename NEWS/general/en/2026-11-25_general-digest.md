## II. US Politics and Economy

### 6. Trump team proposes new tariffs on 14 partners over "excess industrial capacity"

**Summary:** The US Trade Representative on Wednesday unveiled results of an eight-month Section 301 probe, proposing new tariffs on 14 major partners including China, the EU, Japan and Taiwan to counter alleged structural overcapacity, with maximum potential rates of about 20%–40% and caps tied to completed or pending trade deals. For China, the proposal tops out near 20% — and up to 40% if talks fail — and may stack on existing China duties; a comment period and Jan. 25 hearing mean duties are unlikely before early 2027. The move aims to rebuild tariff leverage after the Supreme Court in February struck down broad emergency-based global duties; Democrats criticized it but face veto-proof hurdles to reverse course.

## III. China Policy and Economy

### 9. Shenzhou-24 launches; Pakistani astronaut becomes first foreigner aboard Tiangong

**Summary:** China Manned Space said a Long March-2F rocket launched Shenzhou-24 at 08:18 Beijing Time on Nov. 25 from Jiuquan; the craft entered orbit about 10 minutes later and docked with Tianhe's forward port at 11:56.
