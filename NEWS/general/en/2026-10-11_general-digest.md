The IRGC said it struck the LPG carrier NV Sunshine for trying to use what it called an illegal route along the southern side of the strait. On Thursday, Trump wrote that the United States would not strike Iran before the November 3 midterm elections.

### 7. First-home mortgage subsidies reach their first week, with a reported saving of nearly 40,000 yuan over five years on a 1 million yuan loan (Housing)
**Summary:** China Business Journal reported on the 11th that a notice from the Ministry of Finance, the People's Bank of China, and the National Financial Regulatory Administration put a home-purchase loan subsidy into effect on October 1. The subsidy is 1 percentage point a year for no more than five years, capped at 1 million yuan of principal per household, for a first commercial mortgage on a home of no more than 120 square meters and no more than 1.5 million yuan. The six large state banks apply it automatically when interest is charged. ICBC's Guangdong branch accepted more than 100 applications worth more than 100 million yuan in the first three days of the National Day holiday. A borrower quoted in the report said a 1 million yuan, 10-year loan would cost about 800 yuan less per month in the first year and nearly 40,000 yuan less over five years. Yan Yuejin of the E-House China R&D Institute said buyers between October 1 this year and October 1 next year can receive 50,000 yuan in subsidy, existing mortgages are excluded, and refinancing an old loan to capture the subsidy is blocked. China Construction Bank said the first-home definition covers new and existing homes, while subsidized housing and housing-provident-fund loans cannot be stacked with the subsidy.

### 8. Four agencies seek comment on auto innovation rules as People's Daily calls brake safety a red line (Industry)
**Summary:** On October 10, the Equipment Industry Department of the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology published a draft notice, prepared with the Ministry of Public Security, the Ministry of Ecology and Environment, and the State Administration for Market Regulation, on innovation design and development testing for motor vehicles. Comments are due by November 10. The draft says that from January 1, 2027, newly declared products with innovative designs must file extra technical parameters and complete safety and environmental assessments; inadequate testing or false materials would mean no announcement. Products already announced must supplement their filings by July 1, 2027, and those that still pose safety risks after that date must stop production and be recalled. The Paper reported that the accompanying guide bans fully concealed door handles and bars foldable and flexible display screens, requires reliability testing of at least 30,000 kilometers, and requires environmental testing of at least one year covering two winters and one summer. Lianhe Zaobao reported Sunday that People's Daily said the rules reject "rushed cars" and that a brake pedal must not snap while driving. The article tied that language to reports that the Maextro V800, a Huawei-JAC model priced from about 760,000 to 1.01 million yuan, suffered brake-pedal bracket failures in emergency stops. Maextro called the test a nonstandard extreme case, said it had not happened in actual use, and said an upgrade plan would be set by the end of October and applied in November. The China Association of Automobile Manufacturers on Sunday urged the industry to drop innovation that prizes form over safety.
