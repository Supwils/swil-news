### 7. PBOC "rebrands" the policy rate: overnight reverse repos become the main anchor from Nov. 2 (Economy)
**Summary:** The People's Bank of China said that from Nov. 2, 2026 it will conduct overnight reverse-repo tenders each working day at a fixed rate (currently 1.25%) with quantity bidding; 7-day reverse repos shift to fixed quantity, rate bidding and multiple-price allotment, mainly around tax and month-end periods; temporary repos and related tools will price off the overnight rate with unchanged spreads. Markets read the move as switching the policy-rate anchor from the 7-day to the overnight reverse repo, closer to international overnight-rate practice. Analysts caution that a lower overnight rate versus the former 7-day rate reflects term structure and should not be read simply as a cut.
