### 1. Russian drones hit a Kyiv bridge and a school in daylight (Ukraine)
**Summary:** A Russian drone struck the third floor of a Kyiv school at about 09:00 local time on Thursday. More than 100 students were inside; all had reached a shelter, no one was hurt, and part of a wall collapsed, the BBC reported. Later, two drones hit the Pivdennyi (Southern) Bridge over the Dnipro, one at the base of a central stanchion, while a metro train was crossing. Ukraine's nuclear safety watchdog said a drone hit the grounds of Kyiv's Institute for Nuclear Research on Wednesday night and started a fire; the test reactor was undamaged.

### 9. Fiscal interest subsidy on new personal mortgages takes effect on October 1 (Housing)
**Summary:** China News Service, citing CCTV, reported that from October 1 households using newly issued commercial mortgages to buy a first home can receive a fiscal interest subsidy for a provisional one-year period, the first such national subsidy on personal mortgages. Three conditions apply together: a new commercial loan for a first home, excluding refinancing of existing loans, for a new or existing dwelling; floor area of no more than 120 square meters; and a price of no more than 1.5 million yuan. The subsidized balance is capped at 1 million yuan, at 1 percentage point a year, for up to five years. At a first-home commercial rate of about 3%, a borrower with a 1 million yuan loan longer than five years would save nearly 50,000 yuan in interest. The central budget pays 90% and local budgets 10%. Banks deduct the subsidy monthly after authorization. Provident-fund loans and affordable-housing loans do not stack. Wu Jing of Tsinghua University's real-estate center calculated about 46,000 yuan of subsidy over five years on a 1 million yuan, 30-year loan at 3.05%. Yicai quoted analysts warning that the area and price caps could invite dual contracts.
