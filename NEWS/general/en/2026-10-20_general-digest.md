### 4. Drone strikes across Sudan have killed more than 100 people since Sunday (Africa)

The RSF’s Tasis administration and the Sudanese Emergency Lawyers accused the army of the Al-Dibab strike, while the army said its air defenses intercepted RSF drones aimed at the airport and at the Atbara and Setit dams.

Overall, 65 percent believe the votes will be counted accurately. The midterms are set for November 3.
