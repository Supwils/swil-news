## III. U.S.–China Relations

### 7. U.S. and China extend tariff and entity-list suspensions by about two months from Nov. 10 (Trade)
**Summary:** China’s Customs Tariff Commission said that from 13:01 Beijing time on Nov. 10 it would keep suspending the additional 24% tariffs on U.S. goods for two months while retaining a 10% levy. The Commerce Ministry confirmed a matching two-month pause, also from Nov. 10, of unreliable-entity-list measures on 11 U.S. entities and related export-control list measures; Washington extended 178 Section 301 exclusions and some maritime measures through Jan. 9, 2027.
