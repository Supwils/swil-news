### 1. Saudi Arabia shuts East–West oil pipeline after Iraq-origin drone attack; Baghdad dismisses a commander (Conflict / Energy)
**Summary:** Saudi Arabia’s energy ministry said Friday it temporarily closed the roughly 1,200-kilometer East–West oil pipeline from Abqaiq to Yanbu after drones struck sites in the Riyadh and Medina regions, causing injuries and damage. The line has been a critical Hormuz bypass and recently moved an estimated 4%–5% of global supply. Iraq’s prime minister’s office confirmed Saturday that the attack was launched from Maysan province on the Iranian border, dismissed the provincial operations commander, and opened an investigation; Riyadh said it would not retaliate for now while reserving all necessary measures. The GCC condemned the attack, and Brent has returned above $100 a barrel.
