### 2. Hormuz shipping attacks hit war-era highs as Iran vows to fight the US blockade

**Summary:** A CNN analysis of UK Maritime Trade Operations (UKMTO) data found more than 30 reported attacks around the Strait of Hormuz in October — the highest monthly tally since the US-Iran war began in February — and at least 22 in November through Nov. 22. Over the weekend and Monday, multiple tankers were struck by projectiles or, according to Iranian claims, naval mines and caught fire; Iran's navy said three "violating" tankers hit mines with explosions and fires, with no immediate crew casualties reported. Foreign Ministry spokesman Esmaeil Baghaei said Monday Iran will use every tool against the US naval blockade of Iranian ports, calling it a "crime"; Trump has said he is inclined to resume strikes after the New Year if no deal is reached. Maritime centers warned the Omani corridor remains severely threatened, with large cargo transits briefly near a standstill.
