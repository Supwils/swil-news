### 1. Iran proposes a seven-day roadmap to reopen Hormuz while ruling out nuclear concessions (Middle East / Diplomacy)
**Summary:** Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi said on Thursday at the UN General Assembly that Tehran had put a proposal to Washington via Qatar and other mediators: if the United States lifts its naval blockade of Iranian ports, waives oil sanctions, releases frozen funds and observes a ceasefire that includes Lebanon, the Strait of Hormuz could reopen within seven days and nuclear talks would restart. The plan resembles the June US-Iran memorandum of understanding but compresses the earlier roughly 60-day window into seven days. On Friday a senior Iranian official told Reuters anonymously that even if Washington accepts the Hormuz reopening proposal, Iran will show no flexibility on its nuclear program and that the waterway will stay closed until “all of Iran’s conditions are met.” Oil markets swung between ceasefire hopes and hard red lines.
