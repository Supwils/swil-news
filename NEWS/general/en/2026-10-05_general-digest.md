### 1. Saudi-backed forces retake towns near Bab el-Mandeb as al-Alimi announces Operation Yemen Dawn (Yemen)
**Summary:** Yemeni officials told the Associated Press on Monday that forces allied with the internationally recognized government, under air cover from the Saudi-led coalition, had retaken Dhubab just north of the Bab el-Mandeb Strait from the Houthis and had also captured the towns of Bab and Hadeid, and witnesses saw government tanks enter the towns. ### 2. The Mecca Alliance says Turkey and Pakistan will deploy forces to Saudi Arabia (Middle East)
**Summary:** After an emergency meeting in Riyadh on Monday of the Mecca Alliance's Strategic Political and Defence Committee, Saudi Arabia, Pakistan, and Turkey said they would activate collective deterrence over Houthi attacks on the kingdom and would provide the agreed military forces and capabilities for rapid deployment. The joint statement said the commitments will be carried out under approved arrangements and each country's laws, and it did not give troop numbers or missions.
