## IV. China Policy and Economy

### 9. NBS releases August data: industrial rebound, weak investment and consumption (Economy)
**Summary:** China’s National Bureau of Statistics said on September 15 that industrial value-added at enterprises above designated size rose 5.2% year on year in August, 0.7 percentage points faster than in July. Fixed-asset investment (excluding rural households) fell 7.2% in the first eight months, with real-estate development investment down 19.9% and private investment down 10.1%. Retail sales of consumer goods rose 0.4% year on year in August and 1.1% in January–August. Officials described the economy as generally stable with improving new drivers, while acknowledging external pressures, strong supply versus weak demand, and difficulties for some firms. Spokesperson Fu Linghui said volume and structural pressures “can be overcome with effort,” pointing to equipment upgrades and “two major” / “two new” policy support.
