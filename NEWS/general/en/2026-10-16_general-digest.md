## III. China Policy and the Economy

### 8. Commerce and science ministries seek comment on export controls covering 40 technologies (Industrial policy)
**Summary:** On Friday the Ministry of Commerce and the Ministry of Science and Technology issued Announcement No. 48 of 2026, proposing to revise the catalog of technologies prohibited or restricted from export by adding 40 technologies in artificial intelligence, biomedicine, and photovoltaics. Public comment runs through November 15. From the date of the announcement until the revised catalog takes effect, exports of the listed technologies require temporary registration. A Commerce Ministry spokesperson said 14 technologies would be prohibited and 26 restricted: 23 in AI, covering large models, agents, computing clusters, and information processing; 5 in biomedicine; and 12 in solar manufacturing. Technologies already in the public domain through open source or academic papers would not be covered. The spokesperson said the revision is routine technology-trade administration and is not aimed at any particular country, region, or entity. Examples of proposed bans include AI-driven malicious-code generation and mutation, proprietary cell- and gene-therapy process platforms, and perovskite and tandem solar-module production. A restricted example is polysilicon engineering technology with a single-line capacity of at least 30,000 tons a year. Transfers of ownership abroad and licenses for overseas use must be registered, including exports already underway but not completed.
