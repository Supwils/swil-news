**Summary:** The Guardian reported Thursday that drones struck two tankers near Romania's Black Sea coast and set them on fire. Dan also said Article 5 was not under discussion and that Romania had not requested Article 4. Reuters counted at least 23 sailors missing or killed after attacks on commercial shipping near Bulgaria and Romania over the past 10 days.

**Summary:** An Associated Press-NORC poll released Thursday, less than four weeks before Election Day, found that about 8 in 10 registered voters name the economy or the cost of living among their top issues, and about two-thirds say the same of health care and the war in Iran. Immigration and crime rank lower. Only about one-third of registered voters view President Donald Trump favorably or approve of his job performance. About 8 in 10 disapprove of his handling of the cost of living, and about three-quarters disapprove of his handling of the economy. Among Republican voters, 44% approve of his cost-of-living performance, 54% approve on the economy, and 69% support him on foreign policy. About half of voters want "substantial change," and roughly 3 in 10 want "complete and total upheaval." About half prefer Democratic control of the House or the Senate, roughly 4 in 10 prefer Republican control, and 15% are undecided. About three-quarters of Democratic voters call voting "extremely" important, compared with roughly 6 in 10 Republicans. The survey of 1,119 adults, including 925 registered voters, was conducted October 8-12; the margin of error for registered voters is plus or minus 4.2 percentage points.
