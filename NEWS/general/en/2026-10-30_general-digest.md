### 2. Israel’s final election tally leaves both major blocs short of a governing majority (Politics)
**Summary:** Israel’s Central Elections Committee released the final count early Friday: Benjamin Netanyahu’s right-wing bloc won 53 of 120 Knesset seats, the opposition bloc led by former military chief Gadi Eisenkot took 48, and Arab-led parties secured a record 19—none reaching the 61 seats needed to form a government. Turnout was 77.9%, with about 5.97 million valid votes. Results still require certification and submission to President Isaac Herzog next Wednesday, after which he will consult party leaders and tap a candidate to form a coalition; the new Knesset is due to be sworn in on Nov. 10. Netanyahu’s camp alleged Qatari interference that boosted Arab parties; the elections committee said the claims lacked basis.

### 3. Civilian evacuation completed in occupied Oleshky under localized Russia-Ukraine ceasefire (Humanitarian/Conflict)
**Summary:** On the fourth day of a localized Kherson-front ceasefire brokered by US First Lady Melania Trump, Russian and Ukrainian officials said the evacuation of civilians from Russian-occupied Oleshky and the delivery of humanitarian aid were completed “without incident.” About 25 buses could carry up to roughly 750 people; some residents traveled via Crimea and Anapa in southern Russia, then by air to Gomel in Belarus before entering Ukraine’s Chernihiv region, while others chose Russian-held areas or stayed. The city’s population has fallen from about 24,000 before the war to roughly 2,000. The truce is scheduled through the evening of Nov. 1; the Kremlin said its success should not be read as willingness to extend a ceasefire nationwide.

## III. China Policy and Economy

### 5. PBOC reforms open-market operations, elevating overnight reverse repos as the main tool (Economy)
**Summary:** The People’s Bank of China announced on Oct. 30 that, from Nov. 2, it will further refine short-end rate management: overnight reverse-repo tenders every business day at a fixed rate (currently 1.25%) with quantity bidding; seven-day reverse repos shift to fixed-quantity, rate-tender, multiple-price allotment mainly around tax periods and month-ends; and temporary repos and related tools will price off the overnight rate instead of the seven-day rate. A PBOC Party committee meeting the same day reaffirmed moderately accommodative policy, ample liquidity, and efforts to build multi-channel renminbi cross-border payment systems. The move operationalizes Governor Pan Gongsheng’s earlier signal to switch the main open-market tenor to overnight when ready.

### 8. COFCO makes first inquiry for US soft red winter wheat after Trump-Xi summit (US-China/Trade)

### 10. BOJ holds at 1.25%, declaring underlying inflation “generally consistent” with 2% for the first time (Central Banks)
