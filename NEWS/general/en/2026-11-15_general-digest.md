## III. China Policy and Economy

### 7. FX reserve requirement rises from 4% to 6%, effective today (Finance)
**Summary:** The People’s Bank of China had decided that, effective November 15, 2026, the foreign-exchange reserve requirement ratio for financial institutions would rise by 2 percentage points, from 4% to 6%, to strengthen FX liquidity management. Market commentary said the move should help balance FX supply and demand and curb self-reinforcing expectations of yuan appreciation. The previous adjustment was in September 2023, when the ratio was cut from 6% to 4%. Around the same period, the central bank reported outstanding social financing of about 469.16 trillion yuan at end-October, up 7.2% year on year, with M2 up 7.1%.
