## II. China's Economy

### 4. China's October Data: Industry Steady, Consumption Weak, Investment Still Falling

**Summary:** The National Bureau of Statistics reported on Nov. 16 that October industrial output rose 4.8% year on year, retail sales were 4.6463 trillion yuan (up just 0.4%), and the services production index rose 4.6%. January-October fixed-asset investment fell 8.1% and property development investment fell 21.6%. The urban surveyed unemployment rate was 5.1%, CPI rose 0.7% and PPI rose 5.7% year on year. The bureau called the economy generally stable but acknowledged high external uncertainty and the persistent "strong supply, weak demand" problem.

## V. Europe

### 10. EU Foreign Ministers Agree to Advance an EU-Syria Association Agreement

**Summary:** On Nov. 16, foreign ministers of all 27 EU states meeting in Brussels agreed to move ahead with an association agreement with Syria, reviving a framework first drafted in 2008 and prioritizing transport, energy and digital connectivity. The EU has committed 620 million euros in support for Syria in 2026-2027. EU foreign policy chief Kaja Kallas said the EU wants Syrian refugees to return, but conditions for their return must exist.
