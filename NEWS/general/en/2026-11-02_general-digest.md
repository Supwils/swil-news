## III. China Policy and Economy

### 6. PBOC “Rate Anchor” Switch Takes Effect: 541 Billion Yuan Overnight Reverse Repos on Day One (Economy)
**Summary:** The People’s Bank of China on November 2 conducted 541 billion yuan of overnight reverse-repo operations at a fixed rate of 1.25% via quantity tender. It was the first operation after the October 30 mechanism change, marking the shift of the main open-market tenor to overnight and the re-anchoring of the primary policy rate from the 7-day reverse repo to the overnight reverse-repo rate. Under the new framework, 7-day reverse repos move to fixed-quantity, interest-rate tender with multiple-price allotment, mainly around tax and month-end dates; temporary repos and related tools now add or subtract spreads from the overnight rate. Analysts stressed the move deepens price-based policy and should not be read as a stealth rate cut.
