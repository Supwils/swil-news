## II. US Politics and Economy

### 5. Trump Section 301 tariff plan on 14 partners draws Democratic and industry pushback

**Summary:** A day after USTR unveiled proposed Section 301 duties over alleged "structural excess capacity," Thursday coverage focused on rates of about 20% for China (up to about 40% if talks fall short), as high as about 35% for Vietnam, and starting near 15% for the EU, South Korea, Switzerland and Taiwan, among 14 partners. USTR said it intends to cap new tariffs at levels agreed in completed or ongoing trade deals, with the earliest effect around early next year, and exemptions for goods covered by a roughly $30 billion reciprocal tariff-cut list. Congressional Democrats attacked the plan as raising household costs; trade lawyers argued 301 is being used as leverage and could face fresh lawsuits. US markets were closed for Thanksgiving as Asia and Europe digested the news.
