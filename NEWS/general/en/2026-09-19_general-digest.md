### 7. Central bank adviser Huang Yiping says AI could deepen China's strong-supply, weak-demand gap (Economy)
**Summary:** Reuters reported Saturday that Huang Yiping, a member of the People's Bank of China's monetary policy committee, told an economic forum in Beijing that wider deployment of artificial intelligence and faster innovation could worsen the imbalance between strong supply and weak demand, and that the gap between aggregate demand and supply may not disappear soon and could persist. He noted that the global AI boom has supported China's exports this year, cushioning a property downturn, local-government debt pressure, and cautious household spending, even as the United States and some trading partners urge Beijing to rely less on exports and excess capacity. Huang called for further market-oriented reform, a higher share of household income, and deeper overseas investment and industrial cooperation rather than exports alone. He also proposed that the central government borrow more to repair the balance sheets of local governments, financial institutions, and companies, warning that stimulus would have limited effect otherwise.
