**Summary:** The FAA issued a nationwide ground stop around 4 p.m. ET on November 13 after air-traffic communications and radar instability, lasting about four hours—the longest since 9/11. Officials linked the fault to a contractor (L3Harris) switching copper to fiber at a remote site, with routers flooded by connection attempts; Homeland Security Secretary Markwayne Mullin said there was no indication yet of a nefarious attack. ### 7. 2026 national medical insurance drug list: 107 new drugs, 51 Class-1 innovators (Livelihood)
**Summary:** China’s National Healthcare Security Administration and the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security released the 2026 national basic medical insurance drug list and the commercial health-insurance innovative drug list on November 14.
