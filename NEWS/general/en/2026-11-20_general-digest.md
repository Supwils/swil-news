### 2. ICJ finds Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya — first state held liable

**Summary:** On November 19 the International Court of Justice ruled on the merits in The Gambia v. Myanmar that Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya during the 2017 "clearance operations" in northern Rakhine, and breached duties to prevent, punish and enact Genocide Convention legislation — the first time the court has held a state responsible for genocide. Myanmar was ordered to cease ongoing violations, investigate and prosecute suspects, give guarantees of non-repetition, enable safe voluntary return and family reunification, and pay compensation; the amount and modalities will be set later, with a report due within two years.

## III. China Policy and Economy

### 6. November LPR left unchanged for an 18th straight month; loan rates stay low

**Summary:** The National Interbank Funding Center, authorized by the PBOC, kept the one-year LPR at 3.0% and the five-year-and-above LPR at 3.5% on November 20 — the 18th consecutive month without a change, in line with expectations.
