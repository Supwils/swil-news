## I. Global Headlines

### 1. COP31 ends empty-handed as EU and others reject a draft that omits fossil fuels

**Summary:** COP31 closed Sunday in Antalya, Turkey, without an overarching political cover decision — another major climate summit failure since Copenhagen in 2009. A final draft from the Turkish and Australian presidencies made no direct reference to fossil fuels; Ireland's Climate and Energy Minister Darragh O'Brien, speaking for the EU presidency, said the text "does not meet the moment," and Panama and the Marshall Islands also objected, while climate finance and carbon-capture language remained stuck. COP31 President Murat Kurum said there was no agreement and the decisions were not adopted; UN Secretary-General António Guterres called the outcome "deeply disappointing" but stressed there is "no ambiguity in the science." Overtime stretched more than two days, making Antalya the longest COP on record.

## III. China Policy and Economy

### 6. China cuts margin requirement to 80%, floats ChiNext market-makers and fee relief

**Summary:** On November 22, with CSRC approval, the Shanghai, Shenzhen and Beijing exchanges cut the minimum financing margin ratio from 100% to 80%, effective November 30, a change estimated to free nearly 400 billion yuan in theoretical incremental buying power and lift leverage from about 1x to about 1.25x. Shenzhen Exchange the same day sought public comment on ChiNext market-making rules aimed at deepening liquidity and market resilience. The exchanges also rolled out a new fee-cut package, including waiving trading-unit usage fees and extending waivers on listing fees and bond transaction fees through 2028; China Securities Depository said it would continue waiving certain issuer service and settlement fees on green, tech-innovation and private-enterprise bonds in 2027–2028.
