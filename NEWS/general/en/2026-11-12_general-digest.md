## III. China Policy and Economy

### 8. Premier Li Qiang Chairs State Council Study Session on Multilevel Social Security (Policy)
**Summary:** On November 12, the State Council held its 22nd thematic study session on “improving a multilevel social security system and further weaving a tighter safety net,” chaired by Premier Li Qiang. He stressed that demographic change, technological progress, and more diverse forms of employment raise new demands on social security, calling for broader enterprise annuity coverage, a more attractive personal pension system, orderly progress on long-term care insurance, and better coverage for flexible and new-form workers with gradually higher participation rates. He also urged stronger long-term sustainability of social insurance funds, provincial-level pooling for basic medical insurance, and tighter fund supervision and value preservation.
