### 7. Pan Gongsheng announces a liquidity backstop for large non-bank redemptions and floats an overnight policy rate (Central bank)
**Summary:** People’s Bank of China Governor Pan Gongsheng said Monday at the 2026 Financial Street Forum that the central bank will set up, with the financial regulator and the securities regulator, a liquidity facility for large redemptions by non-bank institutions, to be used only when asset managers face a collective liquidity shock and the bond market enters a redemption-and-fire-sale loop. Counterparties must meet macroprudential conditions and post high-grade collateral; the facility covers wealth-management subsidiaries, public-fund managers, and their bank and securities parents. He also said the standing lending facility will be extended to foreign-funded banks incorporated in China and to national banks, that a link between the interbank and exchange bond markets can open after testing, and that eight offshore yuan clearing banks will be allowed to trade for their own books on the onshore interbank foreign-exchange market. Over the near term he said the central bank will study switching the main open-market operation to overnight, making the overnight repo rate the main policy rate, and retiring the benchmark deposit and lending rates that have long been unchanged, and he said China has no intention of seeking a trade advantage through depreciation.
