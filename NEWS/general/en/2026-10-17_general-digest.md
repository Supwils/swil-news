## I. Global News

### 1. Trump waives the Graham Act's new Russia tariffs and sanctions (Diplomacy)
**Summary:** The White House on Friday published an executive order implementing the Lindsey O. Graham Sanctioning Russia and Iran Act, which Trump signed on September 18. The order first sets a 500 percent ad valorem duty increase on Russian goods and a 100 percent duty on goods from the five largest importers of Russian crude oil and natural gas if they knowingly make new purchases on or after October 18. It then uses the Act's section 115 national-interest waiver to waive duties under sections 112 and 113, plus sanctions and restrictions in sections 102 through 111 and 117, among others. The waivers last until the president or the secretary of state decides further action is appropriate. Sanctions under the earlier Russia emergency executive order continued on March 24 are left in place. Trump certified to Congress that the waiver is in the national interest. The BBC reported Saturday that the move came two days before the implementation deadline, and Virginia Democrat Don Beyer said it defied congressional intent.
