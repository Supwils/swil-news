## IV. China Policy and Economy

### 9. PBOC Q3 Monetary Policy Report: Step Up Capital-Market Tools and Countercyclical Support (Economy)
**Summary:** The People’s Bank of China on November 11 released its 2026 Q3 monetary policy report. It said GDP grew 4.6% year-on-year in the first three quarters under a moderately accommodative stance; at end-September, outstanding social financing and M2 rose 7.1% and 7.2% respectively, and the average rate on new corporate loans in September was slightly below 3.0%. Looking ahead, the PBOC said it will intensify countercyclical adjustment and domestic demand support, step up the Securities, Funds and Insurance companies Swap Facility (SFISF) and the stock buyback/increase re-lending tool, and use the bond-market large nonbank redemption swap facility to keep equity, bond, and FX markets stable. By end-September, buyback/increase loan contracts exceeded 420 billion yuan, with more than 260 billion yuan disbursed. The report also flagged supply-strong/demand-weak dynamics, structural divergence, and external shocks.
