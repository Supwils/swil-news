## III. China Policy and Economy

### 5. August CPI up 0.8% y/y, PPI up 3.8%: imported energy and commodities dominate (Economy)
**Summary:** China’s National Bureau of Statistics reported on September 9 that August CPI rose 0.8% year-on-year (up 0.4% month-on-month after a prior decline), with core CPI at 1.0%. PPI rose 3.8% year-on-year and 0.4% month-on-month; purchasing prices rose 5.8% year-on-year. Official commentary said CPI’s rebound reflected international price moves and seasonal food factors, with energy inflation widening to about 4.1% y/y and gasoline up about 9.3%. PPI was driven by imported commodity passthrough and stronger demand in upgrading industries, led by coal, nonferrous metals, and oil processing. Average CPI for January–August was up 0.9% y/y; PPI averaged +2.0%.
