### 2. Euro Zone Inflation Hits 4%, a Three-Year High; Markets Price December ECB Hike (Economy)
**Summary:** Eurostat’s flash estimate on November 4 showed euro-area October HICP at 4.0% year-on-year, the highest in about three years and up from 3.8% in September, matching a Reuters poll; energy inflation jumped to about 20.8%, while core inflation held at 2.5%. Inflation remains above the ECB’s 2% target across the bloc. The ECB left its deposit rate at 2.5% last week, but several officials have signaled a December move; after the print, markets priced well above a 70% chance of a hike. Sweden’s central bank tightened the same day and signaled more increases may follow.

## II. US Politics and Economy

**Summary:** Major US outlets and the Associated Press projected on November 4 that Democrats won control of both the House and Senate in the November 3 midterms—one of the rare same-night flips of both chambers in half a century. Democrats flipped at least seven Senate seats, including Texas, Maine, Ohio, South Carolina, North Carolina, Iowa, and Kansas, while holding Georgia and Michigan; in the House they netted at least about 28 seats and won 231 or more. Voter anger over inflation, gasoline prices, living costs, and the Iran war drove the wave; Vice President JD Vance called the Iran war the “obvious” reason for the GOP loss. ## III. China Policy and Economy

### 6. PBOC Cuts Stock Buyback-and-Increase Relending Rate to 1%; Banks Seek Over 200 Billion Yuan (Finance)
**Summary:** The People’s Bank of China said on November 4 that, effective the same day, it was cutting the stock repurchase and increase relending rate by 0.25 percentage points to 1% to help stabilize the capital market. The facility covers 21 national banks and five systemically important city commercial banks, with 100% support, a one-year term, and possible rollovers. After a November 2 symposium, banks had already applied for more than 200 billion yuan in quotas, and the PBOC said it would fully meet demand. Separately, it announced a 300 billion yuan outright reverse-repo operation for November 5, with a three-month (91-day) tenor, to keep banking-system liquidity ample.
