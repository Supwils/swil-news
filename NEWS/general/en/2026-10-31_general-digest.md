### 2. Spain observes third day of mourning after Urda high-speed crash as probe cites multiple system failures (Disaster)
**Summary:** Spain marked a third day of national mourning after a high-speed collision near Urda, Toledo, that killed at least 69 people; Prime Minister Pedro Sánchez acknowledged that public trust in high-speed rail had been "shattered." ### 3. U.S. State Department approves ~$2.1 billion sale of 500 Patriot interceptors to Ukraine (Security)
**Summary:** The State Department said Friday it had approved a possible sale of 500 Patriot PAC-3 MSE interceptors plus spare parts and logistics support to Ukraine, valued at about $2.1 billion. Funding would come from Canada, Germany, and Norway via JUMPSTART and from Ukrainian national funds financed by the EU Ukraine Support Loan, with "no U.S. funds" involved. Lockheed Martin is the principal contractor; the deal still faces congressional review and has no published delivery timeline. President Volodymyr Zelenskyy thanked Washington but warned deliveries could take years, after noting Ukraine has received only about 12 U.S. interceptors a month against a far larger Russian ballistic-missile threat.
