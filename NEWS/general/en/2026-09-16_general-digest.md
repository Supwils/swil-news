## III. Global Economy and Central Banks

### 6. Federal Reserve delivers first rate hike in three years, lifting the target range to 3.75%–4.00% (central bank)
**Summary:** The Federal Reserve on Wednesday unanimously raised the federal funds rate by 25 basis points to a 3.75%–4.00% target range—the first hike since July 2023 and the first policy shift under Chair Kevin Warsh. The statement said inflation remains elevated and that the move will support a timelier return to the 2% goal; projections show most officials expect at least one more hike this year, with the rate possibly reaching 4.00%–4.25%. Updated forecasts raised PCE inflation to about 3.7% and delayed a return to 2%. Drivers cited include Iran-war energy shocks, tariff effects, and AI-related capital spending.
