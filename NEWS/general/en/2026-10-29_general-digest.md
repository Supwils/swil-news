**Summary:** The death toll from the high-speed rear-end collision near Urda in Toledo province rose to at least 69 on Oct. 29, with dozens still hospitalized and about 15 in critical care. ### 9. FCC votes to bar Chinese labs from testing US-bound electronics (US-China/Tech)
**Summary:** The Federal Communications Commission on Oct. 29 voted to prohibit test laboratories and certification bodies in China and other economies without mutual-recognition or reciprocal arrangements from testing smartphones, computers and other devices for the US market; the ban is slated to take effect in December 2028. Chair Brendan Carr said about 82% of electronics were tested in China in 2025 versus roughly 4% in the US, arguing such labs can access sensitive pre-market information. The step expands 2025 restrictions on labs owned or controlled by the Chinese government and comes weeks before a possible Trump–Xi meeting on the sidelines of APEC in Shenzhen.
