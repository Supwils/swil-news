**Summary:** On October 16 the Ministry of Commerce and the Ministry of Science and Technology issued Announcement No. 48 of 2026, proposing revisions to the catalogue of technologies prohibited or restricted from export and seeking public comment through November 15. ### 12. Vietnam and EFTA sign a free-trade agreement in Hanoi (Trade)
**Summary:** Norway's foreign ministry announced Sunday that Norway, Iceland, Liechtenstein, and Switzerland signed a free-trade agreement with Vietnam that will eventually eliminate tariffs on almost all Norwegian goods exported there; about half of those exports will be tariff-free on entry into force and more than 98 percent within seven years, with salmon tariff-free immediately and mackerel phased out over three years. Vietnam currently charges up to 15 percent on both fish, and Norway–Vietnam goods trade was about 19 billion Norwegian kroner in 2025. Vietnam's Ministry of Industry and Trade said the signing took place in Hanoi on October 18, witnessed by Party General Secretary and State President To Lam, with Minister Le Manh Hung signing alongside Swiss President Guy Parmelin, Liechtenstein Prime Minister Brigitte Haas, Icelandic Foreign Minister Thorgerdur Katrin Gunnarsdottir, and Norwegian State Secretary Astrid Bergmal. The agreement covers goods, services, investment, intellectual property, government procurement, and sustainable trade, and is not yet in force.
